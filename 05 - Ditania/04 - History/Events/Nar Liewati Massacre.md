The **Nar Liewati massacre** (Standard Wai: Ista Nar Liewati, "Nar Liewati Tragedy"; Peste ji Nar Liewati, "Sequence of events at Nar Liewati") was the intentional, pre-meditated sectarian killing of 900-1,300 Kaner men, women and children by multiple [[Kanli|Kanli militias]] hired by the [[Government of National Salvation]], including the [[Kazier Group]], on the orders of [[Raen Beinofa]].
The massacre is officially denied by the government of [[Ditania|Ditanian Republic]] to the present day, claiming the deaths were the result of a "property dispute" between refugees.

## Background

### The Nar Liewati refugee camp
During the war, a large number of rural Kaner civilians were displaced from their homes, particularly due to fear of [[Paile]] and other Kanli militias, but also due to destruction of homes, takeover of villages by opposing factions, and sectarian violence, such as the [[Kilon Massacre]]. Over time, a large refugee concentration coalesced in the northern outskirs of [[Nar Liewati]], a major Kaner town safely under control of the Government of National Salvation. 
The camp was officially open in 1983 under the auspices of the [[International Centre for War Relief]], an [[ODDN]] organ linked to the presence of the [[Democratizing Multinational Force in Ditania]]. A DMFD platoon under the command of Susian lieutenant [[Dorvňa Ňaruch]]. Infrastructure works followed, building pre-fabricated housing using Confian panelized construction methods to replace the cotton tents, introducing community policing within the camp to reduce crime, and standardizing services such as healthcare, childcare and education. Human rights activist [[Kunseka Istanna]] and urban planner [[Nis Soler]], both [[Confia|Confians]] volunteering for the ICWR, devised a plan for the camp to slowly be incorporated into the city.
The camp was segregated by race on the orders of Raen Beinofa. It was considered one of the best camps in Ditania purely by standard of living, as the Kaner civilians that formed its majority were well-integrated with the northern neighbourhoods of Nar Liewati, working there by day and often attending temple service with the locals.

### Prelude
The GNS was irate after discovering [[Balsheck|Balsheckian]] involvement in the financing of the [[Jadir Front]]. After the government of [[Pewipi Yiwa]] refused to cut off the JF and halted cooperation with the GNS instead, Beinofa ordered two of his battalions, the Kazier Group and other minor militias into the refugee camps north of Nar Liewati.
## The massacre
On the eve of the 24th of Teosian, 1986, the [[Kazier Group]] and four other Kanli militias, composing a group of 700 men wearing GNS uniforms breached gates South I, Southwest I and Southwest II of the camp. The unit, led by Len Kazier, entered the camp while GNS soldiers were seen guarding its entrance and perimetre. Raen Beinofa had personally sent a memorandum instructing Kazier to "[...] enter the zoo and slaughter at will. Its borders are protected by me, and I am protected by the Susians [the DMFD]. [...] This is the day Yiwa will understand the power we own over his people." 
At midday, many of the camp's inhabitants had been rounded up into the central square. Kazier ordered his men to fire at will, immediately murdering around 500 people, with many deaths from trampling as the panicked civilians attempted to flee the square. After that, the groups dispersed and spent the evening going door to door in the camp's streets to kill as many people as possible. However, due to the camp's planning into dense low-rise apartment buildings, the groups' door-to-door approach was severely hampered.
By the evening, GNS troops fired flares in an attempt to guide the militiamen to untouched areas and artillery rounds into the tallest buildings. By then, the GNS dismissed their "advisory role" and decided to occupy the camp, officially declaring its dissolution, and that the surviving refugees would be displaced to other areas.

### The ICWR building sieg
At around 8:30, the street with the building from where the camp's [[International Centre for War Relief]] agents were stationed was barricaded by the DMFD contingent platoon, who sensed danger after watching the military build-up on the camp's borders. Lieutenant Dorvňa Ňaruch met with camp administrator Pisali Kante, warning him to ensure the safety of as many civilians as possible by allowing them to enter the building's basement. Kante quickly proceeded, warning the nearby buildings of the impending tragedy. 
A standoff began at around 10:00, when the soldiers of an unindentified Kanli militia requested the unblocking of the street. Ňaruch refused. With instructions to defer confrontations until the killing began, the militia withdrew.
After the midday massacre ensued, the platoon took position on the edges of the street and began a shootout with a large number of Kanlis. They were quickly outnumbered, forcing a retreat into the building

## Developments and consequences
Upon learning of the masssacre, DMFD commander [[Sataria Žartonna]] immediately severed communications with the Government of National Salvation and the Tekuri Ljas Accord, officially declaring functional autonomy from either authority. Beinofa did not expect the move, allowing the motorized and mechanized components of the army to overrun GNS positions and take Nar Liewati, fighting a short 3-day battle before taking control of the city.
