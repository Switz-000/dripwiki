The **Five of Goretopol'** is an interview and debate program broadcasted by [[KSH]] weekly on Channel 6. During the program, the guests are interviewed by five hosts.
The name of the program is a play on the Five of Karlotopol', the perpetrators of the [[Carlotopolis Uprising]].
