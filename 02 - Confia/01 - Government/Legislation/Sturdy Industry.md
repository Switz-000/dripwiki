The **Sturdy Industry** program was an industrial policy created by the government of [[Boris Serec]] and continued by his successor [[Japlen Razol]]. It involved the heavy and direct subsidization of [[Ragged-sleeve|ragged-sleeve]] conglomerates, reaching a total of Н¢147 billion by 1990. The program aided in providing funds for the reconstruction of Confia post-[[Continental War]], but was heavily criticized for constraining the Confian budget, tightened by [[Senjap Kunsekowić|Kunsekowić]]'s spending caps, leading to austerity under Razol and for further entrenching the ragged-sleeves as a powerful economic force.
