The **Confian National Government** (Confian: Końfiski Sowjažei Wlaššesse/Коњфіски Совяжей Влашшессе) is the national level of the [[Confia|Confian]] government.

# Structure
## President

The **President of Confia**, officially the **President of the Confian Nation** (Confian: Razawec(a) Końfiskie Wjaža/Разавец(а) Коњфиские Вяжа) is the head of state of the United Syndicates of Confia.
