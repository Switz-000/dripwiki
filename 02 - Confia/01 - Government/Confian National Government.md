The **Confian National Government** (Confian: Końfiski Sowjažei Wlaššesse/Коњфіски Совяжей Влашшессе) is the national level of the [[Confia|Confian]] government.

# Structure
## President

The **President of Confia**, officially the **President of the Confian Nation** (Confian: Razawec(a) Końfiskie Wjaže/Разавец(а) Коњфіскіе Вяже) is the head of state of the United Syndicates of Confia.
The President is elected directly via single transferrable vote since 2009. Before that, they were elected via two-round first-past-the-post.
