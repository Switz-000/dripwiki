The **Atom Party** is a [[Confia|Confian]] modernist political party. 
The movement was founded as the **Confian Green Alliance** in 1997 as an expression of the environmentalist movement in Confia. After the party ran the unsuccessful **Confian Green List** in the [[List of Confian elections#2001 Confian general election|2001 general election]], the party's ranks were quickly hijacked by former [[Grawolja Lasmanna List]] organizers, who transformed it into a primarily single-issue party [[List of political positions#Nuclearism|advocating for nuclear energy]] in the form of a "Confian [[Atompron]]". Aside from the flagship GSE proposal, the party supported the legalization of tobacco, same-sex marriage, and civilian firearm registration, while defending balanced budgets and fiscal responsibility. 
The party participated in the quadripartite [[Oždi Front]] demonstrations in 2006 between the youths of the [[Syndicalist League]], [[Mi, Niteraljci!|Us, Liberals!]], and the [[Confian Democratic Party]], aiming to legalize same-sex marriage.
In 2010, the party greatly refined its platform under the leadership of [[Kurki]]
The party reoriented itself around Kolkovian Modernism in 2028, renaming itself to the "**Atom Party - Confian Modernist Movement**"
