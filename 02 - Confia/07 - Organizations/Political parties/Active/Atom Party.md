The **Atom Party** is a [[Confia|Confian]] [[Modernism|modernist]] political party.
The movement was founded as the **Confian Green Alliance** in 1997 as an expression of the environmentalist movement in Confia. After the party ran the unsuccessful **Confian Green List** in the [[List of Confian elections#2001 Confian general election|2001 general election]], the party's ranks were quickly hijacked by former [[Grawolja Lasmanna List]] organizers, who transformed it into a primarily single-issue party [[List of political positions#Nuclearism|advocating for nuclear energy]] in the form of a "Confian [[Atompron]]", a government-sponsored enterprise that would guarantee loans for the construction of nuclear energy. Aside from the flagship GSE proposal, the party supported the legalization of tobacco, same-sex marriage, and civilian firearm registration, while defending balanced budgets and fiscal responsibility. 
The party participated in the quadripartite [[Oždi Front]] demonstrations in 2006 between the youths of the [[Syndicalist League]], [[Mi, Niteraljci!|Us, Liberals!]], and the [[Confian Democratic Party]], aiming to legalize same-sex marriage.
In 2010, the party greatly refined its ideology under the leadership of accelerationist [[Kurki Sirol|Kokarăn "Kurki" Siron]], adopting the "Abundance for Confia 2020 Platform", a supply-side progressive platform that aimed to solve the cost of living crisis by "allowing the market to deliver more than any government could". This included overhauling land use rights defined in the [[Nation's Land and Homes Act]], lifting regulations on automated labour, and the party's iconic proposal of boosting energy supply through permitting and encouraging the mass construction of atomic energy plants.

The party reoriented itself around Kolkovian Modernism in 2028, renaming itself to the "**Atom Party - Confian Modernist Movement**". [[Kalsaper Jasretej]] was elected president under the party in 2038.
