The **Confian Liberal Party** (in Confian: Końfiskej Niteralej Parcesse/Коњфіскей Нітѣралей Парцессе) was a right-wing liberal party in [[Confia]]. The party advocated for a classically liberal model, positioning itself against the institution of syndicates and against the state-led industrial policy.

The party had  its origins in the Free Association of Liberal Unions, an organization founded in 1920 by [[Zanja Banelužni|Žanelj "Zanja" Banelužni]], a [[Farsehec]] writer and poet—in the midst of the [[Confian Secession War]]. Following the Farsehec tradition, Zanja maintained strong ties to labor unions, adopting a liberal model oriented toward social democracy. At the time, the party's platform advocated for wage subsidies for industrial workers and a mixed economy.
After Zanja's death, [[Senjap Kunsekowić]] was chosen as the organization's leader; he began adopting a political stance similar to that of the [[Liberty and Fatherland Movement]], himself beinga recent graduate of the [[University of New Kentu]]. The group also gained an anticlerical ethos, criticizing the Synodal Church and advocating for state atheism.
The party founded the [[Free State of Gorepalje]] during the Anarchic Period—a short-lived, unrecognized republic in the Gorepalje region. The party was officially founded within this country, and Kunsekowić was elected its first president in the first universal suffrage elections in Confian history. During the KAFF Mountain Campaign, the state ceded its territory to the syndicalists without resistance.
The party was banned following the [[Presidential Empowerment Amendment]], with its leaders and key figures facing imprisonment or exile. A significant number of party members joined the ranks of the NBSIH, bolstering the guerrilla struggle against the [[Paulowić regime]] alongside [[Mariana Torres]], [[Genrih Maskernie]] and other syndicalists. Following the [[Bayonet Revolution]], the party continued its alliance with Torres, achieving excellent results in the [[List of Confian elections#1977 Confian general election|1977 general election]] and forming the Orange Coalition, which elected [[Lenseka Japlenowina]] as Prime Minister.
