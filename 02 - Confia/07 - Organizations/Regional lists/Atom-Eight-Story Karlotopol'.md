**Atom-Eight-Story Karlotopol'** is a regional electoral list active in the state of [[Karlotopol']]. 
It was created in 2003 by a group of [[List of political positions#Kawjosism|housing density activists]] led by urban planner [[Nis Soler]] for the mayoral and city council elections of [[Carlotopolis]], naming itself after their flagship proposal of raising the height limit in most of the city to eight stories, matching the historical centre. It merged with the Karlotopol' Regional [[Atom Party]] in 2029.
