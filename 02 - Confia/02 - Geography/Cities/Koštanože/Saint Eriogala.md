**Saint Eriogala** is a working-class suburb of [[Imgospalje]]. 

The suburb was established in the 1860s, having been built by the Southern Armotist Collective and planned by the [[Teššan family]], the de facto owners of the collective. It was inhabited by industrial workers who had migrated to Imgospalje and settled there because of the attractive prices. During most of its history, it suffered from chronic problems, such as bureaucracy in administration, poor cleanliness, and a lack of nearby services. These issues were mitigated with the integration with the main city of Imgospalje from [[Lenseka Japlenowina]]'s urban reforms during her tenure as mayor of the latter.

It is the birthplace of [[Lorelaj Paulowić]].
