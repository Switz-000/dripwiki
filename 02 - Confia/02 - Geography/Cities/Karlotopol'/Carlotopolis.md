**Carlotopolis** (Confian: Karlotopol'/Карлотопољ) is a major city in Confia. It is widely considered the cultural heart of Confia, being the epicentre for many cultural productions, particularly the Confian cinema industry. The [[Theatre of the Nation]] is located in Carlotopolis.
Carlotopolis was the capital of [[General Government of Confia|Confia]] from 1789 to 1834, when it was moved to [[Imgospalje]].
