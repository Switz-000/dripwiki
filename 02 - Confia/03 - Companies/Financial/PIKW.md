**PIKW**, an acronym for **Ploít Konfiskae Wjaže**, meaning **Bank of the Confian Nation** is a Confian bank. Until the late 1990s and early 2000s, it functioned largely as a [[ragged-sleeve|Ragged-sleeve]] enterprise. It financed 64.3% of Confian lending operations in 1988, down to 18.2% in 2008.
