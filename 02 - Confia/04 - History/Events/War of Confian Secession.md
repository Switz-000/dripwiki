The **War of Confian Secession** (1927 to 1950) was fought between the [[Dripstanian Empire]] and the secessionist [[Free Confian Republic]] for the eastern territories of the empire. It ended after the death of [[Emperor Sallemaj]] with the [[Treaty of Sužielaj]] of 1950, which split the empire into two autonomous provinces. The empire formally ended four years later, in 1954, with the [[Tahuni Accords]].
