**Ragged-sleeve** (Confian: Zapartej-zilo/Запартей-зило) is an informal term for a particular class of rent-seeking capitalists active in [[Confia]] from its formation in the 1750s to roughly after the 1999-2000 trials.

After the end of the [[Paulowić regime]], they were primarily associated with [[Boris Serec]] and his [[Armotist Democratic Party]]. Prominent ragged sleeves were [[Olga Karwenna]] and [[Jagan Iliož]].
