The **Theatre of the Nation** is a theatre complex located in [[Carlotopolis]], Confia. It is known for its enormous size, spanning over 25 hectares and containing 20 showing rooms and a total of 40,000 seats. It contains a large amount of internal pathways, shops and restaurants inside its facilities.  
It began construction in 1844 and was inaugurated on 05/12/1849. The construction was funded by the [[Ragged-sleeve|ragged-sleeve]] Ilioz family that headed [[Žošewoš Machinery]].
The complex is of immense importance to Confian culture, to the point where it is said that "Every Confian is born twice: once to their mother, once to the Theatre."
