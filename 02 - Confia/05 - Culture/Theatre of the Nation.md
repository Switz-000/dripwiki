The **Theatre of the Nation** is a theatre located in [[Carlotopolis]], Confia. It is known for its enormous size, spanning over 25 hectares and containing 20 showing rooms and a total of 40,000 seats. Due to its gigantic size, it contains a large amount of internal pathways, shops and restaurants inside its facilities. 
It began construction in 1844 and was inaugurated in Verenian 12, 1849.
