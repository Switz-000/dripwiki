On October 30th, 2011, [[Ganzer Teahowić]], the first President of the [[Confia|Confian Social Republic]], was assassinated while returning from a meeting with the Confian Federation of Labour in the south side of [[Imgospalje]] along with two of his bodyguards. He was rushed to the [[Verene Central Hospital]], but died during the trip. His wife, [[Dorwńa Dasunaha]], survived the attack.
