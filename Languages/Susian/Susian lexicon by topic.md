# Lexicon by topic

Every attested Susian word, one placement each, sorted by English meaning within eighteen topics. Look here before coining: search the meaning, not the Susian form.

Nouns are unmarked. Verbs carry `(v.)`, adjectives `(adj.)`, adverbs `(adv.)`. Derivation lives in the by-root file, not here.

**Filing rules.** File by what a word means, not what it is built from. Every ism goes to Ideology while its base noun stays home. Agent nouns follow their domain rather than People.

## Gaps this pass surfaced

Meanings the vocabulary has no word for, ranked by how often the project already needs them.

**Governance and law:** court, judge (person), police, prosecutor, prison, treaty, amendment, referendum, senate, president, governor, veto, citizenship, oath.

**Economics:** currency, exchange rate, wage, salary, contract labour, corporation, share, dividend, insurance, mortgage, subsidy, tariff.

**Enumerated by canon but uncoined:** open carry and concealed carry (both flagged in the firearm regulation article), the Cericeiro hunting honorific behind Behemoth Tamer, a genus term for bovines.

**Everyday nouns with no entry:** food beyond the word for food itself, any drink, any article of clothing, any body part except blood, any number above six, any month or season.
