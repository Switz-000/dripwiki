# Lexicon by root

Every attested Susian word, filed under the root it derives from. Composites appear under their head element with a pointer from the other element. Glosses live here; the by-topic file holds the same words sorted by meaning.

Nouns are unmarked. Verbs carry `(v.)`, adjectives `(adj.)`, adverbs `(adv.)`.

## Open questions

**Duplicates to resolve**

- Lečove and Velev both gloss as tax.
- Bvrotoje and Ňarich both gloss as city. Ňarich has a derivative (Ňaruch), so it looks primary.
- Kal and Jarne both gloss as against (prepositions, filed in grammar).
- Ge, Ger, and Gaj are three words for and, with Ge also covering with alongside Pel (prepositions, filed in grammar).

**Form collisions**

- Drivire is both positive liberty (noun) and to free (verb).
- Vulre is both dreamer (noun) and to dream (verb). The -er agent suffix would give Vulrer.

**Filing anomalies in the source**

- Hunoch (ant) is listed under Verbos.
- Retevere (import) is listed under Substantivos but glossed as a verb.

**Head position is inconsistent, and this is a real design question.** Institutional compounds are head-final: Virkroch is a kind of power, Aruzespruje is a kind of governance, Tǎlmutoj is a kind of law. Agent compounds and given names are head-initial: Mejdel is a kind of eater, Veronique is a kind of bringer, Doremoj is a kind of gift. Both patterns are attested. Either Susian permits both by word class, or one set is irregular and inherited from Old Susian. The answer determines how every future compound gets built.

**Unglossed bound roots**

- Agǎ- yields benevolence and love. Kind or good would fit both.
- Sinž- yields union, syndicalist, and the pejorative Sinže.
- Ňotrič is glossed little friend in the names list, but Ňotrí- elsewhere is a reduction of Noňotrive (capital), as in Ňotrídulij. One of the two is wrong.
- Kroch appears as a free noun meaning power and as a bound element meaning -cracy. Consistent, but worth stating as a rule.
