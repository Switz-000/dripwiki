### The Panopticon with a smile

Total Data Integration:

- You might think Soites is a large retailer, but don’t be fooled, they’re a data company that just happens to sell products
    
- SoitesCart: With bluetooth-enabled wheels (tracks movement), weight sensors (knows what you put in and what you take out), handle pressure sensors (harder grip = more stress). Soites knows: which aisles you visit, in what order, how long you stand in front of each section, the items you picked up and which you put back. “Now available at your closest [[Soitesmart]]!”
    
- [[SoitesCard]]: “Earn 3% cashback on every purchase! No annual fee! Accepted everywhere!” Soites loses money on the cards, the data is worth way more than the losses. They track every purchase (not just at Soites), they can know what you eat, what are your hobbies, your family size, the medications you take, everything. 37% of [[Susia|susian]] adults own a SoitesCard.
    
- The Database: Real-time updates, cross referenced with purchase history, linked to your Soites Card and integrated with your online behavior all being run by a prediction algorithm
