History as analysed by [[Ňotrič Apaj]]. For [[Ňotrič Apaj|Apaj]]'s perscripton of what society should be, see [[Apajianism]].

# Foundations

- Reality is material.
- History has a direction. Not linear, but verifiable. The arrow points toward less suffering.
- Human nature is not fixed. What appears innate is often adaptive response to material conditions.
- Consciousness is self-ownership. "I think, therefore I am, therefore I possess myself." The [[Versijian Self-Axiom]] is the only truly universal human fact.

# History of Violence and Rational Conflict

- Scarcity is the master variable of human history. Every major institution, religious, political, cultural is downstream of it.
- Violence is rational under scarcity. War, tribalism, hierarchy. They were adaptive solutions to real problems.
- The industrial revolution was the first genuine rupture. For the first time in history, material scarcity became solvable rather than inevitable.
- Progress is fragile but demonstrable. Per capita violence has declined. Disease has retreated.

# Scarcity Ladder

- Scarcity has layers. Solve the bottom layer and you expose the one above it.
- Material scarcity: food, shelter, medicine. The oldest enemy. Largely defeated in developed societies.
- Security scarcity: freedom from arbitrary violence and domination. Partially solved by rule of law.
- Existential scarcity: meaning, identity, belonging, purpose. The layer the industrial world exposed by solving the ones beneath it.
	- Modern conflict lives here. Wars over river names. These existential scarcity wearing political clothes.
- Each layer requires different solutions. Material tools, technology, agriculture, medicine. Existential tools not yet invented.

# Theory of Religion and Ideology

> *See: [[Impotence Doctrine]]*

- Religion is a technology of impotence. Built to manage what could not be controlled: weather, disease, death, luck.
- Gods are mirrors of human limitation. Every pantheon maps the boundaries of what a civilization could not master.
- Secularization is empirical evidence. As material conditions improve, religious adherence organically declines. 
- Ideology functions identically to religion. Nationalism, dogma, political faith, all fill the same psychological role. All downstream of scarcity.
- This does not make religion or ideology evil. They were genuine solutions to genuine problems. They become dangerous when the problems change but the solutions don't.
