# List of political stances

An atomic vocabulary of political positions used across [[Dripstan]]. Each stance is a single coherent position, not an ideology. Ideologies are defined in [[List of ideologies]] as combinations of stances.

Link to individual stances using heading anchors: `[[List of political stances#Civic Armament]]`.

Stances marked _(historical)_ are no longer active positions in mainstream politics but appear in historical contexts. Stances marked _(Susia-specific)_ have no close equivalent elsewhere in Dripstan.

---

## Philosophy

### Sapientism

The position that moral and political consideration is owed to all beings capable of knowing choice, regardless of species or origin. Includes Tekurs, Dripstanians, Ariwaroids, and any other sapient peoples. Grounds arguments for minority civic integration and, in its more radical forms, for the moral status of cognitively enhanced non-humans.

### Cosmopolitanism

The position that all sapient beings are members of a single universal community above any national or ethnic one. Advocates universal moral standards, cross-border solidarity, and institutional platforms for political expression across national lines. Tends to sit in tension with nationalism in all its forms.

### Transhumanism

The position that technological modification of human biology and cognition is a positive development and a natural extension of self-determination. Cognitive enhancement is its most current expression, primarily in [[Susia]].

### Bio-Conservatism

The position that the unmodified human form has intrinsic value and that technological alteration of cognition or biology carries risks that justify caution or outright opposition. Not necessarily religious in origin.

## Constitutional and Governance

### Federalism

The distribution of sovereign authority between the national government and subnational governments. The default constitutional structure of [[Susia]]; present in modified forms in post-war [[Confia]].

### Centralism

The concentration of governing authority in the national government. The operating mode of the [[Lorelaj Paulowić|Paulović]] government in Confia and of most imperial administrations across Dripstanian history.

### States' Rights

The position that subnational governments are the primary locus of legitimate authority and that national power should be narrow and strictly enumerated. In Susia, the defining constitutional stance of the [[Coalition of State's Rights]] and during the pre-transition era, the [[Susian Liberal Party]]

### Regionalism

The position that distinct geographic, cultural, or economic regions within a nation deserve meaningful political autonomy or formal representation as regions rather than as administrative subdivisions. Distinct from separatism in that it seeks power within the existing state rather than outside it. Active in Susian states, in Zaphonian regional politics, and historically in the captaincy rivalries of the [[Dripstanian Empire]].

### Competitive Federalism

The position, derived from [[Yário Kolkov|Kolkov]], that multiple governance models should coexist and compete for citizens, with mobility between them as the primary accountability mechanism. The theoretical basis of the Susian [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ system]].

### Technocracy

Governance by credentialed experts rather than elected representatives. In Susia, institutionalized in the [[Chamber of Experts]] and the [[Secretariat of Efficiency]].

### Meritocracy

The principle that advancement and authority should be based on demonstrated competence rather than birth, wealth, or connection. Complementary to technocracy but distinct: meritocracy governs who rises within any system; technocracy is a claim about what kind of system is legitimate.

### Oligarchy

The concentration of effective political power in a small group, typically defined by wealth, family, or institutional position, regardless of formal constitutional arrangements. A descriptive accusation more often than a declared position. Associated with the [[Ragged-sleeve|ragged-sleeve]] industrial families in Confia and with Yarnojte executive influence in Susia by its critics.

### Corporatocracy

The position that corporations are legitimate governance actors with territorial authority. The Susian FEZ system is its partial institutional expression; critics apply the label pejoratively to the same arrangement.

### Republicanism

Government as a public trust, not the property of any faction, class, or dynasty. The foundational constitutional commitment of Susia since 1954 and of post-war Confia since 1977.

### Constitutionalism

The position that enforceable legal limits on state power are a precondition for legitimate government. Central to [[Armadesh Versij|Versij's]] philosophy and the Susian constitutional tradition.

### Presidentialism

The concentration of executive authority in a directly elected president with a strong independent mandate. The Susian system is presidentialist in structure, modified by confidence votes, the [[Chamber of Experts]] and the [[Susian Senate]].

### Parliamentarism

Executive authority derived from and accountable to the legislature. The dominant model in post-war Confia and in Kapiogg following its constitutional transition.

## Economic

### Free Trade

The elimination of tariffs and trade barriers between nations. Associated across Dripstan with post-war economic recovery and with agreements such as the [[Lasman Economic Initiative]].

### Protectionism

The use of tariffs, quotas, and trade barriers to shield domestic industry. The dominant trade stance of Confia through the Paulowić era and the Serec government; present in varying degrees across most Dripstanian economies.

### Mercantilism

The position that national economic strength depends on accumulating trade surpluses, controlling strategic resources, and subordinating trade partners to domestic interests. _(Historical)_ The operating logic of [[Dripstanian Empire|Dripstanian]] imperial economic policy; echoed in Confia's arrangements with Zaphonia under the [[New Oil Agreement]].

### Fiscal Conservatism

The position that long-term sustainability of public finances requires restraint on debt and deficits. Associated in Confia with the [[Senjap Kunsekowić|Kunsekowić]] government's stabilization program following the Roškoša failures.

### Industrialism

The position that industrial production is the foundation of national wealth, power, and social progress, and that policy should actively promote it. Distinct from developmentalism in that it does not require state leadership; private industrial expansion is equally acceptable. The background economic assumption of the Dripstanian Empire and of most Dripstanian states through the twentieth century.

### Dirigisme

Active state direction of the economy through planning, industrial policy, and strategic investment. The operating mode of Confia's Mixed Planning era; present in softer forms across most Dripstanian development economies.

### Developmentalism

State-led industrialization and import substitution as the path to economic modernity. The aspiration of Zaphonian [[Tahuni School of Economics|Tahuni School]] economists and of several post-war Confian governments.

### Welfarism

The position that the state should provide social insurance, healthcare, education, and pensions as a baseline.

### Redistributionism

The use of taxation and transfer policy to reduce wealth inequality. Distinct from welfarism in its emphasis on narrowing the gap rather than providing a floor.

### Producerism

Priority given to industrial and agricultural output over consumption or finance. Associated with Confian syndicalist economic culture and the ragged-sleeve industrial interest.

### Extractivism

National economic strategy built on resource extraction for export. The dominant model in [[Zaphonia]] under both the First Republic and the Union of Zaphonian Syndicates; present in [[Kamino]]'s agricultural export economy.

### Syndicalism

> *Main article: [[Syndicalism]]*

Worker ownership and management of the means of production through syndicates. The founding economic model of Confia; survived in institutional form after 1977 despite the retreat of its economic content.

### Cooperativism

Worker- or community-owned enterprises as the preferred economic unit, without the centralized state apparatus of syndicalism. A minority position in post-war Confia; present in worker-owned formations such as the [[Sužielaj Energy Cooperative]].

### Automationalism

The position that automation of labor is a social and economic good to be accelerated rather than managed cautiously. Primarily a Susian position, associated with [[Troli Ustaras]] and the Modernist Alliance's economic wing.

### Post-Scarcity

The position that technological abundance can eliminate material want as a social problem. An aspiration more than a current policy stance; associated with the utopian wing of TU's corporate culture.

### Universal Income

Unconditional cash transfers to all citizens regardless of employment status. Proposed by the Modernist Alliance's utopian faction in Susia; not enacted as of 2080.

## Social

### Conservatism

Preservation of established social institutions, traditions, and hierarchies. A broad position across Dripstan; in Susia it runs from moderate constitutional conservatism to the traditionalist wing of the Coalition.

### Progressivism

Reform of social institutions toward greater equality and individual freedom. Present across the Susian and Confian center-left; rarely used as a self-description in either country.

### Traditionalism

Explicit rejection of modernity in favor of pre-industrial or pre-republican social norms. Strongest in [[Nessel]] and rural [[Vitrika]] in Susia and in religiously conservative regions of [[Pierej]].

### Communitarianism

Priority of community bonds and collective obligation over individual autonomy. A common undercurrent in Sorzenko culture and Cericeiro identity in Susia; also present in the glysan tradition of [[Kamino]].

### Individualism

Priority of personal freedom and self-determination over collective demands. The default philosophical stance of Versijian liberalism in Susia.

### Secularism

The exclusion of religious authority from public and political life. The operating norm of the Susian federal government; largely absent in Pierej and Yakiria.

### Armotist Confessionalism

The political expression of Armotist religious values in governance. Distinct from private religious practice; refers specifically to the position that the state should reflect Armotist moral teaching in law and policy. Active in Confia's [[Armotist Democratic Party]] and in parts of the Susian Coalition.

### Multiculturalism

Active state support for the coexistence of distinct cultural communities. Relevant across Dripstan in contexts involving indigenous populations, diaspora communities, and post-war demographic mixing.

### Assimilationism

The expectation that minority communities adopt the dominant national culture over time. The implicit policy of most Dripstanian states toward indigenous and minority populations through most of their histories.

### Tekur Integrationism

_(Susia-specific)_ Full civic and legal integration of the [[Tekur]] people into Susian society on equal terms. Achieved formally by 1997; the debate has since shifted to the pace and depth of economic integration.

### Tekur Separatism

_(Susia-specific)_ Autonomous or independent Tekur political institutions. A minority position; present in [[New Celiolaj]] regional politics but without significant national representation.

## Nationalism

### Nationalism

The position that the nation and the state should be coextensive, and that national self-determination is a fundamental political value. A family of positions rather than a single stance; subdivided below.

### Civic Nationalism

The variant that defines national membership through shared political commitment and legal citizenship rather than ethnicity, religion, or ancestry. Membership is open to anyone who accepts the civic compact.

### Susian Nationalism

_(Susia-specific)_ A form of civic nationalism that defines Susian nationhood as a social contract between citizens and the constitutional order, centered on a shared commitment to the perpetuation of the "Project of Liberty": democracy, property rights, free speech, and gun rights. Agreement on process rather than outcomes.

### Ethnonationalism

The variant that defines national membership through shared ancestry and ethnic origin. Present as a legacy position in northern Susia; more active in Zaphonian politics around [[Huttivia]] territorial claims and in Kaminoan clan politics.

### Racial Nationalism

The variant that defines national membership through racial categories. A fringe position in contemporary Dripstan; historically associated with the Dripstanian Empire's treatment of indigenous peoples and the Tekurubicni Patrol era.

### Religious Nationalism

The variant that defines national membership through shared religion, seeking to unite a people with a common faith into a national identity. Active in Yakiria and parts of Pierej; associated with the Armotist Church of the Confian Synod's political wing.

## Foreign Policy

### Civilizational Supremacism

_(Susia-specific)_ The position that Susia represents a superior form of civilization whose survival and triumph over [[Ashgerad]] is not merely a geopolitical interest but a philosophical necessity. Losing would not just be a military defeat but would invalidate the Versijian premise that democracy is the correct answer to the question of governance.

### Democratic Evangelism

_(Susia-specific)_ The position that Susia has a moral duty to actively spread democracy and liberal values beyond its borders, through support for pro-democracy movements, conditional aid, and where necessary regime change. Evolved from anti-syndicalist interventionism during the [[Continental Divide]].

### Reconciliationism

The position that former adversaries should be integrated into shared economic and security architecture rather than isolated or punished. The intellectual basis of the [[Lasman Economic Initiative]] and the expansion of the [[ONDD]] to include Confia and Kamino.

### Continental Fortress Doctrine

_(Susia-specific)_ The position that Susia must maintain military and economic dominance over the Dripstan continent as its primary defensive perimeter against Ashgerad. Justifies ONDD expansion, forward military presence in allied nations, and intervention in neighboring affairs when the continental balance is threatened.

### Pan-Continental Unity

The aspiration to eventually unite Dripstan's nations into a federal union or confederation. Treats the shared [[Dripstanian Empire|Dripstanian]] imperial heritage as the basis for eventual political reunification. A long-horizon vision rather than a current policy program.

### Isolationism

The position that a nation should minimize foreign entanglements and treat domestic affairs as the governing priority. A recessive position in Susian politics since the Continental Divide; more active in [[Dripland]] and [[Yakiria]] politics.

### Détente

The position that hostility between rival powers should be reduced through negotiation and mutual accommodation. Associated in Susia with [[Ergagério Sienes|Sienes]] and the early 1970s; discredited in Susian mainstream politics after the [[Continental Divide#The Sutsa-Fuhu Civil War (1972–1973)|Sutsa-Fuhu outcome]].

### Colonialism

The position that a nation has the right or duty to extend authority over foreign territories and peoples, governing them as subjects rather than citizens. _(Historical)_ The foundational foreign policy of the Dripstanian Empire; the institutional logic behind the General Governments of Confia and Kamino.

### Neo-Colonialism

The use of economic leverage, debt, and asymmetric trade arrangements to exercise effective control over formally independent states without direct territorial administration. Critics apply the term to Confia's arrangements with Zaphonia under the New Oil Agreement and to some Susian Yarnojte operations in ONDD partner states.

## Military and Security

### Civic Armament

_(Susia-specific)_ The position that an armed citizenry is a civic obligation and a structural guarantee against tyranny, not merely a personal right. Grounded in [[Armadesh Versij|Versij's]] "citizen guards of the republic" argument and the memory of [[Jartes II]]'s disarmament campaign before the [[Liberal Revolts]].

### Conscriptionism

The position that mandatory military service is a civic duty, a social institution for cross-class solidarity, and a practical guarantor of the right to rebel. Active in Susia; present in modified forms in Kapiogg and Pierej.

### Militarism

High military expenditure and permanent readiness as a governing priority. Associated across Dripstan with states facing existential pressure: Susia during the Continental Divide, the Paulowić government in Confia, and [[Dripland]] during the Wars of the Forest.

### Intelligence Expansionism

The position that broad surveillance and intelligence powers are a necessary price of security. The operating assumption of the Susian [[Frumentarze]] and of equivalent bodies across Dripstan.

## Science, Technology, and Enhancement

### Techno-Optimism

The position that technology is the primary solution to social, economic, and political problems. The background assumption of TU corporate culture and the Susian Modernist Alliance's technology policy.

### Techno-Skepticism

The position that technology-driven solutions carry human and social costs that require active management. Not opposed to technology; skeptical of the claim that technical solutions resolve political problems.

### Enhancement Advocacy

The position that cognitive enhancement is a right and a social good to be encouraged and made accessible. The Modernist Alliance's official stance in Susia.

### Enhancement Skepticism

The position that cognitive enhancement carries unknown long-term risks and creates structural coercion, and should be approached with caution. Distinct from Bio-Conservatism in that it does not reject enhancement in principle; it objects to the current pace and the absence of long-term data.

### Data Sovereignty

The position that individuals or communities hold a primary right to control data generated about them. Contested by [[Soites Group|Soites']] business model and the Frumentarze's operational requirements.

## Energy and Environment

### Fossil Fualism

The defense of fossil fuel industries as economically and strategically essential. Associated with [[POCOIL]] and the Troli and Postia oil sectors in Susia; the dominant energy stance in Zaphonia and Sutsa-Fuhu.

### Nuclearism

Active advocacy for nuclear energy expansion as the correct and morally positive energy source. Near-consensus in Susian energy policy since the post-war boom; rare elsewhere in Dripstan where nuclear infrastructure is less developed.

### Energy Nationalism

The position that strategic energy resources should be under state control or subject to state direction. Active in Zaphonian politics around the oil industry; present in the histories of Confia's [[Końteraf]] and Susia's POCOIL.

### Environmentalism

Active protection of natural ecosystems as a policy priority. A minority position across most of Dripstan; most visible in disputes over extractive industries and offshore drilling.
