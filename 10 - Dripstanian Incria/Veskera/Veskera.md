**Veskera** is an archipelago state in [[Incria]] and an [[ODDN]] ally of [[Susia]]. It refines and ships the sorophine that [[Yar-Firol]] and [[Sekyo]] extract from [[Bershad]], and its smuggling registry carries the [[Western Coalition Zone]]'s sorophine to international markets.

## Geography

Veskera is an archipelago and has no land borders.

## Economy

> *Main article: [[Sorophine]]*

Veskera refines and ships Bershadi sorophine delivered through Yar-Firol and Sekyo. Its smuggling registry inherits the character of the empire's ports that "don't keep records they don't have to keep".

## Foreign Relations

Veskera took part in the [[Bershadi Conference]] and has recognized the southern Bershadi government since. It joined the Susian nation-building intervention in Bershad from around 2000, and sanctions the Western Coalition Zone. It does not practice [[Modern degredo|degredo]] directly, but pays Yar-Firol a fee to handle its convicts.
