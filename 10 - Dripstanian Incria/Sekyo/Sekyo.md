**Sekyo** is a country in [[Incria]], made up of the island of [[Yerpos]] and the coastal strip of [[Lawhá]]. It was chartered to the Yerpos-Kelej Company in 1831, became a Dripstanian dominion in 1843 and gained independence in 1954. It has been the United Republic of Sekyo since 1985. It draws on illicit extraction from [[Bershad]] through cross-border kinship, and presents its democratic transition by comparison with Bershad.

## Geography

Yerpos is a fertile volcanic island with a polyglot urban citizen class. Lawhá, a mainland strip running south and west of Yerpos along the coast, is a plantation and logging belt with a [[Xaya people|Xaya]] majority. It holds roughly 70% of Sekyo's population and has no proportionate political weight. On the mainland Sekyo borders [[Bershad]] to the west, along the Xaya northeast of Bershad, and [[Yar-Firol]] along a short border.

The Sekyan Free State began with a self-coup in 1956 and a military junta from 1956 to 1957, after which [[Teltev Zumir]] governed until 1976. Fifteen governments held office in the 792 days of the Provisional Government.

## Economy

> *Main article: [[Sorophine]]*

Illicit sorophine extracted from Bershad is laundered into Sekyo's official economy through state-owned agricultural and chemical boards.

## Foreign Relations

### Bershad

Sekyo draws on Bershadi extraction through cross-border kinship among the [[Haiel people|Haiel]] and the Xaya. Susian military basing in Sekyo gives the operation depth. Official rhetoric of "stabilizing southern Bershad" covers the maintenance of access to extraction zones. Sekyo presents its democratic transition by comparison with Bershad.

Sekyo has recognized the southern Bershadi government since the [[Bershadi Conference]]. It runs a small program of [[Modern degredo|modern degredo]], returning Lawhá Xaya defendants to Bershad.

### Susia

Sekyo hosts Susian military bases and is an [[ODDN]] ally of [[Susia]] in Incria.
