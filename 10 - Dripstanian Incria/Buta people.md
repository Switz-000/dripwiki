The **Buta** are a people of [[Incria]] living across [[Yar-Firol]], [[Bershad]] and [[Ashgerad]]. In the 2080s they are stateless. Every state of the region has persecuted them: Yar-Firol carried out a genocide against them, the peoples of Bershad treated them as prey, and Ashgerad deports them.

## Distribution

Before the [[Buta genocide]] the Buta made up about 48% of Yar-Firol's population and inhabited its interior. The 1950 census recorded them as 5% of Bershad's population, living inland in the northwest along the border with Yar-Firol, 52% pagan and 31% Armotist.

## History

### Yar-Firol

> *Main article: [[Buta genocide]]*

From 1963 the Yar-Firolian state carried out an ethnic cleansing of the inland Buta. Over one million were displaced into Bershad. Assimilated coastal Buta professionals were spared and later formed the [[Yar-Firolian Buta survivor caucus]].

### Bershad

Under the [[Nguan regime]] the Buta were added to the prey tier. Refugees from Yar-Firol were subjected to predation by [[San'motko]] between around 1972 and 1976. In the [[Bershadi Civil War]] Buta militias fought among the eastern factions; their treatment depends on which faction controls the territory.

### Ashgerad

Ashgerad discriminates against its small Buta population and deports Buta to Bershad. The indigenous-purity framework of the [[Cult of Ryо̄žža]] sorts peoples by which indigenous group they belong to, not by whether they are indigenous.

## Diaspora

The Buta diaspora spans [[Susia]], through the second wave of the [[Bershadi diaspora]], as well as [[Sekyo]], [[Veskera]] and likely [[Confia]]. Buta scholars of the second wave produce critical scholarship that can address every state of the region at once.
