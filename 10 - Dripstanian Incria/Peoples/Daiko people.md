The **Daiko** are an inland people of northwestern [[Bershad]]. Their cannibalism functions as medical care and as a response to scarcity. They belong to the [[Western Coalition Zone]].

## Distribution

The 1950 census recorded the Daiko as 7% of Bershad's population, 92% pagan.

## Practices

> *Main article: [[Cannibalism in Bershad#Daiko]]*

The Daiko legend of [[Mt'yorsur]] concerns a chief said to have extended his life by eating the hearts of children.

## History

Coastal Parund and Mtole raided the Daiko. Under the [[United Republics of East Incria]] the Daiko opposed the government. Under the [[Nguan regime]] they were a tolerated-instrumental group and received third priority in land redistribution. In the [[Bershadi Civil War]] they joined the Western Coalition led by [[San'motko]].
