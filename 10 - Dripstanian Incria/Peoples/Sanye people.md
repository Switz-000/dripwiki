The **Sanye** are an Armotist people of the coast of [[Yar-Firol]] and of [[Bershad]]. [[Bejňar Keke]], first president of Yar-Firol, and [[Umbata Zambar]], leader of the [[United Republics of East Incria]], were both Sanye.

## Distribution

Before the [[Buta genocide]] the Sanye made up about 38% of Yar-Firol's population, in the coastal zone. They are absent from the 1950 census table of Bershad.

## History

Under imperial rule the Sanye were among the peoples the empire favoured and integrated. Under the United Republics they supported the government. In the [[Bershadi Civil War]] the Sanye of Bershad's northeast fought among the eastern factions.
