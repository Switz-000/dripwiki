The **Kadus** are a people forming an ethnic enclave in the western interior of [[Bershad]]. They have never practiced cannibalism but belong to the [[Western Coalition Zone]], living among peoples who do.

## Distribution

The 1950 census recorded the Kadus as 3% of Bershad's population, 98% pagan, with no Dripstanian contact.

## History

Coastal Parund and Mtole raided the Kadus. Under the [[United Republics of East Incria]] the Kadus opposed the government. Under the [[Nguan regime]] they were a tolerated-instrumental group. In the [[Bershadi Civil War]] they joined the Western Coalition led by [[San'motko]].
