The **Xaya** are a people living in the northeastern interior of [[Bershad]] along the border with [[Sekyo]], and the majority of Sekyo's mainland strip of [[Lawhá]]. They practice funerary endocannibalism, ceremonial and rare.

## Distribution

The 1950 census recorded the Xaya as 5% of Bershad's population, 73% pagan. In Sekyo they are the majority of Lawhá, which holds roughly 70% of the country's population.

## Practices

> *Main article: [[Cannibalism in Bershad#Xaya]]*

## History

Under the [[United Republics of East Incria]] the Xaya were among the peoples opposed to the government. Under the [[Nguan regime]] they were a tolerated-instrumental group.

Sekyo draws on Bershadi extraction through Xaya kinship across the border, and returns Lawhá Xaya defendants to Xaya regions of Bershad under [[Modern degredo|modern degredo]].
