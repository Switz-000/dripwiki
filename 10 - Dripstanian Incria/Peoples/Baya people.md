The **Baya** are a people of the eastern coast of [[Bershad]], with a small spillover across the border with [[Yarteroy]]. They practiced seasonal ceremonial endocannibalism until their conversion to [[Armotism]].

## Distribution

The 1950 census recorded the Baya as 2% of Bershad's population, 74% Armotist and 8% pagan. Dripstanian contact with the Baya took the form of trade, conversion, integration and miscegenation.

## Practices

> *Main article: [[Cannibalism in Bershad#Baya]]*

Armotist missions ended the Baya practice by substituting an Armotist rite for it.

## History

Under the [[United Republics of East Incria]] the Baya supported the government.
