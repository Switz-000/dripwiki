The **Haiel** are a people of [[Bershad]] dominant on the south coast and the islands. Largely Armotist and integrated under imperial rule, they were among the chief targets of the [[Nguan regime]]. They formed much of the first wave of the [[Bershadi diaspora]] and the southern government recognized by [[Susia]].

## Distribution

The 1950 census recorded the Haiel as 23% of Bershad's population, 95% Armotist. They have never practiced cannibalism. Dripstanian contact with the Haiel took the form of trade, conversion, integration and miscegenation.

## History

Under the [[United Republics of East Incria]] the Haiel supported the government. In 1966 [[Boe Nguan]] had his main political opponent and other Haiel arrested for treason. The Haiel were placed in the prey tier, subjected to mass victimization between 1968 and 1972, and barred from fishing and boats on their coast in 1968.

The Haiel coastal class left in the first diaspora wave and was absorbed by [[Yar-Firol]]. Under [[Bejňar Keke]], Haiel facing persecution in Bershad were encouraged to settle the Yar-Firolian interior.

In the [[Bershadi Civil War]] a coalition of the Haiel and the coast won the election of around 2000, and the southern Haiel government has been recognized by Susia, [[Sekyo]] and [[Veskera]] since the [[Bershadi Conference]]. [[Sekyo]] draws on Bershadi extraction through Haiel kinship across the border.
