The **Mtole** are a people of southwestern [[Bershad]] whose territory runs from a coastal section deep into the interior. They practice gastronomic cannibalism, making no distinction between human and animal flesh. They are the junior partners and rivals of the [[Parund people|Parund]].

## Distribution

The 1950 census recorded the Mtole as 8% of Bershad's population, living in the southwest and mostly inland, 77% pagan.

## Practices

> *Main article: [[Cannibalism in Bershad#Mtole]]*

## History

Under the [[United Republics of East Incria]] the Mtole opposed the government. Under the [[Nguan regime]] they formed the junior predator tier and received second priority in land redistribution. Mtole resentment of Parund pretension has persisted through and after the regime. In the [[Bershadi Civil War]] they joined the Western Coalition led by [[San'motko]].
