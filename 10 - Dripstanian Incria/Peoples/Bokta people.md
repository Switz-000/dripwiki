The **Bokta** are a people of the coast of [[Yar-Firol]] and of [[Bershad]]. Under the [[Nguan regime]] they were treated as prey, and many left Bershad in the first wave of the [[Bershadi diaspora]].

## Distribution

The Bokta make up about 6% of Yar-Firol's population, in the coastal zone. They are absent from the 1950 census table of Bershad.

## History

Under the [[United Republics of East Incria]] the Bokta supported the government. Under the Nguan regime they were placed in the prey tier and, with the [[Haiel people|Haiel]], subjected to mass victimization between 1968 and 1972.
