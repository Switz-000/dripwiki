The **Yokno** are the largest people of [[Bershad]], living in the central interior. The [[Nguan regime]] divided them between a junior collaborator tier and prey. Yokno revanchists led the northern coalition in the [[Bershadi Civil War]].

## Distribution

The 1950 census recorded the Yokno as 25% of Bershad's population, 70% Armotist and 30% pagan. They have never practiced cannibalism. Dripstanian contact with the Yokno took the form of settlement, extraction and quotas. [[Hertpatt]] is predominantly Yokno.

## History

Coastal Parund and Mtole raided the Yokno. Under the [[United Republics of East Incria]] the Yokno supported the government.

Under the Nguan regime, pagan Yokno who renounced Armotism and accepted Parund primacy received a junior collaborator tier; the rest were progressively treated as prey. An educated Yokno cohort from the collaborator tier left in the first wave of the [[Bershadi diaspora]].

In the Bershadi Civil War Yokno revanchists and the Western Coalition formed the northern coalition, which rejected the election of around 2000 and was recognized by [[Yar-Firol]] and [[Ashgerad]].
