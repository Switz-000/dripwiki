The **Parund** are a people of [[Bershad]] living along the western seaboard. They practice ritualized exocannibalism with human predation as an expression of superiority, a pre-Dripstanian ideology of Parund supremacy. [[Boe Nguan]] was Parund, and the Parund were the dominant group of the [[Nguan regime]], [[San'motko]] and the [[Western Coalition Zone]].

## Distribution

The 1950 census recorded the Parund as 11% of Bershad's population, living along the western seaboard and 82% pagan. They keep to the coast.

## Practices

> *Main article: [[Cannibalism in Bershad]]*

The Parund eat only people from outside their community. They are rivals of the [[Mtole people|Mtole]], who also practice predation but treat it as ordinary food.

## History

Under the [[United Republics of East Incria]] the Parund were among the peoples opposed to the government. Under the Nguan regime they formed the dominant predator tier, received first priority in land redistribution, and saw their language sacralized in 1971 as the language of the cosmos and of the sovereign. In the [[Bershadi Civil War]] they formed the core of the Western Coalition led by San'motko.
