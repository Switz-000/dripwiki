**Tehtíe** is a country in [[Incria]], bordering [[Yar-Firol]] to the south and [[Ashgerad]] to the west, with the sea to the north and east. It is a source of legitimate [[sorophine]], and after Yar-Firolian independence Armotists from Tehtíe migrated to settle the Yar-Firolian interior.

## Geography

Tehtíe borders Yar-Firol to the south and Ashgerad to the west. The sea lies to the north and east.

## Economy

> *Main article: [[Sorophine]]*

Tehtíe is one of the sources of the legitimate sorophine pipeline to [[Susia]].

## Foreign Relations

Under [[Bejňar Keke]], Armotists from Tehtíe were encouraged to migrate to Yar-Firol and settle its interior.
