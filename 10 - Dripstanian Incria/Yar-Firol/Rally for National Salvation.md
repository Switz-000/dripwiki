The **Rally for National Salvation** (RNS) is the ruling party of [[Yar-Firol]]. It was founded by [[Bejňar Keke]] in 1962 and has governed continuously since, as the sole party until the managed democracy of the 2010s.

## History

The party has held power since Keke's consolidation in 1962. After Keke left office in 1989 another member of the party succeeded him, and as of the 2020s the party had never lost an election.

## Ideology

Officially the party is developmentalist, Yar-Firolian-nationalist, anti-imperial in rhetoric, modernist and secular-Armotist. In practice it is pro-Susian.
