**Bejňar Keke** was the first president of [[Yar-Firol]], ruling from 1962 to 1989 at the head of the [[Rally for National Salvation]]. He built Yar-Firol's one-party state, encouraged Armotist settlement of the country's interior, and presided over the [[Buta genocide]].

## Early Life

Keke was a [[Sanye people|Sanye]] Armotist. He emerged from the civil service and technical-administrative class that Yar-Firol inherited from the [[General Government of Incria]].

## Presidency

Keke took power in 1962 through an emergency-administrative consolidation, not a coup, and governed through a one-party state.

He built his legitimacy on order, prosperity and comparison: [[Bershad]] below and [[Ashgerad]] across the border. He encouraged Armotists from across [[Incria]], especially [[Haiel people|Haiel]] facing persecution in Bershad and Armotists from [[Tehtíe]], to migrate to Yar-Firol and settle its interior.

> *Main article: [[Buta genocide]]*

The ethnic cleansing of the inland [[Buta people|Buta]] began under his rule by 1963 and peaked between around 1965 and 1975.

## Personal Life

Keke had one child, who is not named.

## Legacy

Keke was dignified in public and mythologized in life, and was granted a state funeral.
