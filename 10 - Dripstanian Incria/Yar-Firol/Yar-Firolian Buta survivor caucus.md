The **Yar-Firolian Buta survivor caucus** is the informal network of coastal [[Buta people|Buta]] professionals in [[Yar-Firol]] who survived the [[Buta genocide]] and rose in the civil service, the foreign ministry, the intelligence services and academia. It is operationally indispensable to Yar-Firolian policy on [[Bershad]].

## History

Coastal Buta professionals who had assimilated were not targeted by the genocide and were retained in state service. From the 1980s onward members of the caucus brokered cross-border extraction relationships with Bershad. The caucus brokered Yar-Firol's recognition of the northern Bershadi government at the [[Bershadi Conference]].

## Operations

Members speak Buta, have surviving relatives in the Buta militia networks of Bershad, and staff Yar-Firol's diplomatic and intelligence channels into the country.

Buta kinship across the border was formed in shared silence: relatives on both sides were lost at the same time through the 1970s.
