**Yar-Firol** is a country in [[Incria]], bordering [[Bershad]] and [[Ashgerad]]. It inherited the capital, civil service and Armotist church infrastructure of the [[General Government of Incria]] and has been a one-party state under the [[Rally for National Salvation]] since 1962. The state carried out the [[Buta genocide]] from 1963, and its official prosperity is substantially funded by illicit extraction from Bershad laundered through state boards. It is the most influential outside actor in Bershadi affairs.

## Geography

Yar-Firol divides into a coastal zone and an interior. The coast is home to the [[Sanye people|Sanye]], [[Bokta people|Bokta]] and Dripstanian populations. The interior was historically the [[Buta people|Buta]] zone and was settled after independence by Armotist migrants from elsewhere in Incria. Yar-Firol borders Ashgerad directly to the west.

## History

### Imperial era

Under imperial rule Yar-Firol was the seat of the General Government of Incria. Dripstanians were concentrated in the cities and along the coast.

### Independence

Yar-Firol inherited the capital, the civil service and the Armotist church infrastructure of the General Government at independence. [[Bejňar Keke]] took power in 1962 through an emergency-administrative consolidation rather than a coup, and the Rally for National Salvation has governed since.

### Keke era (1962 to 1989)

> *Main article: [[Bejňar Keke]]*

Keke encouraged Armotists from across Incria to migrate to Yar-Firol and settle the interior, especially [[Haiel people|Haiel]] facing persecution in Bershad and in [[Tehtíe]]. The settlers have developed a distinct mixed culture of their own.

> *Main article: [[Buta genocide]]*

From 1963 the state carried out an ethnic cleansing of the inland Buta population, with its peak between around 1965 and 1975. Over one million Buta were displaced into Bershad.

During the [[Nguan regime]] in Bershad, Bershadi sorophine moved through Yar-Firolian ports, the only functional route out of the country. The extraction architecture of the later decades was built in these years. Yar-Firol also absorbed the first wave of the [[Bershadi diaspora]].

### Since 1989

Keke was succeeded in 1989 by another member of the Rally for National Salvation, who has held the office since. By the 2010s the state had taken the form of a managed democracy; as of the 2020s the ruling party had never lost an election.

Yar-Firol recognized the northern government of Bershad at the [[Bershadi Conference]], a recognition brokered by the [[Yar-Firolian Buta survivor caucus]]. By the 2080s it sanctions the [[Western Coalition Zone]].

## Government

Yar-Firol has been a one-party state under the Rally for National Salvation since 1962, in managed-democracy form since the 2010s.

## Economy

| --------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Official cash crop                | 25 to 30%                 |

> *Main article: [[Sorophine]]*

Extraction from Bershad is laundered into official prosperity through state-owned agricultural and chemical boards.

## Foreign Relations

Yar-Firol is officially anti-imperial in rhetoric and pro-Susian in practice, and is a member of the [[ODDN]]. Its regime has built its legitimacy on comparison with its neighbours: Bershad on one side, Ashgerad across the border on the other.

Yar-Firol has recognized the northern Bershadi government since the Bershadi Conference. It runs the largest [[Modern degredo|modern degredo]] program in the region, and handles Veskeran convicts under deportation orders for a fee.
