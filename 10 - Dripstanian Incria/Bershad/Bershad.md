**Bershad** is a country in [[Incria]], formerly part of the [[General Government of Incria]]. It became independent in 1962 and was ruled by the [[Nguan regime]] until the state collapsed in 1973. Since the [[Bershadi Civil War]] it has been partitioned between two governments, neither of them sovereign. Between 1962 and the 2020s an estimated six to ten million Bershadis died.

## Geography

Bershad borders [[Yar-Firol]] to the north, [[Sekyo]] and [[Yarteroy]] to the east, and [[Ashgerad]] along a short border in the northwest. The sea lies to the south, west and southeast.

> *Main article: [[Cultures of Bershad]]*

The [[Parund people|Parund]] hold the western seaboard and the [[Haiel people|Haiel]] the south coast and the islands. The [[Yokno people|Yokno]] occupy the central interior. The [[Buta people|Buta]] live in the northwest along the border with [[Yar-Firol]], the [[Xaya people|Xaya]] in the northeast along the border with [[Sekyo]], and the [[Baya people|Baya]] on the eastern coast along the border with [[Yarteroy]].

## History

### Imperial era

Bershad was governed as part of the General Government of Incria. In 1950 [[Filevir Gatojete]] held a referendum in each of the colonies on whether to remain part of the empire. Only creoles and Dripstanians could vote, and almost all colonies voted to remain.

### United Republics (1954 to 1962)

> *Main article: [[United Republics of East Incria]]*

In 1954 the General Government was reformed into the United Republics of East Incria, with a franchise restricted to property owners. [[Umbata Zambar]] led the republic from 1955 until a coup in 1962 dissolved it, and Bershad became independent.

### Nguan regime (1962 to 1973)

> *Main article: [[Nguan regime]]*

[[Boe Nguan]], a Parund, came to power in 1962 as Zambar's opposite, on a native-nationalist platform. His regime failed at reform, turned to terror and improvised crisis measures from 1968, and ended with the collapse of the state in 1973. Deaths under the regime are estimated at two to four million.

### Collapse (1973 to 1990)

After the collapse, [[San'motko]] ruled parts of the northwest and warlords divided the rest of the country. Nguan remained head of state as a figurehead until his death in 1981. No national authority existed.

### Civil war

> *Main article: [[Bershadi Civil War]]*

Open conflict between the consolidated zones began around 1990. The [[Bershadi Conference]] produced a constitution and an election around 2000, whose result was rejected; war resumed, and [[Susia]] intervened.

### Partition

The partition froze. As of the 2020s the southern government, recognized by Susia, Sekyo and [[Veskera]], held effectively a capital and two ports. The west is governed by San'motko as the [[Western Coalition Zone]].

## Government

Bershad has two governments, neither sovereign: a southern government and a northern government, each recognized by a different group of states since the Bershadi Conference.

## Economy

> *Main article: [[Sorophine]]*

No reliable economic data exists after 1968. The sorophine trade, laundered through neighbouring states, is the principal source of revenue for the zones.

## Foreign Relations

International recognition has been split since the Bershadi Conference: Susia, Sekyo and Veskera recognize the southern government; Yar-Firol and [[Ashgerad]] recognized the northern. Neighbouring states return convicts across the Bershadi border under the practice of [[Modern degredo|modern degredo]].

> *Main article: [[Bershad–Susia relations]]*

## Culture

> *Main articles: [[Cultures of Bershad]], [[Cannibalism in Bershad]]*

### Population

The population was probably 35 to 40 million before the Nguan regime and around 30 million by the 1990s. Cumulative deaths from 1962 to the 2020s are estimated at six to ten million. By the 2020s millions of internally displaced people lived in semi-permanent camps.
