**Umbata Zambar** was a [[Sanye people|Sanye]] Armotist who led the [[United Republics of East Incria]] from 1955 to 1962. He promoted a one-party system, arguing that multiparty democracy would fragment the republic along ethnic lines, and founded the [[United Independence Party]]. He was deposed in a coup in 1962; the republic was dissolved and [[Bershad]] became independent.

## Legacy

[[Boe Nguan]] rose to power as Zambar's opposite, on a platform of native nationalism.
