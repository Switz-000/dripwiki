**San'motko** is a [[Parund people|Parund]] supremacist organization in [[Bershad]]. It originated as the radical fringe of [[Boe Nguan]]'s coalition and served his regime as an instrument of terror, then captured the regime's program before the state collapsed in 1973. It has held northwestern Bershad as a parallel state since around 1975 and is the state party of the [[Western Coalition Zone]].

## History

### Origins (1962 to 1965)

San'motko originated during the reform phase of the [[Nguan regime]] as the radical fringe of Parund supremacism, among those who read each of Nguan's coalition compromises as a betrayal.

### Partnership with Nguan (1965 to 1973)

Its public partnership with Nguan was formalized between 1965 and 1966, at the time of his trip to [[Neoveli]] and the onset of his sorophine dependency. The organization was founded in 1966. Nguan used it in three roles:

- an instrument of terror against coalition partners judged insufficiently aligned;
- an operator for the regime's worst acts, allowing the regime to deny direction of them;
- a custodian of ritual purity, whose existence legitimated Nguan's compromises elsewhere.

Between around 1972 and 1976, San'motko subjected [[Buta people|Buta]] refugees fleeing the [[Buta genocide]] in [[Yar-Firol]] to predation on a large scale in the northwest. The campaign was directed, not spontaneous.

### Capture of the regime

The relationship between the regime and San'motko inverted. San'motko won the regime's internal ideological contest, and by the collapse of the state in 1973 Nguan was a figurehead.

### Parallel state (from 1975)

By around 1975 San'motko had consolidated northwestern Bershad as a parallel state. It has held the territory continuously since, through the [[Bershadi Civil War]] and the partition.

### Refoundation (1981)

San'motko refounded itself publicly in 1981, after Nguan's death.

### State party

> *Main article: [[Western Coalition Zone]]*

By the 2080s San'motko functions as the state party of the Western Coalition Zone. It led the Western Coalition in rejecting the result of the first Bershadi election around 2000.

## Ideology

From 1973 to around 1995, in the absence of a central government, San'motko presented itself publicly as the restoration of Parund traditional authority. Internally it operated as a political-revolutionary organization. It held both positions at once.
