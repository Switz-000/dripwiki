The **Bershadi Conference** was an internationally observed peace conference on [[Bershad]], convened by [[Susia]] at a neutral venue between around 1995 and 1998. It produced a brokered constitution, a transitional administration and the first internationally administered Bershadi election. Its lasting product was a split in international recognition between two Bershadi governments.

## Background

> *Main article: [[Bershadi Civil War]]*

Open conflict between the consolidated zones of Bershad had begun around 1990.

## Participants

[[Yar-Firol]], [[Sekyo]] and [[Veskera]] took part publicly while protecting their extraction interests in Bershad. [[Ashgerad]] was absent.

## Outcome

### Constitution

The Conference produced a constitution that every party signed and none accepted.

### Recognition

| Government recognized                         | Recognized by                  |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Southern ([[Haiel people\|Haiel]])            | [[Susia]], [[Sekyo]], [[Veskera]] |
| Northern ([[Yokno people\|Yokno]] and Western Coalition) | [[Yar-Firol]], [[Ashgerad]] |

Susia's recognition of the southern government has been continuous since.

### Transitional administration and election

A transitional administration governed from around 1998 to 2000. The first election, held around 1999 to 2000, was internationally administered. Susian helicopters delivered the ballot boxes, and Yar-Firolian and Sekyan electoral workers counted the votes in tents.

A coalition of the Haiel and the coast won narrowly. Within weeks the northern coalition of Yokno revanchists and the Western Coalition led by [[San'motko]] ([[Parund people|Parund]], [[Daiko people|Daiko]], [[Mtole people|Mtole]] and [[Kadus people|Kadus]]) rejected the result. [[Buta people|Buta]] militias mobilized defensively.

## Aftermath

Open war resumed around 2000.
