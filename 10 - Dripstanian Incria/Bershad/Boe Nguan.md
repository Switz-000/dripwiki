**Boe Nguan** was a [[Parund people|Parund]] nationalist who ruled [[Bershad]] from 1962 until his death in 1981. He governed in fact only until the collapse of the Bershadi state in 1973, after which he remained head of state as a figurehead held hostage in his villa. His rule is covered in [[Nguan regime]].

## Early Life

Nguan was born in a small village in inland Parund territory. He served as a public order officer in the [[General Government of Incria]].

## Presidency

> *Main article: [[Nguan regime]]*

Nguan came to power in 1962 on a native-nationalist platform and a campaign to "decolonize" Bershad from Dripstanian influence, with [[Ashgerad|Ashgeradi]] sponsorship.

### Reform (1962 to 1965)

His first years were spent on land redistribution favoring the Parund, commercial protectionism and the nationalization of foreign property. The reforms failed.

### Consolidation (1966 to 1968)

In 1966 he had his main political opponent arrested on charges of treason, and persecution of the [[Haiel people|Haiel]], Dripstanians and Armotists began. In 1967 he ordered the arrest of his Minister of Economy and Minister of Foreign Relations, who were dismembered. In 1968 a plebiscite and decree gave him absolute power for life.

In 1968 Nguan personally dismembered the president of the central bank after an argument over the country's debt and inflation, so that the man could "learn how to count".

### Villa years (1969 to 1973)

In 1969 Nguan purged his entire cabinet. Out of fear for his life, he left the capital permanently for a presidential villa in the jungle, taking 500 loyal men and all of the state's foreign currency reserves. The reserves held in the villa later became the seed of Bershad's extraction economy. International cholera vaccines were hoarded in the villa during the epidemic of 1970.

### Figurehead (1973 to 1981)

After the state collapsed in 1973, Nguan remained head of state without power, held hostage in his villa. He died there in 1981 of cardiac failure related to sorophine use. He left no successor and no plan for transition.

## Personal Life

In 1965 Nguan made a secret trip to [[Neoveli]] with an aide to receive psychiatric treatment. His dependency on [[sorophine]] began around this time, possibly from the medication he was prescribed.

## Legacy

[[San'motko]] refounded itself publicly after his death. Susian readings of his rule are covered in [[Bershad–Susia relations]].
