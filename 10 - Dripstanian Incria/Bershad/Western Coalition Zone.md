The **Western Coalition Zone** is an unrecognized polity in western [[Bershad]], governed by [[San'motko]] as its state party. Its coalition comprises the [[Parund people|Parund]], [[Daiko people|Daiko]], [[Mtole people|Mtole]] and [[Kadus people|Kadus]], with the Parund dominant. In the 2080s it is an international pariah, sanctioned by most of the world.

## History

The zone descends from the parallel state San'motko consolidated in northwestern Bershad around 1975. During the [[Bershadi Civil War]], the San'motko-led Western Coalition joined the northern coalition that rejected the first national election around 2000, and was recognized with it by [[Yar-Firol]] and [[Ashgerad]]. By the 2080s it stands alone under sanctions.

## Government

San'motko is the state party. Internal life is organized around predation status as a political hierarchy. "Enemies of the people" are added to the pool of those who may be preyed upon by state decree.

## Economy

Outside the sorophine trade the economy is subsistence. Infrastructure is in decline.

> *Main article: [[Sorophine]]*

The zone's sorophine reaches international markets through smuggling, primarily via [[Veskera]].

## Military

An elite unit enforces non-predation inside the foreign-protection district.

## Foreign Relations

The zone is sanctioned by [[Susia]], Yar-Firol, [[Sekyo]], Veskera and most of the world. Ashgerad declines to recognize it formally. It may keep unrecognized missions in states aligned with Ashgerad.

### Foreign-protection district

The capital contains a walled district for foreigners where the state suspends predation as a matter of policy. It houses embassies, unrecognized missions, foreign journalists on tours, the compounds of non-governmental organizations and aid distribution.

When foreign citizens or aid workers are harmed, the state holds televised internal punishments that restore the district's protection.

## Culture

> *Main article: [[Cannibalism in Bershad]]*

Three of the four coalition peoples practice cannibalism, each in its own mode: Daiko medical and scarcity cannibalism, Mtole gastronomic cannibalism and Parund predation. The Kadus do not practice it but live among those who do. Public ritual practice forms part of state celebration, and broadcast festivals show the leadership taking part.
