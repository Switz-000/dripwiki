**Modern degredo** is the successor-state continuation of the imperial practice of _degredo_, the abandonment of convicts on distant shores. States of the Incrian cluster remove convicts and others across the border into [[Bershad]]. [[Yar-Firol]] runs the largest program. [[Susia]] does not participate.

## Origins

The practice continues the imperial degredo of the [[Dripstanian Empire]].

## Programs

### Yar-Firol

Yar-Firol's program is administered locally by the provincial judiciary and border officials, with no national policy on paper. Convicts are driven to the Bershadi border in vans and told to walk.

### Sekyo

[[Sekyo]] runs a smaller program, primarily returning [[Lawhá]] [[Xaya people|Xaya]] defendants to Xaya regions of Bershad as "ethnic repatriation".

### Veskera

[[Veskera]] does not practice degredo directly. It pays Yar-Firol a fee to handle Veskeran convicts processed under deportation orders to Yar-Firol.

### Susia

Susia does not participate. The Susian prison system absorbs the country's carceral costs.

## Categories

Those removed include stateless persons, Bershadi-descended residents convicted of any offence, mentally ill persons the host system cannot or will not house, political undesirables, and foreigners whose home countries decline repatriation.

## Degredados in Bershad

Some degredados with useful skills, such as accountants, chemists and former soldiers with training in logistics, rise in Bershadi warlord factions.

## Terminology

Diaspora and Modernist critics use the archaic term _degredo_ deliberately. Receiving states use "voluntary return", "third-country resettlement" and "repatriation processing".
