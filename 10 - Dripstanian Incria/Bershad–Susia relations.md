**Relations between [[Susia]] and [[Bershad]]** began with Susian recognition of Bershad at its independence in 1962. Susia did not revisit that recognition through the [[Nguan regime]]. Susian opinion turned toward intervention in the early 1980s, and from around 2000 Susia led a nation-building intervention in Bershad. Since the intervention Susian policy has been sanctions and non-intervention.

## Nguan regime

In 1967 photographs of Bershadi atrocities reached the Susian press through foreign journalists in the Bershadi capital. The Susian establishment received them as proof that the imperial civilizing mission had been necessary. The regime's public fiction of separation from [[San'motko]] made that reading possible.

The intelligence on the Nguan regime and on the [[Buta genocide]] was available to the [[Frumentarze]] from 1967 onward, in the photographs of foreign journalists and in refugee testimony.

Susian establishment historiography reads [[Boe Nguan]] as confirmation of the necessity of the imperial civilizing mission.

## Turn toward intervention

Between around 1980 and 1982 Susian public opinion turned toward intervention. The shift was driven by a figure of the first wave of the [[Bershadi diaspora]] publishing in Susian, who confronted the readers of the imperial-apologist framing with the fact that the victims were the empire's own: Susian-educated, Susian-named, Susian-Armotist families.

## Intervention

> *Main article: [[Bershadi Civil War#Susian intervention]]*

From around 2000 Susia led a nation-building effort in Bershad in collaboration with its [[ODDN]] allies in Incria: [[Veskera]], [[Yar-Firol]] and [[Sekyo]]. It was framed as the rescue of the empire's own people.

## Since the intervention

Settled Susian doctrine holds that no Susian government puts troops on the ground of the Bershadi partition. Policy consists of sanctions and non-intervention. Susia has recognized the southern Bershadi government continuously since the [[Bershadi Conference]].

Sorophine extracted from Bershad reaches the Susian pharmaceutical market through the [[Yarnojte]] chains.
