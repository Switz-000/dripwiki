The **General Government of Incria** (GGI) was the colonial administration of the [[Dripstanian Empire]]'s mainland possessions in [[Incria]]. It was established under [[Jartes II]] and reformed in 1954 into the [[United Republics of East Incria]]. Its seat and civil service passed to [[Yar-Firol]].

## History

[[Jartes II]] reversed [[Empress Veronique|Veronique]]'s policy of non-interference in Incria. The explorer [[Agamilos Račev]] and his teams signed unequal treaties with Incrian peoples that in practice made them subjects of the emperor and authorized the collection of tribute. Jartes II then established the Public Order Force of the General Government of Incria.

In 1950 the General Government conducted the last census of [[Bershad]], and [[Filevir Gatojete]] held a referendum in each colony on whether to remain part of the empire. In 1954 the General Government was reformed into the United Republics of East Incria.

## Structure

The **Public Order Force** combined the functions of army, police and resource extraction. It operated through quotas imposed on native peoples, enforced with torture, kidnapping, sexual violence and murder.

## Legacy

Yar-Firol inherited the General Government's capital, civil service and Armotist church infrastructure.
