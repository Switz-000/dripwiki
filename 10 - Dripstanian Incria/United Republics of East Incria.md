The **United Republics of East Incria** were the successor state to the [[General Government of Incria]], from 1954 to 1962. The franchise was restricted to property owners. [[Umbata Zambar]] led the republic from 1955 until a coup in 1962 dissolved it and [[Bershad]] became independent.

## History

The General Government was reformed into the United Republics in 1954. Zambar promoted a one-party system and founded the [[United Independence Party]]. He was deposed in a coup in 1962, the republic was dissolved, and Bershad became its own country.

## Government

The constitution restricted the vote to property owners, excluding most rural native peoples from government.

The Armotist, wealthier and more integrated peoples supported the government: the [[Sanye people|Sanye]], [[Baya people|Baya]], [[Haiel people|Haiel]], [[Yokno people|Yokno]], [[Bokta people|Bokta]] and Dripstanians. The more isolated, pagan and poorer peoples opposed it: the [[Parund people|Parund]], [[Daiko people|Daiko]], [[Kadus people|Kadus]], [[Mtole people|Mtole]], [[Xaya people|Xaya]] and [[Buta people|Buta]].
