**Sorophine** is a pharmaceutical drug produced in [[Incria]] and sold in [[Susia]] through the pharmaceutical chains of the [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]]. Its trade is the economic core of the Incrian cluster. Illicit production in [[Bershad]] is laundered into the official economies of [[Yar-Firol]] and [[Sekyo]] and refined in [[Veskera]], and it funded the [[Bershadi Civil War]].

## Production

Legitimate sorophine is sourced from the interior of Yar-Firol, from [[Tehtíe]] and from [[Ditania]]. Illicit production comes from the zones of Bershad.

## Trade

### Legitimate pipeline

A legitimate pharmaceutical pipeline runs from Yar-Firol through Veskera to Susia and routes around the [[Western Coalition Zone]]. Susian Yarnojte pharmaceutical chains are the downstream buyers.

### Laundering of Bershadi extraction

Sorophine extracted from the eastern and southern zones of Bershad is laundered into official prosperity through state-owned agricultural and chemical boards in Yar-Firol and Sekyo. Veskera refines and ships what the two states deliver. Yar-Firol runs its channel through Buta kinship across the border, brokered by the [[Yar-Firolian Buta survivor caucus]]; Sekyo runs its channel through Haiel and Xaya kinship.

### Smuggling from the Western Coalition Zone

Sorophine from the Western Coalition Zone reaches international markets through smuggling, primarily via Veskera. The Veskeran smuggling registry is the link between the zone and the international market, inheriting the character of the empire's ports that "don't keep records they don't have to keep". Adulterated sorophine enters the legitimate pipeline under false provenance, and buyers do not test it.

### History

During the [[Nguan regime]], Bershadi sorophine moved through Yar-Firolian ports because no other functional route existed. The extraction architecture of the following decades was built in these years.

## Use

[[Boe Nguan]] developed a dependency on sorophine from 1965, possibly from medication prescribed during psychiatric treatment in [[Neoveli]]. Militias in the Bershadi Civil War dosed child soldiers with sorophine, particularly in the Western Coalition.

In Bershad sorophine is cheap; buying it there is dangerous, and carrying it out is illegal.
