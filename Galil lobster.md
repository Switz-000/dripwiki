The **Duolij Lobster** is a species of slipper lobster found in the [[Sea of Galil|Galil coast]], chiefly from [[Postia]], [[Vitrika]] and [[Orlítia]]. It is also known as **Galil Lobster**, **Susian Lobster** or **flathead lobster**. Despite their name, they are not true lobsters.
