The **Lasman** is the thrid longest river in [[Dripstan]]. It flows from the [[Confian Highlands]] east into [[Susia]], being the boundary for the [[New Celiolaj]] - [[Misocévia]] border, then south through [[Misocévia]] and east again into the city of [[Jaffnidia]] in [[Dripia]], where it meets the [[Sea of Galil]].
