**Port Tekur**, often referred to by its native name **Tekuri Ljas** (Racpalian Alphabet: Текури Љас) is a major city in [[Pierej]]. It was the signing place of the [[Tekuri Ljas Accord]], which authorized the multinational intervention in [[Ditania]].
