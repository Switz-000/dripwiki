The Dripstanian Empire was the polity that dominated the majority of the territory of the [[Dripstanian Continent]] at its peak.


It was founded in 1674, when [[Saint Emperor Mantichev|Mantichev]] united the Armotist coastal kingdoms at the [[Convention of Forchev]]. After the death of [[Emperor Sallemaj]] in 1950, the [[Treaty of Sužielaj]] ended the [[War of Confian Secession]] and split the empire into two autonomous provinces. The empire formally ended in 1954 with the [[Tahuni Accords]], which divided it into several countries, [[Susia]] and [[Confia]] among them.
