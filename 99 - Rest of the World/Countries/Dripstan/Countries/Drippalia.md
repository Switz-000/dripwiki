**Drippalia**, officially the **Republic of Drippalia**, is a presidential republic on the [[Dripstan]] continent, bordered by [[Susia]] to the west, [[Kapiogg]] to the east, [[Celiolaj]] to the north and [[Confia]] to the south. [[Tekur|Tekurs]] are the majority of its population. It was annexed by Confia and Kapiogg in 1960, restored after the [[Continental War]], and joined the [[ODDN]] in 1981.

## History

In the [[Dripstanian Civil War]] (1815 to 1823) Drippalia and its vassals declared for [[Jartes I|Jartes]] against [[Empress Prazde|Prazde]]. In 1948 the [[Confederation of Drippalia]] fought Kapiogg in the [[First War of the Forest]].

In 1958 Drippalia invaded Kapiogg to take the provinces of [[Oesburg]] and [[Jaeregg]], and moved against the Confian region of [[Legrinpija]]. It lost the [[Second War of the Forest]], and in 1960 its territory was annexed and split between Confia and Kapiogg. Drippalian civilians were among the casualties of the Susian [[Sweet Apple Incidents|Eplevakt campaign]] of 1976 and 1977. Drippalia was restored after the Continental War and joined the ODDN in 1981. It is also a member of the [[Transcontinental Trade and Developmental Organization]].

## Politics

Isolationism is more active in Drippalian politics than in Susian.
