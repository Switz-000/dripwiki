The **ODDN** (Organization of the Democratic Dripstanian Nations) is an alliance of democratic states on the [[Dripstan]] continent. [[Susia]], [[Kapiogg]] and [[Astofayan]] founded it in 1961 as a mutual defense and anti-syndicalist alliance during the [[Continental Divide]]. It has since been reoriented toward the containment of [[Ashgerad]].

## History

> *Main article: [[Continental Divide]]*

The ODDN was the institutional vehicle of the Susian bloc against [[Confia]] and the [[Pact of Imgospalje]], coordinating covert operations, economic pressure and support for proxies across the continent. Susia made Kapiogg's entry conditional on its gradual transition from absolute monarchy to parliamentary government. [[Pierej]] joined in 1970 after a Susian-sponsored coup. Kapiogg held the northern front for the alliance in the [[Continental War]] (1975 to 1977).

After the war the alliance became a broader democratic security structure. [[Drippalia]], restored after the war, joined in 1981, [[Kamino]] in 1986 and Confia, the former adversary, in 1993. From around 2000 Susia led an intervention in [[Bershad]] with ODDN states in [[Incria]]: the member [[Veskera]] and the regional ODDN partners [[Yar-Firol]] and [[Sekyo]].
