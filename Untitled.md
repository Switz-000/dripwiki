**Soites Group** (commonly known as **Soites**) is a [[Susia|Susian]] multinational conglomerate and one of the nation's six [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]], operating in retail, logistics, financial services, entertainment, data analytics, and advertising. Founded in 1969 as a discount retail chain by [[Suizo Soites]] (1911–1995), Susia's first president, it is headquartered at the [[Soites Federal Free Trade Zone]] in [[Postia]] and employs approximately 2.4 million people. Its market capitalization is D$3.8 trillion, and its corporate motto is "Your life, Your way."

## History

### Founding and early years (1969–1981)

Soites was founded in 1969 by Suizo Soites, seven years after the end of his second presidential term, as a discount retail chain in [[Neoveli]], [[Dripia]]. The early stores operated on high volume, low margins, and efficient distribution. Suizo retired from active management in 1981 and was succeeded by [[Chedji Soites|Chedji Jaroch Soites]], a business associate he had legally adopted as a brother. Leadership of the company has remained within the Soites family since its founding.

### Expansion and diversification (1981–2038)

Under [[Chedji Soites]], the company expanded from discount retail into logistics, supply chain management, and financial services, launching the private label brand [[Sisra]] in 1983 and combining shopping, entertainment, and dining under unified management. During this period Soites concluded that customer purchasing data held more value than product margins, and built the data analytics operation that became central to its business model. The company acquired the messaging platform [[Lizne]] in 2022 as part of a diversification into data and entertainment.

In 2032 the company launched the [[Soites Experimenter Program]], which paid participants to test products and build social media presences while serving as behavioral and psychological research for the company's predictive algorithms.

> *Main article: [[Soites Experimenter Program]]*

From 2034 to 2048 Soites partnered with [[Troli Ustaras]], combining its behavioral research with TU's hardware and AI development. The partnership produced early [[Cognitive enhancement|cognitive enhancement]] prototypes tested on Experimenter Program participants and contributed to the [[Federal Cognitive Enhancement Program]].

> *Main article: [[Cognitive enhancement]]*

### Yarnojte status (2038–2050)

[[The Great Transition|The Great Transition of 2038]] created the [[Free Economic Zone]] system. Soites was granted [[Yarnojte]] status in 2050 and established the [[Soites Federal Free Trade Zone]] (FFTZ) in [[Postia]], relocating its headquarters there.

### Modern era (2050–present)

> *Main article: [[Kolkov line]]*

Soites publicly supported the construction of the [[Kolkov line]] and contributed to its funding, building and retaining ownership of the line's FFTZ station. The line's opening in 2061 placed the FFTZ within commuting range of [[Duolij]] and [[Neoveli]], and FFTZ business tripled in the years that followed. Rides on the line are free for [[SoitesCard]] holders under an agreement between Soites and the line's operator.

By 2070, approximately 58.2 million people visited the FFTZ annually and 37% of Susian adults carried a SoitesCard. The company has faced continuing scrutiny over its data collection practices, its financial services operations, and the Experimenter Program.

## Operations

Soites' retail division operates the [[Soitesmart]] store network and the Sisra private label, alongside the Soitesexpress delivery network. Its financial services division issues the [[SoitesCard]] and provides consumer credit through Soites Credit and banking through Soites Bank. The company produces film and television content through its entertainment division and operates [[Lizne]]. Its data analytics and advertising division processes customer data collected across all of these operations into predictive consumer models.

### Leadership

The company has had five chief executives, all members of the Soites family: [[Suizo Soites]] (1969–1981), [[Chedji Soites]] (1981–2031), [[Trevda Soites]] (2031–2052), [[Yepodij Soites]] (2052–2068), and [[Natesse Soites]] (2068–present).

## Free Economic Zones

> *Main article: [[Soites Federal Free Trade Zone]]*

Soites operates one FEZ, the Soites Federal Free Trade Zone in Postia, which serves as its headquarters and flagship retail destination. The zone houses approximately 102,000 permanent and 32,000 temporary residents and functions as a national shopping destination, drawing visitors from across Susia.

## Corporate Culture

Soites positions itself against the paternalism of other Yarnojtes under the motto "Your life, Your way," presenting its services as expansions of consumer choice rather than guidance. Company marketing states: "In a world where everyone tells you how to be, think, and do, we at Soites believe that you're foremost, an adult. We give you the options, you decide." In contrast to the exclusivity cultivated by [[Troli Ustaras]] and [[Van Ritter]], Soites emphasizes mass accessibility, a positioning reflected in the open class mixing of the FFTZ's central cafeteria.

All Soites retail locations are scented with the company's signature tangerine fragrance.

Since 1979, Soitesmart has marked each new store opening in a small city with a large public event featuring live bands and attractions, drawing visitors in the tens of thousands. The site where [[Armadesh Versij]] wrote [[Architecture of Freedom]] is now a Soitesmart; a small sign at the location commemorates him.

## Notable Controversies

Soites' data collection infrastructure tracks customer behavior across its operations. SoitesCard transactions are recorded at all participating merchants, not only Soites properties, and in-store equipment including instrumented shopping carts records customer movement and product handling. The resulting database links purchase history with online behavior under the company's prediction systems. The company has defended these practices under its disclosure policies, summarized in the campaign slogan "You decide what we know."

The [[Soites Experimenter Program]] has drawn criticism over informed consent, the psychological condition of long-term participants, and the company's selection criteria.
