**Ti-ka** is a susian newspaper headquarted in [[Duolij]], [[Postia]]. The name translates to "This, and I was there!"

## History

### Ant Wars

> *Main article: [[Nevirs]]*

During the [[Ant wars]], *Ti-ka!* printed letters from the [[Nevirs]] in full on receipt, as did other Postian papers. On 19/05/1956 its morning edition carried a Nevir letter announcing the death of governor [[Filevir Matri]], mailed ahead of a [[Matri assassination attempt (1956)|car bomb]] that had failed to detonate under his official car. After the attempt, the editors of Duolij's newspapers agreed to pass every Nevir letter to the police before printing it.
