The **Nevirs** (Susian: *Nevir*, "no man"), rendered in English as the **No Men**, were an anarchist current active in [[Susia]] during the [[Ant wars]]. They were known through threat letters and claims of responsibility issued under a single pseudonym, Nevir, which belonged to no real person. Attacks attributed to them include the [[Neoveli Stock Exchange Bombing]] of 30/05/1955, the assassination of Senator [[Halios Dravda]] in 1955, and the [[Matri assassination attempt (1958)|attempt on governor Filevir Matri]] of 14/11/1958.

## Name and letters

*Nevir* is formed from the Susian negative prefix *ne-* and *vir*, "man". Every letter was signed with the name in the singular, and any person who wrote one used it. The plural *Nevirs* was applied by the press and by the state, and no surviving letter uses it.

The letters carried the motto *Kroch, dulij. Nekroch, drivire.* ("Power, servant. No-power, freedom."). The Postian press rendered it as "Order enslaves, chaos liberates", translating *nekroch* as chaos.

Postian newspapers, among them [[Ti-ka!]], printed the letters in full until 1956, when the editors of [[Duolij]] agreed to pass every Nevir letter to the police before publication.

> *Main article: [[Matri assassination attempt (1956)]]*

## Ideology

The Nevirs were anarchists who rejected the state and any organized power, including the syndicates. They took much of their language from the left and from [[Syndicalism|syndicalism]], but their practice had little in common with the syndicalism of the [[Paulowić regime]] in [[Confia]]. The campaign sought public attention, and its attackers were prepared to die in the attempt.

The label "anarcho-syndicalist" was applied to them from outside, by [[Filevir Matri|Matri's]] government and the press of the period. It joined the anarchists to the syndicalist movement in official usage.

## Organization

The Nevirs had no membership, leadership or command structure. Most of the attacks attributed to them were carried out by individuals acting alone, connected to one another only by the pseudonym and the motto.

[[Filevir Matri|Matri's]] state government treated them as a network of cells and pursued those cells across [[Postia]] throughout his governorship. The network described in official records was in large part the product of the investigations themselves.

## State response

> *Main article: [[Ant wars]]*

The Neoveli bombing led to the [[Dravda Act]] of 1956, which founded the federal security agency [[Frumentarze]]. In [[Postia]], the Nevirs supplied the justification for [[Filevir Matri|Matri's]] crackdown, which reached unions, syndicalist organizations, the eossajs and the [[Movement of New Susians]].

## Operation Nevirkroch

Letters signed Nevir continued to appear occasionally after the Ant Wars ended in 1959. In 1967, [[Filevir Matri]], then [[Secretary of Justice]], launched Operation Nevirkroch through the federal security agency created by the Dravda Act. The operation made mass arrests in several states and was announced as the dismantling of the Nevir network.

No Nevir letter was published after the operation. Since 1956 the letters had passed to the police before reaching the press. Whether any were written after 1967 is not recorded.
