The **Democratic Laborist Party** is a labor party in [[Postia]], affiliated with the [[Susian Liberal Party]] federation as its labor wing in the state. During the [[Ant wars]] its syndicalist eossaj fought the [[Knights of the Republic]], the paramilitary of the [[Pačese ro Virspruje]], the federation's other Postian affiliate.

## Federation

> *Main article: [[Susian Liberal Party]]*

The Liberal federation was organized in three wings: a Versijian wing, represented in Postia by the [[Pačese ro Virspruje]]; a labor wing, represented by the Democratic Laborists; and a centrist pragmatic wing associated with [[Suizo Soites]]. The Laborists and the PrV shared the federation's federal program while opposing each other in Postia.

## Eossaj

> *Main article: [[Eossaj]]*

The party maintained a syndicalist eossaj that was more radical than the party itself. It was the principal opponent of the [[Knights of the Republic]] in the street fighting of the Ant Wars, and it was dissolved after the [[Supreme Court of Susia|Supreme Court]] ruling against eossajs and the crackdown that followed.
