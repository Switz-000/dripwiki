The **Susian Liberal Party** (PNS, Susian: *Pačese Niterali Sužietich*) was a [[Party Federation|party federation]] in [[Susia]], founded in 1954 by [[Suizo Soites]], [[Ergagério Sienes]] and [[Filňar Čantij]]. With the [[Susian Democratic Union]] it was one of the two federations that dominated the republican era. It held the presidency for five terms and was dissolved in 2038 at [[The Great Transition|the Great Transition]].

## Origins

The party developed out of the Liberty and Fatherland Movement, founded in 1931 by [[Suizo Soites]] and [[Filňar Čantij]] while both were students at the [[University of New Kentu]]. The movement existed to propagate constitutionalism and liberalism, and gained support through the political and social instability of the late imperial period, which the [[War of Confian Secession]] intensified. [[Ergagério Sienes]] is also recorded as a member.

The movement's positions circulated beyond Susia through graduates of the university. [[Senjap Kunsekowić]], a [[Confia|Confian]] exile who took a degree at New Kentu in 1940, adopted a comparable position on assuming the leadership of the [[Confian Liberal Party]] in 1942.

## Founding

The party was founded in 1954, the year the [[Dripstanian Empire]] was dissolved under the [[Tahuni Accords]] and the [[Susian Constitution|constitution]] was ratified. Soites had led negotiations between the former imperial provinces through the [[National Agreement]] as a delegate to the constitutional convention, and was elected the republic's first president in the same year. The [[Susian Democratic Union]], the [[Supreme Court of Susia]] and several state parties were established in 1954 as well.

## Structure

> *Main article: [[Party Federation]]*

The party was a federation of affiliated state parties coordinating on federal policy and running under a common banner for the presidency and for seats in the [[Susian Senate]]. Its recorded affiliate is the [[Pačese ro Virspruje]] in [[Postia]], founded in 1954, which maintained the newspaper [[The Versijian]], the youth organization [[Young Versijians]], and the paramilitary wing [[Knights of the Republic]]. The federation maintained no equivalent bodies of its own.

## Republican era

### Ant Wars

> *Main article: [[Ant Wars]]*

The [[Knights of the Republic]] were dissolved in 1959 following the [[Supreme Court of Susia|Supreme Court]] ruling that declared eossajs unconstitutional and the crackdown conducted in [[Postia]] under governor [[Filevir Matri]]. The [[Pačese ro Virspruje]] contested the ruling and lost its principal organizational instrument.

### Continental Divide

> *Main article: [[Continental Divide]]*

[[Ergagério Sienes]] held the presidency from 1970 to 1974 and pursued the [[Sienes Doctrine|détente policy]] toward [[Confia]]. The entry of [[Sutsa-Fuhu]] into the syndicalist bloc in 1973 ended the policy's remaining credibility within significant parts of the party.

### Opposition

Following the election of [[Čalsor Boňár]] in 1974, the party was the principal source of constitutional objection to the expansion of federal authority undertaken during his presidency. Criticism of the retention of emergency measures and of the blurring of civil and military authority was associated with [[Ergagério Sienes]]. Opposition to the [[National Care Act of 1981]] was concentrated in the party and among figures associated with [[Filňar Čantij]], on the argument that the constitution assigns the federal government no authority over healthcare.

### Čantij presidencies

[[Filňar Čantij]] held the presidency from 1986 to 1994. The party bans and labor restrictions of the [[MAGEN act]] were unwound in stages under his presidency and that of [[Filevir Matri]].

## Dissolution

The federation was dissolved in 2038 at [[The Great Transition|the Great Transition]], with most of its constituent state parties joining the [[Coalition of State's Rights]] or the [[Democratic-Federalist Party]].
