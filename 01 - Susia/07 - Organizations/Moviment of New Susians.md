The **Movement of New Susians** was a self-defense organization formed by [[Confia|Confian]] refugees in [[New Duloc]], [[Postia]], in the early years of the republic. Organized around neighborhood militias, it operated outside partisan politics and functioned primarily to protect immigrant enclaves from street violence during the [[Ant Wars]] (1954–1959).

## Origins

The movement emerged among Confian refugees who had settled in New Duloc's port and industrial neighborhoods following the [[Confian anarchy]]. Many members were veterans of the [[Confian secession war]], arriving in Susia with military experience and without citizenship. Their presence in Susia followed a unanimous [[Susian Senate]] bill authorizing the reception of Confian refugees, passed in the early republic period.

Concentrated in low-income areas alongside the city's dockworkers and factory workforce, the community faced economic competition with native laborers and exposure to the street violence of Postia's eossaj period. By 1956 these conditions had produced a structured organization from the informal alert networks and mutual aid arrangements of the preceding two years.

## Organization and methods

The movement operated on a castle doctrine principle: defense of homes, stores, and neighborhood perimeters, without pursuit or aggression beyond those boundaries. It did not affiliate with any political party or federation, and its leadership deliberately avoided public political statements. Members did not participate in strike actions or labor organizing, a choice that reflected both the community's anti-syndicalist orientation and a strategic effort to distance themselves from the associations that the native working class and state authorities attached to Confian immigrants.

The movement maintained no formal relationship with the [[Pačese ro Virspruje|PrV]] or its paramilitary wing, the [[Knights of the Republic]], though both organizations were active in the same city during the same period. The PrV leadership regarded the movement with distant respect. The movement regarded Knight retaliations as escalatory and strategically counterproductive.

## Ant Wars and the New Duloc riot

> *Main article: [[Ant Wars]]*

During the [[New Duloc riot (1958)]], the movement's military discipline and the combat experience of its membership produced outcomes distinct from the rest of the city. While surrounding neighborhoods suffered sustained looting and arson over several days, the movement's defended blocks held. Casualties among the Confian community were significantly lower than in comparable areas. The contrast was visible and documented when the [[Postian National Guard]] arrived to restore order.

The performance did not translate into political protection. [[Filevir Matri|Matri's]] subsequent crackdown under the state of emergency swept the movement into its surveillance and arrest operations alongside syndicalist organizations, despite its explicitly non-political character and its record during the riot.

## Federal Loyalty Act and aftermath

The movement's position deteriorated sharply after 1961, when the [[Federal Loyalty Act]] reframed Confian immigrants as a potential security risk following evidence of [[Lorelaj Paulowić|Paulowić's]] infiltration of Susian institutions. An organized, armed network of Confian nationals in a port city was difficult to treat as politically neutral once the geopolitical context shifted. Organizations that had operated legally and defensibly during the Ant Wars were subjected to ongoing surveillance regardless of their record.
