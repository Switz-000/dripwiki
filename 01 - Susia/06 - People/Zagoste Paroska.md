**Zagoste Paroska** (1796–1844) was a [[Dripstanian Empire|Dripstanian]] naval officer who rose to admiral in the imperial navy and then sat on the general government of the [[Federated Provinces of Galil]] during the [[Liberal Revolts]]. He was publicly executed alongside [[Armadesh Versij]] in 1844 and is remembered in [[Susia]] as a martyr of liberty.

## Early Life

Paroska was born in 1796 in [[Duolij]], in the imperial territory that later became the state of [[Postia]]. He came from a prestigious family. He trained at the [[Susian Naval Academy]].

## Liberal Revolts

> *Main article: [[Liberal Revolts]]*

### The general government

In 1840 a general assembly in [[Neoveli]] proclaimed the Federated Provinces of Galil, joined by Duolij, [[New Kentu]], and [[Niqueparj]]. Paroska sided with the republic and sat on its general government alongside Versij and General [[Kaichet Satratonie]]. The body was constituted as three equals, and its authority consolidated around Versij as the war continued.

### Rivalry with Satratonie

Paroska and Satratonie were rivals throughout the revolt. Paroska had conducted an affair with the [[Countess of Jafnidia]], a woman Satratonie wished to marry, and Satratonie repeatedly sought to duel him over it.

The challenges went unanswered. Formal dueling in this period was a western institution conducted between social equals, and Satratonie, who had risen through the imperial officer corps by merit rather than birth, was not accepted as one by the order Paroska belonged to.

Satratonie concluded that wealth, status, and the death of his rival together would secure the marriage. On that expectation he accepted [[Jartes II]]'s offer of land, money, and a royal pardon in exchange for changing sides.

### Defeat and execution

Satratonie defected. The fort at [[Jaffnidia]] fell, the republic was split in two, and imperial troops entered Neoveli in 1844. Paroska, Versij, and the rest of the republican leadership were publicly executed. Paroska was convicted on 22 counts of treason, having pleaded not guilty, and was hanged.

## Legacy

Paroska and Versij are remembered in Susia as the martyrs of the Liberal Revolts, and Jartes II as the emperor who killed them. Versij's legacy is doctrinal and is carried by [[Versij Day]], [[Versijian Square]], and the philosophical tradition that bears his name. Paroska's rests on the revolt itself and on the personal quarrel that ended it. [[Jaffnidia]], where the defection began, holds a museum dedicated to the revolts.
