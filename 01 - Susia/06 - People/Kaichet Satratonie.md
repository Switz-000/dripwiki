**Kaichet Satratonie** (1809–1872) was a [[Dripstanian Empire|Dripstanian]] general who sat on the general government of the [[Federated Provinces of Galil]] during the [[Liberal Revolts]] and defected to [[Jartes II]] in 1844 in exchange for land, money, and a royal pardon. The defection split the republic in two and was followed within the year by its collapse and by the public execution of [[Armadesh Versij]] and [[Zagoste Paroska]]. His name entered the Susian language as the term for betraying one's principles for personal gain, a usage that carries no rehabilitative reading.

## Early Life

Satratonie was born in 1809 in [[Lore-Kurji]], in [[Misocévia]], and trained at the [[Military Academy of Niqueparj]]. He rose through the imperial officer corps by merit rather than by birth, reaching the rank of general.

Advancement did not carry standing with it. The professionalized officer corps drew its recruits from across the imperial regions and was the principal setting in which the eastern frontier tradition Satratonie came from met the formal conventions of the western settled gentry. Satratonie sought a place in the western aristocratic order and was not accepted into it as an equal.

## Liberal Revolts

> *Main article: [[Liberal Revolts]]*

### The general government

In 1840 a general assembly in [[Neoveli]] proclaimed the Federated Provinces of Galil, joined by [[Duolij]], [[New Kentu]], and [[Niqueparj]]. Satratonie sided with the republic and sat on its general government alongside Versij and Admiral Paroska. The body was constituted as three equals, and its authority consolidated around Versij as the war continued. The war itself was fought as guerrilla warfare across four years, without a decisive engagement.

### The rivalry with Paroska

Satratonie and Paroska were rivals throughout the revolt. Satratonie wished to marry the [[Countess of Jafnidia]], whom he had first met at [[Jaffnidia]] and who had conducted an affair with Paroska. He sought repeatedly to duel Paroska over it and was never answered.

The refusals followed from the form of the institution rather than from the quarrel. Formal dueling in this period was a western practice conducted through fixed procedure between social equals, and Satratonie, whose advancement had come by merit, was not held to be one. The episode is treated in [[Cericeiro honor code]] as the case that distinguishes the western formal duel from the eastern frontier code.

### The defection

Unable to win the war militarily, Jartes II opened a secret contact with Satratonie and offered him land, money, and a royal pardon in exchange for changing sides. Satratonie had concluded that wealth, status, and the death of his rival together would secure the marriage, and he accepted on that expectation.

The defection began at Jaffnidia, the border town at the mouth of the [[Lasman river]] whose fort controlled the link between the republic's two halves. The fort fell, the republic was split, and imperial troops entered Neoveli in 1844. Versij, Paroska, and the remainder of the republican leadership were publicly executed.

## Later life

Satratonie died in 1872. Neither the place nor the circumstances are recorded.

## Legacy

The idiom "pulling a Satratonie" means betraying one's principles for money or status. It is used damningly and has no rehabilitative reading in common circulation. The idiom preserves the public account of the defection, the bribe and the change of sides, and omits the personal motive that the historical record carries.

[[Cericeiro honor code]] reads his career as a man who satisfied neither of the two codes available to him. By the standards of the frontier code he committed its central breach, using capacity for personal gain and abandoning those alongside whom he was obligated to stand. By the standards of the western tradition he lacked the breeding the formal duel presupposed. The western refusal to accept him rested on a perceived absence of honor rather than on his origin.

At [[Jaffnidia]], the wall where he met the countess is covered in graffiti curses often enough that the city clears it roughly weekly. The town holds a large museum dedicated to the revolts.

The line "A nation of citizen soldiers is mightier than any standing army" originates with Satratonie and circulates widely in Susia under Versij's name. The misattribution is generally treated as ironic or embarrassing once corrected.
