**Yaneoli** (1752–1815) was the fourth ruler of the [[Dripstanian Empire]], reigning from 1787 to 1815. She intervened in the [[Seventh Bakuro-Zaphonian War]] and brought [[Zaphonia]] into the empire as a vassal kingdom, and created the vassal [[Kingdom of Tekuria]] under her brother Colin. Her death without a settled succession produced the [[Dripstanian Civil War]] between her two children.

## Early Life

Yaneoli was born in 1752, the eldest child of [[Empress Veronique]] and [[Faranes the Feared]]. She married [[Astere]] in 1768.

## Accession

Yaneoli was the clear heir at her mother's death and was crowned in 1787. Her accession was received more favourably by the conservative sectors of Dripstanian society than Veronique's had been, particularly among the landowners of [[Troli]], who invited her to attend the Zaphonoterfaj at Troli that year as guest of honour.

## Reign

### Seventh Bakuro-Zaphonian War

> *Main article: [[Seventh Bakuro-Zaphonian War]]*

In 1790 the Bakuro under King [[Bharjette II]] invaded the [[Zaphonia|Empire of Zaphonia]]. The Zaphonian emperor [[Mawesi Khoseri]] travelled to [[Sužielaj]] to request Dripstanian military assistance. Yaneoli offered intervention on the condition of vassalage, under which Dripstan would become suzerain and Zaphonia would be reorganised as the Kingdom of Zaphonia.

Mawesi refused the terms at first and was persuaded by Yaneoli, who is recorded as answering: "Мираете у насе и шерожаете се или мираете у вахурьах и всепередно выйте раби", rendered as "Die in us and be reborn, or die in the Bakuro and be enslaved forever". The agreement was sealed by a marriage between the heirs of the two houses, Yaneoli's daughter [[Empress Prazde|Prazde]] and Mawesi's son [[Lekhertos]].

The Dripstanian army was technologically and numerically superior to the Bakuro forces and recovered the occupied Zaphonian territory within six months. A statue of Yaneoli 43 meters tall was raised at [[Huttivia]] after the victory, and Prazde, as princess consort of Zaphonia, was granted the title Protector of Huttivia.

The Zaphonian expression *Pekherit se te Prazde*, "to marry Prazde", remains in use for an agreement that is unfavourable in the long term but must be accepted to escape something worse.

### The Tragedy of Takhesire

Members of the Zaphonian court conspired to assassinate Prazde after the vassalage was established. In 1793, while Prazde was away on a two-week journey the conspirators were unaware of, they poisoned food they believed had been prepared for her. The dish was eaten instead by Lekhertos and his mother [[Tedeli Khoseri]]. Both fell ill and died three days later.

Mawesi took his own life a week afterwards. The sequence is known as the Tragedy of Takhesire, after the castle where it occurred.

Prazde returned to find herself next in the Zaphonian succession and was crowned queen of Zaphonia the same month. The three court members party to the conspiracy resigned their positions, stating that they could not serve under a ruler without Zaphonian blood.

### The Kingdom of Tekuria

In 1794 Yaneoli crowned her brother [[Colin, King of the Tekurs|Colin]] as King of the Tekurs, making him ruler of the western [[Tekur]] tribes held in vassalage to Dripstan. The two tribes, [[Lijssick]] and [[Baabren]], were united as the [[Kingdom of Tekuria]], which remained a Dripstanian vassal.

## Personal Life

Yaneoli married Astere in 1768. They had two children, Prazde and [[Jartes I|Jartes]].

Yaneoli died of tuberculosis in 1815, at the age of 63.

## Legacy

Yaneoli left two heirs and no settled succession. Prazde was the heir to the throne at Neoveli and was crowned in 1815, holding the Dripstanian and Zaphonian crowns at once. Jartes contested the succession, and the [[Dripstanian Civil War]] that followed ran for eight years and ended in his victory.

The vassal arrangements she created outlasted her. Zaphonia remained under Dripstanian suzerainty, and the Kingdom of Tekuria survived until the reign of Jartes I, who deported the Tekur population it had been created to govern.
