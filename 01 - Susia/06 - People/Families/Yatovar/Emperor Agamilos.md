**Emperor Agamilos** (1657–1738), also called Agamilos of Frajes, was the second emperor of the [[Dripstanian Empire]], reigning from 1686 to 1738. He succeeded his elder brother [[Saint Emperor Mantichev]] under the imperial succession law. His 52-year reign was the longest of the early empire, and its close produced the succession crisis that placed his daughter [[Empress Veronique]] on the throne.

## Accession

Mantichev was assassinated in 1686 without children. The succession law passed the crown to his brother Agamilos, who was crowned the same year. The transfer established the precedent that the imperial title descended within the house of Frajes rather than reverting to election among the captaincies, as the [[Convention of Forchev]] might otherwise have implied.

## Reign

> %% TODO: the substance of Agamilos's 52-year reign is not established. Only the succession at its start, the standardisation of the treye in 1732, and the crisis at its end are recorded. %%

In 1732 the [[Units of measurement|treye]], an imperial unit of length inherited from [[Gaiyan]], was standardised at the length of Agamilos's thumb, fixed at 2.37 centimetres. The unit remains in use and operates on an inverse scale.

## Family

Agamilos had three children: [[Prince Apreže|Apreže]], [[Empress Veronique|Veronique]], and [[Infante Babal|Babal]]. Apreže, the eldest, was the expected heir throughout his father's later years.

## Succession crisis

> *Main article: [[Empress Veronique]]*

Apreže died in a jousting accident at the age of 25, days before his father's own death in 1738. The imperial succession law was ambiguous on whether a woman could take the throne, and the [[Council of Governors]] convened to determine the validity of Veronique's claim.

A regency governed the empire for two years while the question was argued. In 1740 the Council permitted Veronique's accession. The dispute did not end there: her coronation was immediately contested by the [[Babalist Revolt|Babalists]], who held that the crown belonged to her younger brother Babal.
