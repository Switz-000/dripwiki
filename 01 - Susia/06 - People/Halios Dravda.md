**Halios Dravda** (died 1955) was a [[Susia|Susian]] politician of the [[Pačese ro Virspruje]] who served as a senator for [[Postia]] from 1954 until his death. After the [[Neoveli Stock Exchange Bombing]] he drafted a bill of federal intervention in Postia. He was assassinated by a Nevir in 1955, and the bill passed the following year as the [[Dravda Act]].

## Senate career

Dravda was elected to the [[Susian Senate]] for [[Postia]] in the first Senate election of 1954, on the list of the [[Pačese ro Virspruje]], the Versijian wing of the [[Susian Liberal Party]] in the state.

> *Main article: [[Dravda Act]]*

Following the [[Neoveli Stock Exchange Bombing]] of 30/05/1955, Dravda drafted a bill of federal intervention in Postia, whose state government under [[Filevir Matri]] was conducting its own response to the violence of the [[Ant wars]]. The bill provided, among other measures, for a federal commissioner to take command of the internal security of a state in crisis.

## Assassination

Dravda was killed by a Nevir in 1955, before the bill came to a vote.

## Legacy

The bill passed in 1956 as the [[Dravda Act]]. The commissioner provision was removed in the Senate before passage. Dravda's Senate seat passed to the next candidate on the Pačese ro Virspruje list.
