**Urgiri Tečlan** (1927–1958) was a Susian anarchist affiliated with the [[Minutemen]] who attempted to assassinate [[Filevir Matri]], then governor of [[Postia]], during a public event in [[Duolij]] on 14 November 1958. Three people were killed in the attack and Matri was wounded. Tečlan was shot dead at the scene by security personnel.

## Background

Little is reliably documented about Tečlan's life. He was 31 years old at the time of the attack. Because he was killed at the scene and the [[Minutemen]] did not claim him publicly, the historical record on his background, motivations, and affiliations is thin and contested. The official account established his Minutemen affiliation and the premeditated nature of the attack; details beyond this were shaped substantially by the subsequent investigation and by [[Filevir Matri|Matri's]] political use of the event.

## The assassination attempt

> *Main article: [[Matri assassination attempt]]*

On 14 November 1958, Matri addressed an event at the Duolij Civic Hall announcing the construction of a highway connecting [[Duolij]] and [[Nikuria]]. Tečlan attended posing as a member of the press, having concealed a firearm within the shell of a camera. The press pool's position near the podium placed him within one to two meters of Matri.

Tečlan opened fire during the speech, discharging multiple rounds toward the podium. A security officer pulled Matri to the ground; a round struck Matri in the lower abdomen during the fall. Two [[White Stork Party]] officials standing near the podium and one security officer were killed. Tečlan continued firing into the resulting chaos before being shot dead by security personnel.

The event was being recorded by multiple cameras and broadcast live by Postian Public Television, making it the earliest known recording of a political assassination attempt in Susian history.

## Legacy

The official record offered no platform for Tečlan's account, and the absence of a trial or testimony left his stated reasoning undocumented. His identity and motivation were subsequently subordinated to the political narrative [[Filevir Matri|Matri]] constructed around the attack, which strengthened Matri's anti-syndicalist credentials and validated the crackdown of the [[Ant Wars]] period in public memory.
