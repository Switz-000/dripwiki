**Venvek Rashera** was an technology entrepreneur and founder of [[Yerčemark|Yerčemark Corporation]] and co-founder of [[Verene Biosciences]].

In 2063, his fortune was estimated to be D$43 billion, all of which was divided equally among his children.
## Personal Life

### Children

In 2043, Rashera claimed to have fathered more than 100 children via sperm donation in 12 countries since 2032. In the same year, he announced that he would leave his fortune to be divided equally to all of his 112 children after his death. He has open-sourced his DNA to make it easier for his children to find each other.
