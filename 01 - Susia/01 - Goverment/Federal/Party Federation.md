A **party federation** is a type of political organization in [[Susia]], composed of state parties that agree on a common federal policy. Federations put forward candidates for the presidency; state parties put forward candidates for the [[Susian Senate]], under their own names and identified with their federation.

## Origins

The two federations of the republican era, the [[Susian Liberal Party]] and the [[Susian Democratic Union]], were both founded in 1954, the year of the constitution.

## Federations and their affiliates

A federation's program concerns federal questions and the general direction of the republic, not the politics of any one state. Its affiliates therefore vary widely in position from state to state, and a state may have more than one affiliate of the same federation.

In [[Postia]], the Democratic Union's affiliates included the [[White Stork Party]], the centre-right party of the Duolij commercial class, and the [[Postian Free Farmers]]. In [[Dripia]], its affiliate held positions closer to those of Postia's [[Democratic Laborist Party]], an affiliate of the rival Liberal federation, than to those of the White Storks.

A federation can contain organized wings. The Liberal federation had three: a Versijian wing, a labor wing and a centrist pragmatic wing.

## Ballots

On the presidential ballot, a candidate appears with his or her name and federation only, because the federation, as a collection of parties, puts the candidate forward. On the Senate ballot, a candidate appears with his or her party and, beside it, the party's federation, which influences some votes.

## Federal and state politics

In the republican era, matters internal to a state stayed in the [[Susian Senate]] and were not taken up by the federal executive. The practice made Susian politics of the period Senate-centred, closer to a parliamentary system than it later became. [[Suizo Soites]] governed with broad support from both federations and left the states largely to themselves.

The practice was tested in 1956, when the Senate removed the federal commissioner provision from the [[Dravda Act]]. It eroded under the pressure of the [[Continental Divide]], as foreign threats came to be treated as national matters inside the states, beginning with the [[Federal Loyalty Act]] of 1961. [[Čalsor Boňár]] ended it outright with the [[MAGEN act]] of 1975, in a change whose causes were chiefly external to Susian politics.

The presidency, elected by national popular vote, tends to represent the larger states; the Senate, with equal representation, gives weight to the smaller ones. As the presidency gained power after 1974, the two became rivals.

## Contemporary Relevance

The federations active as of 2080 are the [[Coalition of State's Rights]], the [[Democratic-Federalist Party]] and the [[Modernist Alliance]]. The Liberal federation and the Democratic Union dissolved in 2038.
