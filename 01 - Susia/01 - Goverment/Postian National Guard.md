The **Postian National Guard** is the [[National Guard]] of the state of [[Postia]]. Like the guards of other Susian states, it can be activated for natural disasters, public disorder, and federal missions. During the [[Ant Wars]] it served as the principal instrument of order under governor [[Filevir Matri]] and functioned in practice as the enforcement arm of his administration.

## History

The Postian National Guard exists within the framework of the Susian [[National Guard]] system, which ties state militia capacity to the republic's founding institutions and the constitutional [[Right to rebel|right to rebel]]. Its most significant early deployment came during the [[Ant Wars]] of 1954 to 1959, the period of eossaj violence centered on [[Postia|Postia's]] industrial cities.

During the [[New Duloc riot (1958)]], [[Filevir Matri]] activated the Guard after police containment of the riot collapsed. The Guard deployed across [[New Duloc]] and parts of [[Duolij]], enforcing the curfews and state of emergency Matri declared. Following the [[Supreme Court of Susia|Supreme Court]] ruling that dissolved the eossajs, the Guard was central to the crackdown that ended the eossaj period.

## Structure

The Postian National Guard is a state institution under the executive authority of the governor of [[Postia]], operating within the national [[National Guard]] framework. It is distinct from the political parties that operated their own eossajs during the early republic.

## Powers and Limitations

The Guard may be activated for natural disasters, public disorder, and federal foreign missions. Its activation during the [[Ant Wars]] fell under the public disorder provision. Its status as a state body rather than a party paramilitary placed it outside the scope of the [[Supreme Court of Susia|Supreme Court]] ruling that declared eossajs unconstitutional, a distinction that left the [[White Stork Party]] with access to organized force after its rivals' paramilitary wings had been dissolved.

## Culture

As with the national [[National Guard]] system, service in the Postian Guard draws on a population that has completed [[Military Service in Susia|mandatory national service]], producing a body with baseline firearms competence and organizational discipline.

## Controversies

The Guard's role during the [[Ant Wars]] is the central point of contention in its early history. Because the [[White Stork Party]] maintained no eossaj of its own, the Guard functioned in practice as the governing party's instrument of force. Critics of [[Filevir Matri]] held that his activation of the Guard, and his use of it during the post-ruling crackdown, applied state power selectively against political rivals while the party that controlled it remained formally outside the street violence of the period.
