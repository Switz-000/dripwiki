The **Techno-Federative Republic of Susia** is a federal presidential democracy located on the continent of [[Dripstan]], bordered by [[Confia]] and [[Zaphonia]] to the east, [[Dripland]] to the northeast, [[Astofayan]] to the south and southwest, and [[Sutsa-Fuhu]] to the south-southeast. With a population of approximately 240 million as of 2070 and a capital at [[Soiteslaj]], it is the continent's most populous and economically developed nation. Its official language is Susian, and its majority religion is [[Armotism]].

Susia is a constitutional democracy with an unusual hybrid structure that combines elected democratic institutions with meritocratic technocratic bodies and a formally regulated system of corporate self-governance through [[Free Economic Zone|Free Economic Zones]] administered by corporations called [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]]. This system, established by the [[The Great Transition|Great Transition of 2038]], replaced a traditional federative republic that had governed the country since its founding in 1954 following the dissolution of the [[Dripstanian Empire]].

The country is defined externally by a cold war rivalry with [[Ashgerad]], a technocratic civilization on the continent of [[Incria]], which shapes Susian foreign policy, military spending, and domestic governance debates. Internally, it is defined by the tension between democratic tradition, corporate power, and the cultural inheritance of [[Armotism|Armotist]] settlement, the [[Liberal Revolts]], and the [[Continental War]].

## History

> *Main articles: [[Susian History]], [[Dripstanian Empire]], [[The Great Transition]]*

### Settlement and Empire (1651-1954)

Susia's origins lie in the arrival of [[Armotism|Armotist]] religious refugees from the continent of [[Gaiyan]] in 1651, following the suppression of the [[Armotist Revolution]] in the [[Holy Kingdom of Duloc]]. The ocean crossing, made treacherous by the tidal forces of the planet's two natural satellites, killed an estimated 40% of the original colonists. The first permanent settlement was established at [[Forchev]] in 1654.

The settlements gradually consolidated into the [[Dripstanian Empire]], formalized in 1674 when [[Emperor Saint Mantichev]] convened the Convention of Forchev and unified the western coastal kingdoms. Over the following three centuries, the empire expanded eastward across the continent through military conquest, frontier settlement by the [[Cericeiro|Cericeiros]], and the colonization of [[Confia]] and [[Kamino]]. It became the largest empire in Dripstanian history.

The empire's later centuries were marked by increasing constitutional conflict. [[Empress Veronique]]'s reforms in the eighteenth century created the Dripstanian Senate and a permanent professional army while also overseeing the ethnic cleansing of the [[Tekur|Konph people]] of Confia. [[Jartes II]]'s reign in the nineteenth century produced the [[Liberal Revolts]] of 1840-1844, a republican uprising that, despite its defeat, established the martyrs [[Armadesh Versij]] and [[Zagoste Paroska]] as foundational figures in Susian national memory and the execution of Versij as the defining symbol of the cost of disarmament and tyranny.

The empire began its terminal decline with the [[War of Confian Secession]] (1927-1950), which split it into autonomous Susian and Confian halves. Following the death of Emperor Soliman in 1950, the [[Tahuni Accords]] of 1954 formally dissolved the empire and granted independence to both successor states.

### The Republic (1954-2038)

The [[Techno-Federative Republic of Susia|Federative Republic of Susia]] was founded in 1954 under the leadership of [[Suizo Soites]], who had organized the constitutional convention and secured agreement among the former imperial provinces. Soites became the country's first president, serving two terms and establishing the republic's core institutions: the elected Senate, the constitutional protection of natural rights drawn from [[Armadesh Versij|Versij's]] philosophy, the interstate highway system, the national service requirement, and the [[ONDD]] military alliance.

The republic's early decades were dominated by the [[Continental Divide]] (1957-1977), a cold war between Susia's liberal capitalist bloc and [[Confia]]'s authoritarian syndicalist bloc under [[Lorelaj Paulovic]]. The divide produced proxy conflicts across the continent, the [[MAGEN Act]] of 1961 restricting domestic left-wing parties, and eventually the [[Continental War]] (1975-1977), a direct military confrontation that ended with the defeat of the syndicalist bloc and the post-war [[Flower Revolutions]] that brought democratic governments to Confia and several other states.

The post-war boom produced rapid economic and technological growth, the rise of corporations that would later become [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]], and Susia's development of nuclear weapons, which arrived too late for the continental war but established the country as a nuclear power. The emergence of [[Ashgerad]] as an adversary after 2006 and the humiliating [[War of Arempur]] (2012), a proxy defeat overseas, intensified pressure for institutional reform.

### The Great Transition (2038)

> *Main article: [[The Great Transition]]*

The [[The Great Transition|Great Transition of 2038]] replaced the traditional republic with the current Techno-Federative system. Driven by the [[Modernist Alliance]] political movement and influenced, in distorted form, by the competitive governance theories of philosopher [[Yário Kolkov]], the transition introduced the [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ system]], the [[Secretariat of Efficiency]], the bicameral [[Chamber of Experts]], and the constitutional framework for [[Yarnojte]] corporate governance. The transition was the product of negotiation between competing political factions and substantial corporate lobbying, particularly from [[Troli Ustaras]] and [[Nayotai]], and its relationship to Kolkov's actual philosophy remains a subject of debate.

## Government

Susia is a federal presidential republic with a hybrid democratic-technocratic structure operating across three levels: federal, state, and corporate Free Economic Zones.

### The Executive

The President serves terms of up to ten years, with a confidence vote required every five years to continue. Presidents may serve two terms for a maximum of twenty years. The president is constitutionally prohibited from affiliation with any political party, campaigns on issues that cross party lines, and is selected by ranked-choice vote. The philosophical basis for this structure is the "Great Executive Theory": the president embodies the nation as a whole rather than any faction, derives legitimacy directly from the people, and functions as a referee among competing institutional powers rather than a daily administrator or ideological leader.

Presidential powers include command of the military, foreign policy, executive appointments subject to Senate confirmation, veto power over legislation, and limited emergency powers subject to judicial review.

### The Legislature

The federal legislature is bicameral, comprising a democratic Senate and a meritocratic [[Chamber of Experts]].

The Senate holds 50 seats, five for each of Susia's nine states and five for the [[Neutral District]]. Senators are elected representatives who create legislation, set budgets, ratify treaties, confirm major appointments, and can override presidential vetoes with a supermajority or impeach the president for high crimes.

The Chamber of Experts holds 350 seats filled by professionals from diverse technical fields, selected through a national examination system on rotating three-year terms with a maximum of two terms. The chamber provides technical review of complex legislation and must approve bills alongside the Senate. The structure forces compromise between democratic representation and specialized expertise, and is intended to prevent both populist legislation that ignores technical reality and technocratic legislation that ignores democratic input.

### The Secretariat of Efficiency

> *Main article: [[Secretariat of Efficiency]]*

The [[Secretariat of Efficiency]] is an independent body separate from both the Cabinet and the legislature that oversees the [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ system]]. Its 350 members are selected through a rigorous national examination and serve rotating three-year terms capped at two, with strict conflict-of-interest rules that disqualify anyone with corporate affiliations. Its functions include granting and revoking [[Yarnojte]] status, mediating disputes between corporations, ensuring constitutional compliance within FEZs, and directing the [[Federal Authority of Commerce and Competition|AFCC]].

The Secretariat is the federal institution most resistant to corporate capture, though intellectual capture through corporate-funded research and conferences remains an acknowledged vulnerability. The [[Troli Ustaras Corruption Scandal (2047)|2047 TU bribery attempt]] established the clearest precedent for the absoluteness of its independence: attempting to corrupt federal officials results in immediate Yarnojte status revocation with no right of appeal.

### The Free Economic Zone System

> *Main article: [[Free Economic Zone]]*

[[Free Economic Zone|Free Economic Zones]] are city-sized territories leased by states to [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]], the six corporations holding strategic status under the Great Transition constitution. Within their FEZs, Yarnojtes govern with broad autonomy, providing housing, healthcare, education, and infrastructure to residents, who are overwhelmingly employees or their families. Residents retain federal constitutional rights regardless of FEZ governance structures, and FEZs may not prohibit firearm ownership, though gun culture is informally discouraged within most of them.

Approximately 0.5% of the Susian population lives within FEZs as of 2070. A larger share works for Yarnojtes in their non-FEZ operations. The FEZ system funds a significant portion of state budgets in [[Dripia]] and [[Postia]], and Susians who never enter a FEZ benefit indirectly from the products, energy, and healthcare they produce.

### Federal Agencies

The federal bureaucracy includes a small Cabinet of eight to ten secretaries reporting to the president, the [[Federal Intelligence Service|Frumentarze]] (the federal intelligence service), the [[Federal Marshals Service]], the [[Federal Authority of Commerce and Competition|AFCC]], the Federal Environmental Protection agency, and the Federal Border Guard.

The [[Federal Intelligence Service|Frumentarze]] employs approximately 45,000 people officially, operates with a partially classified budget estimated at D$180 billion annually, and is headquartered at the Citadel in [[Soiteslaj]]. Its mandate covers counter-intelligence, foreign operations, domestic security, and technical surveillance. It has been involved in several significant controversies, including the extrajudicial killing of a defecting enhancement researcher in 2062, the rendition and interrogation of an Ashgeradn diplomat in 2073, and a 2071 domestic wiretapping scandal in which the agency was found to have monitored over 1.2 million Susians without warrants.

## Economy

Susia is the most economically developed nation on the Dripstan continent and the only one classified as fully developed by continental economic organizations. Its GDP per capita ranges from D$72,000 in [[New Celiolaj]] to D$160,000 in [[Dripia]] as of 2070.

### The Dual Currency System

> *Main article: [[Susian Monetary System]]*

Susia operates two parallel currencies. The **Transactional Drip** (D$ or TD$) is a standard fiat currency issued by the Susian Central Bank, used for daily transactions, wages, and taxes, and maintained at a deliberate 3-4% annual inflation rate. The **Federal Reserve Unit** (FD$) is a commodity-basket backed currency pegged to a weighted basket of electricity futures, metals, food commodities, oil, and computing power, issued only when reserves exist, and stable at 0-1% annual change. The exchange rate in the 2070s is approximately FD$1 to D$3.2.

FD$ accounts are used for long-term contracts of ten years or more, including FEZ leases, infrastructure bonds, mortgages, and retirement accounts, shielding long-term financial planning from the inflation that affects the everyday economy. The system is widely credited with enabling the middle class to build generational wealth and providing Yarnojtes with the stability needed for multi-decade FEZ investment.

### Major Industries

Nuclear energy is a legal monopoly held by [[Atompron]], reflecting Susia's post-continental-war adoption of atomic power as a point of national pride and technological identity. Oil and gas extraction is an oligopoly historically dominated by [[POCOIL]], whose Yarnojte status was revoked in 2087 as nuclear power displaced petroleum. Robotics, AI, and automation are near-monopolies of [[Troli Ustaras]]. Manufacturing and automotive production are led by [[Nayotai]]. Retail, logistics, and consumer services are dominated by [[Soites Group]]. Healthcare and biomedical research are led by [[Lischev-Verene]]. Defense and aerospace are dominated by [[Van Ritter]].

Banking is more fragmented, with [[Nassoj Holdings]], [[Triževa Bank]], [[Yerčemark Corporation]], [[Silver Coast Bank]], [[Astralis Banking Group]], and [[Red Iron]] each serving distinct market segments.

## Society

### Demographics

Susia's population of approximately 240 million is predominantly ethnically Gaiyanese in descent, following the original Armotist settler waves of the seventeenth century. Approximately 16% of the population is foreign-born, with the largest groups being Confians, Zaphonians, Sutsa-Fuhans, Kaminoans, and Astofayanse. The [[Tekur]] people, indigenous to the Dripstan continent and subject to centuries of Dripstanian imperial persecution, were formally excluded from citizenship at Susia's founding in 1954 and granted full citizenship only in 1997 under the Reconciliation Act, which is commemorated by the federal holiday [[Reconciliation Day]].

### Religion

[[Armotism]] is the majority religion, observed by the overwhelming majority of the population. The faith traces its origins to the prophet Armotos, whose followers fled persecution in Gaiyan and founded the original Dripstanian settlements. Armotist theology is pluralistic in practice and has undergone significant evolution since the imperial era, with the religion's relationship to state authority shifting from an established theocracy under the early empire to a cultural background faith in the modern republic. [[Nessel]] has a distinct religious tradition, [[Doremojism]], that differentiates it culturally from the Armotist majority.

### Gun Culture

> *Main article: [[Susian Gun Culture]]*

Firearms ownership and competence are deeply embedded in Susian civic identity, rooted in the settlement era's practical necessity, the [[Cericeiro]] frontier tradition, the lesson drawn from [[Jartes II]]'s disarmament of the population before the [[Liberal Revolts]], and [[Armadesh Versij|Versij's]] philosophical argument that rights that cannot be defended are privileges waiting to be revoked. Gun safety is a required component of the civics curriculum in all Susian middle and high schools. The [[Sorzenko]] shooting club system remains an active civic institution, particularly in rural areas and the eastern states. Both open carry and concealed carry are constitutionally protected at the federal level. Gun culture is more reserved in urban western areas and actively discouraged within most FEZs.

### Military Service

Susia has maintained mandatory military service since the imperial era, reinstated at the start of the continental cold war. All citizens are required to serve one year in combat roles or two years in non-combat roles. After service, citizens may join their state's [[National Guard]], which can be activated by state governors for disasters or public disorder and by the federal government for foreign missions.

### Civic Institutions

The [[Sorzenko]] system, the [[Hăjaven]] communal festival, and mandatory military service collectively produce cross-class civic bonds that political observers regard as unusual in a society otherwise marked by sharp stratification between FEZ and non-FEZ populations, enhanced and unenhanced citizens, and regional economic inequality.

## Culture

Susian national culture is shaped by the intersection of [[Armotism|Armotist]] religious heritage, the natural rights philosophy of [[Armadesh Versij]], the pragmatic liberalism of [[Suizo Soites]], the competitive governance theory of [[Yário Kolkov]], and the [[Fortress Complex]] of perpetual civilizational siege. These traditions are in genuine tension and produce a political culture marked by intense philosophical debate, deep attachment to constitutional process, and recurring anxiety about whether that process is adequate to the challenges Susia faces.

[[Crolball]] is the national sport, created during the imperial era and now popular across the continent. The city of [[Velúria]] in [[Orlítia]], with its legal identity-change system and permissive legal environment, functions as a unique cultural institution within Susia, attracting whistleblowers, celebrities, criminals, and people seeking fresh starts in roughly equal measure.

The [[Vuževas]] phenomenon, referring to young adults who have withdrawn from physical social life into digital and simulated environments, is an emerging sociological concern concentrated in large cities, with estimates suggesting over two million affected individuals as of 2080.

## Foreign Relations

Susia's foreign policy is shaped by three commitments: the [[ONDD]] military alliance with its democratic partners on the Dripstan continent, the [[Lasman Economic Initiative]] customs union with [[Confia]], and the cold war posture toward [[Ashgerad]].

The ONDD, founded by Susia in 1961 to contain Confian syndicalism, shifted its focus after the Continental War toward Ashgeradn containment and the maintenance of what Susian strategic doctrine calls the "Continental Fortress." Its current members include Susia, [[Kapiogg]], [[Astofayan]], [[Pierej]], [[Dripland]], [[Confia]], and [[Kamino]].

Susia's relationship with Confia has transformed from existential rivalry to close partnership. The Lasman Economic Initiative of 1995 established a customs union, Confia joined the ONDD in 1998, and Susian corporations including [[Nayotai]] and [[Soites Group]] operate extensively in the Confian market.

The Ashgeradn cold war is the defining foreign policy challenge of the contemporary era. The two civilizations share no cultural, historical, religious, or philosophical common ground, having developed in complete isolation until contact in the nineteenth century, and their fundamental disagreement about the nature of human rights and the relative value of freedom versus efficiency has produced a rivalry that Susians across the political spectrum tend to understand in civilizational terms.
