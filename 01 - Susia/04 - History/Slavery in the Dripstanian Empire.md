Slavery in the [[Dripstanian Empire]] was a regional institution concentrated in [[Troli]]. It was small by comparison with the plantation systems of other continents, and its enslaved population was drawn principally from [[Zaphonia|Zaphonians]] captured in [[Cericeiro]] raids before Zaphonia's annexation to the empire. [[Empress Prazde]] abolished it during the [[Dripstanian Civil War]].

## Origins

> *Main article: [[Cericeiro]]*

Troli lies on the empire's southeastern frontier and shares a border with Zaphonia. Cericeiro expansion carried Dripstanian settlement east through [[Misocévia]], [[Sužielaj]], and Troli, and raiding across the Zaphonian border supplied the captives who became the enslaved population of the Trolian interior. The raids predate the vassalage of 1790, which brought Zaphonia into the empire under [[Empress Yaneoli]] and ended the conditions that had allowed them.

The [[Cericeiro honor code]] extended its obligations to other Cericeiros and to those under their protection, and populations the settlers were in armed conflict with fell outside it.

## Extent

Slavery never became a general institution of the empire. It was confined largely to Troli, whose agriculture was organised on a plantation model of a scale that supported bonded labour.

Neighbouring [[Vitrika]], settled on smaller homesteads, did not develop a comparable demand and the institution did not establish itself there. The distinction between the two states is agricultural rather than legal, and it is the principal reason the institution stayed regional.

## Abolition

> *Main article: [[Dripstanian Civil War]]*

Prazde abolished slavery during the civil war, while the throne was contested. The landowners of Troli revolted against her in the course of the same war.

## Integration

The freed Zaphonian population was absorbed into the empire over the period running from the reign of [[Jartes I]] to that of [[Emperor Soliman|Soliman]]. Zaphonians converted to [[Armotism]], took up Dripstanian customs and institutions, moved within the empire under internal migration, and volunteered for imperial military service.

Freed Zaphonians and their descendants intermarried with the settled population of Troli. They constitute the largest minority in the modern state.

## Contemporary relevance

Mixed Trolijians observe traditions that are historically associated with slavery, and the association is not treated as contentious in the state.

Troli also observes [[Cericeiro Heritage Week]], which honours the frontier settlers who explored and settled eastern Susia. The state's largest minority descends from the population those settlers raided.
