The **Dripstanian Civil War** (1815–1823), also called the **Fraternal War**, was a war of succession between the twin children of [[Empress Yaneoli]], [[Empress Prazde|Prazde]] and [[Jartes I|Jartes]]. Prazde was crowned on her mother's death and held the throne for the length of the war. Jartes won it, and reigned until 1837.

## Background

Yaneoli died in 1815 leaving two heirs and no settled succession. Prazde was heir to the throne at [[Neoveli]] and was crowned in 1815, holding the Dripstanian and Zaphonian crowns simultaneously. Jartes contested the succession, claiming that the throne was his, and sent Prazde a letter containing a threat. She rejected it, and at her coronation read it aloud to her new subjects in a mocking tone before tearing it up in public.

## Outbreak

[[Colin, King of the Tekurs]], Prazde's uncle and ruler of the vassal [[Kingdom of Tekuria]], declared later the same year that Jartes was the rightful Dripstanian emperor and that Prazde was "a sandstone-fucking impostor". The army of [[Drippalia]] mobilised and rebel troops seized key buildings in [[Lijssick]] and [[Baabren]]. Drippalia and its vassals declared for Jartes.

The revolt spread north. The dukes of [[Nessel]] and [[New Celiolaj]] joined Jartes, recruiting their subjects on the promise of land at the end of the war.

## The war

Prazde mobilised the [[Dripstanian Central Army]], which won the opening years of the war, including the battles of [[Ketu]] and [[Vassénia]].

## Aftermath

Jartes took the throne and reigned until 1837. He dated his claim to 1815 rather than to the end of the war, and [[List of Dripstanian emperors]] records both his claimed reign and Prazde's disputed one across the same eight years.
