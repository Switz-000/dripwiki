The **Matri assassination attempt of 1957** was an attempted shooting of [[Filevir Matri]], governor of [[Postia]], during his [[Versij Day]] address in [[Nikuria]] on 30/09/1957. The attacker, a Postian dockworker whose son was being held without trial after a crackdown sweep, was disarmed by a [[Confia|Confian]] veteran and bystanders before he fired. He had no connection to the [[Nevirs]] but was recorded as one. It was the second of three attempts on Matri's life during the [[Ant wars]].

## Background

> *Main article: [[Ant wars]]*

Matri delivered the Versij Day address of 1957 in Nikuria, a city where a battle of the [[Liberal Revolts]] had been fought. The address was given in the open air to a crowd of several thousand. Much of the crowd carried firearms by custom, and screening it was not possible.

The attacker was a native Postian dockworker in his fifties. His son had been detained in a sweep about a year earlier and was being held without trial.

## The attempt

The attacker drew a pistol as Matri began to speak. A veteran of the [[War of Confian Secession]] who lived in the [[Movement of New Susians|Movement of New Susians']] neighborhood of [[New Duloc]], standing a few meters away, saw the weapon and tackled him before he fired. Bystanders disarmed him. No shots were fired, and Matri finished the speech.

## Aftermath

The attacker was tried in the Postian state courts in late 1957 and convicted of attempted murder. He was sentenced to 15 years in prison. The authorities recorded him as a Nevir. His testimony about his son's detention is part of the court record and received little coverage in the press. His son was released without charge in 1958.

The veteran was naturalized as a Susian citizen in 1959. He later received the [[Order of the First Guard of the Republic]], the first recipient born outside Susia.

Matri's habit of walking in a zigzag dates from this attempt.
