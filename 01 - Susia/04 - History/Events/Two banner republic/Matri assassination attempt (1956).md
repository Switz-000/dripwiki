The **Matri assassination attempt of 1956** was a failed car bombing of [[Filevir Matri]], governor of [[Postia]], in [[Duolij]] on 19/05/1956. A bomb placed under his official car failed to detonate and was found that evening. A Nevir letter announcing his death had been mailed in advance and was printed by [[Ti-ka!]] that morning. It was the first of three attempts on Matri's life during the [[Ant wars]].

## Background

> *Main article: [[Nevirs]]*

By 1956 the [[Nevirs]] had carried out the [[Neoveli Stock Exchange Bombing]] and the assassination of Senator [[Halios Dravda]]. Postian newspapers printed Nevir letters in full on receipt.

## The attempt

The device was placed under Matri's official car overnight at the state garage in Duolij. The firing mechanism failed. Matri was driven to three public engagements across the city during the day with the device in place. His driver found it during the routine check of the car at the garage that evening. The investigators' technical report on the device was never released in full.

The Nevir letter claiming the attack had been mailed the day before so that it would reach the press ahead of the explosion. [[Ti-ka!]] printed it in its morning edition, announcing the death of the governor on the day he was alive and at work. A rival paper published a photograph of Matri at the door of the governor's office with that edition under his arm.

## Aftermath

No one was charged with planting the device.

The editors of Duolij's newspapers agreed to pass every Nevir letter to the police before printing it. The arrangement was voluntary and never written into law.

Matri's car was inspected daily from then on, and his routes were varied.
