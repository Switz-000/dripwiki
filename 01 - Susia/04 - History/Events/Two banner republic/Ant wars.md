The **Ant Wars** were a period of political violence in [[Postia]] between 1954 and 1959, during the early years of the [[Susia|Susian]] republic. The conflict was driven by eossajs, party-affiliated paramilitary organizations that fought in the streets of Postia's cities, and by the [[Nevirs]], an anarchist current conducting bombings and assassinations under a single pseudonym. The period ended with a [[Supreme Court of Susia|Supreme Court]] ruling declaring eossajs unconstitutional and the subsequent crackdown overseen by governor [[Filevir Matri]]. The name derives from eossaj, the Susian term for ant nest.

## Background

Postia in the early republic combined conditions that made sustained political violence likely. Its port and industrial cities supported a militant labor movement, its institutions were new and untested, and it held a large [[Confia|Confian]] refugee population. The [[Susian Constitution|constitution]] ratified in 1954 protected the right to bear arms and enshrined an explicit [[Right to rebel|right to rebel]], which provided ideological justification for armed political organization across the spectrum.

Political parties formed eossajs as paramilitary wings. These organizations paraded, intimidated rivals, and fought in the streets. The principal eossajs in Postia were the [[Knights of the Republic]], the wing of the [[Pačese ro Virspruje]], and the syndicalist eossaj of the [[Democratic Laborist Party]]. Both parties were Postian affiliates of the [[Susian Liberal Party]], belonging to its Versijian and labor wings respectively, so the main street conflict in Postia was fought between two members of the same federation. The [[Movement of New Susians]], a Confian refugee self-defense organization, operated on similar lines but without party affiliation. The [[White Stork Party]], the party of governor [[Filevir Matri]], maintained no eossaj, relying on the [[Postian National Guard]] instead.

## The factions

The eossaj landscape was not a simple division between left and right. The [[Knights of the Republic]] were civic republicans grounded in the philosophy of [[Armadesh Versij]], restrained in conduct but willing to retaliate when provoked, and they fought both syndicalist eossajs and, increasingly, the state itself. The Laborists' eossaj was more openly aggressive, treating the republic as structurally unjust, and was more radical than the party it served. The [[Nevirs]] stood apart from the eossaj system entirely: anarchists who conducted bombings and targeted assassinations rather than street demonstrations.

The labor movement added a further dimension through strikes and workplace standoffs at the docks and factories of [[New Duloc]]. The [[Movement of New Susians]], composed largely of veterans of the [[War of Confian Secession]], confined itself to defending immigrant neighborhoods and deliberately avoided both the labor movement and partisan politics, a stance that drew resentment from a native working class that viewed Confian labor as undercutting strikes.

## The Nevirs

> *Main article: [[Nevirs]]*

The Nevirs announced and claimed their attacks in letters signed with a single pseudonym, Nevir, which belonged to no real person. Most of their attacks were carried out by individuals acting alone. [[Filevir Matri|Matri's]] government described them as an anarcho-syndicalist network of cells and pursued that network across Postia, and the label joined the anarchists to the syndicalist movement in official usage.

The [[Neoveli Stock Exchange Bombing]] of 30/05/1955 killed 43 people and took the campaign outside Postia. Senator [[Halios Dravda]] of the [[Pačese ro Virspruje]] drafted a bill of federal intervention in Postia in response and was killed by a Nevir letter bomb in [[Soiteslaj]] on 17/10/1955. The bill passed in 1956 as the [[Dravda Act]], without its provision for a federal commissioner, leaving the response in Postia with the governor.

Three attempts were made on Matri's life during the period: a car bomb in 1956 that failed to detonate, a shooting in 1957 stopped by the crowd, and the shooting by [[Urgiri Tečlan]] in 1958.

## The New Duloc riot

> *Main article: [[New Duloc riot (1958)]]*

The central event of the Ant Wars was the [[New Duloc riot (1958)]]. The assassination of a [[Knights of the Republic]] leader by a Nevir, occurring while a labor standoff was already underway at the New Duloc docks, triggered a citywide breakdown of order. Police redeployed from the docks to contain the mobilizing Knights, the dock standoff collapsed, and the two situations merged into several days of rioting that left several hundred dead. The [[Movement of New Susians]] held its neighborhoods through the violence while surrounding districts burned.

## Supreme Court ruling and crackdown

Following the riot, [[Filevir Matri]] declared a state of emergency, deploying the [[Postian National Guard]] and imposing curfews. The constitutional status of eossajs was brought before the [[Supreme Court of Susia]], which possessed the authority to strike down arrangements violating the constitution. The Court ruled that the formal combination of political affiliation and armed organization fell outside constitutional protection.

The ruling was contested. The [[Knights of the Republic]] and [[Pačese ro Virspruje]] argued it misread Versij's citizen-defender framework and the constitutional logic that treated organized civilian militia capacity as the practical guarantee of the right to rebel. The argument did not prevail. The ruling gave Matri a legal instrument for a comprehensive crackdown that dissolved the eossajs and swept up syndicalist organizations, union leadership, the cells attributed to the Nevirs, and the politically unaffiliated [[Movement of New Susians]].

## Aftermath

The crackdown ended the eossaj period in Postia. Eossajs did not reappear as a formal institution in Susian political history. The [[Matri assassination attempt (1958)]] of 11/1958, carried out by [[Urgiri Tečlan]], an anarchist recorded as a Nevir, during the crackdown, wounded Matri and provided him with political capital that shaped the remainder of his career.

The [[Movement of New Susians]] suffered a lasting deterioration in its position after the 1961 [[Federal Loyalty Act]], which recast Confian immigrants as a security risk. The Ant Wars are remembered as a formative episode of the early republic, and Matri's conduct during the period remains contested in assessments of his record.
