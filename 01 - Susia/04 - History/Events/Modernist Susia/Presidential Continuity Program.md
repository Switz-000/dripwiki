The **Presidential Continuity Program** was a classified [[Susian Federal Government|federal]] program running from 2077 to 2084. It was organized by the [[Frumentarze]] and contracted to [[Van Ritter]], with the artificial intelligence work conducted in partnership with [[Troli Ustaras]]. Its purpose was to maintain the function of government in the event of thermonuclear war by constructing artificial intelligence reconstructions of senior officeholders. The program is unknown to the public.

## Background

> *Main article: [[Fortress complex]]*

The program was authorized under the strategic assumptions of the [[Fortress complex]] and the [[global-cold-war|cold war]] with [[Ashgerad]]. Reconstructions were built of the President, five key Senators, three [[Supreme Court of Susia|Supreme Court]] justices, and the Director of the [[Secretariat of Efficiency]]. Only the President was informed of the program's full extent.

## Objective

The program's objective was the reconstruction of its subjects' cognition, including voice, temperament, and decision making, rather than the reproduction of their speech patterns. A reconstruction was intended to reach the same decisions as its subject and to reach them by the same reasoning.

The success criterion followed from this. A set of 100 questions was put to both the subject and the reconstruction, and the reconstruction was counted as successful only where its answers matched the subject's and the reasoning that produced them matched as well.

## Method

Reconstructions were assembled from speeches, recorded decisions, writings, and interviews, combined with neural mapping and full brain scans of the subjects.

## Discontinuation

The criterion set out at the program's authorization was not met, and reconstruction proved substantially more difficult than projected. The program was officially discontinued in 2084 on grounds of ethical concerns. Available evidence indicates that technical limitations were the operative factor.

The underlying data remains archived. [[Van Ritter]] retains the capability and could restart the program if ordered to do so.
