**Lizne** (literally "connect") is a [[Susia|Susian]] messaging service and social media platform owned by [[Soites Group]]. It is the principal platform associated with the [[Vuževa|Vuževas]].

## History

Lizne was created by [[Klech Gourini]] and [[Hervan Naveo]] in 2014.

In 2022, [[Soites Group]] bought Lizne for D$3 billion in cash and stock in a move by [[Chedji Soites]] to diversify from retail into data and entertainment.

## Algorithmic promotion finding

In the 2050s Lizne's recommendation system was found to have promoted some political parties over others during an election period. The pattern was produced by the recommendation system itself rather than by editorial direction from [[Soites Group]].

The [[Secretariat of Efficiency]] required Lizne to make the recommendation system auditable. The [[Susian Senate]] subsequently passed legislation requiring social media platforms to maintain balance in the political content they promote, modeled on the existing equal-time requirements applied to television broadcast.
