**Lizne** (Lit. 'Connect') is a [[Susia|Susian]] messaging service and social media platform owned by [[Soites Group]]. 


# History

Lize was created by [[Klech Gourini]] and [[Hervan Naveo]] in 2014. 

In 2022, [[Soites Group]] bought Lizne for 3 Billion drips in cash and stock in a move by [[Chedji Soites]] to diversify from retail to data and entertainment.
