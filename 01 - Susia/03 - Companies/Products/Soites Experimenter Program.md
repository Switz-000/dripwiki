## The Pitch:

- “What if your job was just… living? Join the Soites Experimenter Program. Get paid to try new products. Share your authentic experience. Build your brand. Live the life you always dreamed of.” (Soites recruitment video)
- Why do people apply: Get paid to consume (sounds like a dream job), become a micro celebrity, travel for free, free housing food and products, content creation, networking
- Content creation and publicizing are secondary. The primary motive of the program is to study comprehensively how different psychological pathologies respond to consumption and to ensure the maximization of the appeal of Soites products. This involves: Daily brain scans, rating products etc. 
- Every year [[Soites Group|Soites]] receives more than 300,000 applications worldwide (mostly in [[Susia]] but international interest too). About 7,000 of these are accepted annually.
- Soites explicitly selects for: Physical attractiveness, charisma, narcissistic traits and "Interesting" personalities. 
  
## Life of a Experimenter

- Years 1-5, The Ascent: Everything for free, everything fun, travel, parties, products, gaining followers, confidence soaring
- The 5 year contract renewal: Every five years Soites offers three options: 
	- Renew (Increased pay, better housing, more high profile appearances), 
	- Transition to corporate (competitive, but some manage to score good position in marketing, HR and content creation) or 
	- Leave (Free courses in marketing, communication, business. Alumni network access, resume building and 3 months of job placing assistance)
	- 68% Renew “Just five more years! Next year i will leave” (they won’t), 12% Transition, 20% Leave
- Years 6-10, A Boiling Frog: Still fun, but novelty wears off. Followers plateau or decline (new Experimenters more interesting), weight gain, identity blurring, aging visible, skills atrophying, jealousy emerges.
- Years 11-15, The Desperation: Marginalized, significant weight gain, sabotage behavior begins (spreading rumors, social exclusion)
- Years 16+, Irrelevance: Ghost status, maintained as study subjects only. Fired if deemed unnecessary of violations of contract (criminal actions, causing public scandals, breaking a NDA)
## The Fall: Living hell after tasting heaven

- Drunk on popularity: After five years, Soites noticed how Experimenters get accustomed to the life style, as expected, but some develop 


## Miscellaneous Information
