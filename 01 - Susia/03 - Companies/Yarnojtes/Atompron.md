**Atompron** is a [[Susia|Susian]] nuclear energy corporation and one of the six [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]]. It was founded in 1979 by the [[Susian Federal Government]] under President [[Čalsor Boňár]] as a state energy company, with its headquarters in [[Niqueparj]]. It holds a monopoly on nuclear power in Susia.

## History

Atompron was founded during the post-war reconstruction, as part of Boňár's program of state-directed development. Nuclear power was adopted widely in Susia in the decades after the [[Continental War]]. Atompron was among the first corporations granted Yarnojte status, in 2041. It commercialized a thorium fuel cycle in 2061 and fusion in 2081. Its dominance in nuclear power displaced [[POCOIL]], whose Yarnojte status was revoked in 2087.

## Operations

In 2080 Atompron supplied about 78% of Susia's electricity. With [[Van Ritter]] it builds the reactors for nuclear-powered submarines and for thorium-powered commercial freight ships, which made up about 40% of Susian civil freight ships in 2080, and it works with Van Ritter on research into nuclear propulsion in space. It operates the [[Central Energy District]] and [[Karjo Nuclear Complex]] FEZs.

## Corporate Culture

Atompron is the least visible of the Yarnojtes to most Susians. Its corporate culture treats civilian firearms as anachronistic.
