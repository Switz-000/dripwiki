### The Merger

[[Lischev Pharmaceuticals]] (1962)

- Origin: Named after [[Apostle Lichev]], the healer from [[Armotism|Armotist]] tradition (named after a saint because the founder thought it was good marketing)
- Roots: Public traded corporation headquartered in [[Vitrika]]
- Focus: Traditional Pharmaceuticals, pain management and disease treatment manufacturing 

[[Verene Biosciences]] (2001)

- Origin: Named after [[Verene]] the goddess of rest and caretaker of the Garden of Latice in Armotist religion
- Roots: A Public Benefit Corporation from [[Damčev]]
- Focus: Genetic therapy, regenerative medicine and enhancement procedures

The Deal (2065)

- After the enhancement boom of the 2060s, Verene Biosciences struggled to keep up with demand as it lacked manufacturing capabilities.
- By the 2050s Lischev Pharmaceuticals was losing market share to competitors due to lackluster innovation and wasn’t able to adapt to the enhancement boom
- Lischev-Verene was founded as a traditional corporation, eliminating Verene’s PBC status. As the bigger and more established partner, Lischev’s more pragmatic and professional culture absorbed Verene’s more idealistic culture

### Competitors and Partners

[[Troli Ustaras]]: Hostile but forced partnership
- Cooperates in enhancement hardware: TU builds neural interfaces, LV develops biological compatibility drugs and realizes the medical procedures
- [[Susian Federal Goverment|Federal]] Mandate: "Cooperate or forfeit military contracts"
- Cross-licensing Patents: Legally required, mutually resented
- Talent Poaching: Aggressive recruitment of each other’s researchers
- Academic Warfare: Competing papers, conference presentations attacking each other’s philosophy
- Philosophical Clash: LV: “Consciousness IS biology. You can't upload a soul.” TU: “Consciousness can be separated from biology. Flesh is just the first draft.”
### Corporate Culture: “The transactional Clinic”

- No “Community” Rhetoric: “You’re here to get treatment and then leave. I’m your doctor, not your friend.”
- Professional Opacity: Uses standard medical/legal jargon. They don’t lie, but don’t go on their way to explain it either.
- Medical Paternalism: “We know what is best for you”
- High turnover expected: People come for 2 to 5 years, get treatment, then leave. Why bother making relationships?
### Corporate Philosophy: “Perfecting the Flesh”

- In a world where the human body is increasingly abused and commodified, a company standing up against it, is appealing
- “Consciousness IS biology. You can’t upload the soul, so we perfect the flesh”
- “The body is a temple and must be well maintained.”
- Opposes the organ trade, conflicted opinions on enhancement.
- LV is a counterweight to [[Troli Ustaras|TU]]’s radical post-human future.
- Goal: Perfect humanity through genetic optimization, biological enhancement, life extension.
### The LV Compact

What You Get:

- Affordable, proven treatment: D$800/month for full healthcare coverage (free for employees)
- Rare disease treatments unavailable elsewhere included
- Free experimental treatments, high risk, but potentially life saving
- Life Extension access ([[VITAKEI]])

What You Costs:

- Your genetic information becomes company property forever
- Total and invasive surveillance while resident (biological functions monitored, daily and weekly tests)
- Your Autonomy: VITAKEI tells you how to eat, drink, sleep.

### Major Achievements

- Genuine life extension, residents in the [[Garden of Latice FEZ]] live to 120-140 years old
- Cutting edge treatments, cured rare diseases
- Affordable healthcare: D$800/month Full coverage with genetic sharing and D$2000/month without genetic sharing
- World class research and research finding sharing
- Cured Alzheimer’s and Parkinson’s, Osteoporosis and some types of Dementia. Nearly eliminated in Susia: Breast, Prostate, Colorectal, Stomach and Lung Cancer by 2090.
- Created its own version of [[Cognitive enhancement]], focused on being less invasive and more “health” oriented, competes with [[Troli Ustaras|TU]] in the market
