**Van Ritter Incorporated** is a [[Susia|Susian]] defense and aerospace corporation and one of the nation's six active [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]]. Founded in 1923 in [[Lore-Kurji]], [[Misocévia]] as an aerospace engineering firm, it is now Susia's primary defense contractor, producing military hardware, aerospace systems, and government cybersecurity and intelligence technology. The company is headquartered at the [[Eastern Industrial Complex FEZ]] in [[Misocévia]] and employs approximately 680,000 people. Its market capitalization is D$1.4 trillion as of 2080. [[Yarnojte]] status was granted in 2044.

Van Ritter occupies a structural position in the Susian state with no equivalent among the other Yarnojtes. Its primary client is the [[Susian Federal Government]], and much of its work is conducted under classification levels that place it outside normal institutional oversight. The company also maintains substantial civilian product lines in transportation, communications, and GPS infrastructure, competing directly with [[Nayotai]] in several sectors. The company's culture, its FEZs, and its employment structure all reflect an organization that treats security as both its core product and its governing logic.

## History

### Founding and Imperial Era (1923–1954)

Van Ritter was founded in 1923 in [[Lore-Kurji]], [[Misocévia]], primarily as an aerospace engineering firm during the late imperial period. Its early contracts were with the [[Dripstanian Empire|imperial]] military, supplying aircraft and transport systems during the final decades of imperial rule.

### Republican Era and the Continental Divide (1954–1977)

The founding of the republic and the onset of the [[Continental Divide]] transformed Van Ritter from a military supplier into a strategic institution. The sustained proxy conflict with [[Confia]] and the creation of the [[ONDD]] drove rapid expansion of Susia's defense industrial base. Van Ritter was the primary beneficiary. The company expanded its product lines from aircraft into missiles, armored vehicles, military satellites, and electronic warfare systems during this period, and secured the federal contracts that would define its position for decades.

### Post-War and Global Cold War (1977–present)

The end of the [[Continental War]] in 1977 and the subsequent reorientation of Susian foreign policy toward the [[Ashgerad]] cold war shaped Van Ritter's post-war development. Investment shifted toward technologies with direct applicability to a peer adversary: advanced fighter systems, military satellite networks, cyber capabilities, and AI-powered threat detection developed in partnership with [[Troli Ustaras]]. Space nuclear propulsion research, conducted in partnership with [[Atompron]], began during this period as part of the broader push for strategic advantage over [[Ashgerad]].

The [[Susian Federal Government]] granted Van Ritter [[Yarnojte]] status in 2044, recognizing its strategic indispensability and establishing the [[Eastern Industrial Complex FEZ]] in [[Misocévia]] as its primary operating base. 

Following the revocation of [[POCOIL]]'s Yarnojte status in 2087 and the conversion of the Harioslaj Oil Zone in [[Postia]], Van Ritter leased the facility from the [[Postia|state of Postia]], establishing it as the [[Harioslaj Naval Station FEZ]], a naval test facility. POCOIL retains its offshore oil operations at the same site under separate arrangement.

## Operations

### Military Hardware

Van Ritter's core production lines cover fighter jets and bombers, military helicopters and transport aircraft, missiles and defense systems, armored vehicles, military satellites and surveillance systems, and electronic warfare systems. Naval vessel production, specifically nuclear-powered submarines, is conducted in partnership with [[Atompron]], which provides reactor systems.

In the civilian maritime sector, Van Ritter and [[Atompron]] jointly manufacture long-range commercial freight ships powered by thorium propulsion reactors. As of 2080, approximately 40% of [[Susia|Susian]] civil freight ships are nuclear-powered, the majority using reactors produced through this partnership. The arrangement draws on [[Atompron]]'s thorium fuel cycle expertise, commercialized since 2051, and Van Ritter's naval engineering and hull construction capacity.

### Aerospace

The company produces commercial satellites for communications, weather monitoring, and navigation, alongside launch vehicles, space infrastructure, and space station components. Space nuclear propulsion remains in research phase, conducted in partnership with [[Atompron]].

Van Ritter also manufactures civilian airliners and helicopters for the commercial market, placing it in direct competition with [[Nayotai]] in the aviation sector. Its flagship civilian aircraft is the **[[Jartes-class airliner]]**, a supersonic airliner named after Emperor [[Jartes II]]. The choice of name has generated periodic commentary in [[Susia]]: Jartes II is the emperor who disarmed the population, suppressed the [[Liberal Revolts]], and publicly executed [[Armadesh Versij]], and his name occupies a specific place in Susian civic memory as a byword for tyranny. Van Ritter has never publicly addressed the naming. The prevailing interpretation is that the association is deliberate: Jartes II was also the emperor who electrified [[Duolij]] and [[Neoveli]], built [[Soiteslaj]], and invested in monumental infrastructure on a continental scale. Two centuries of historical distance have complicated the straightforward condemnation, and a supersonic airliner marketed to the wealthy is perhaps not an accidental vehicle for that rehabilitation.

The Jartes class is not mass-produced. It operates primarily as a premium and first-class option on routes where carriers have configured the aircraft for high-end travel. Economy-class seats on Jartes-class flights are available at a significant premium over standard fares, and the configuration is used primarily on transcontinental routes where the speed advantage justifies the cost differential.

### Civilian Products

Beyond aviation, Van Ritter produces GPS systems, laser systems, and civilian communications infrastructure, competing with [[Nayotai]] in several of these markets. The civilian product lines are structurally distinct from the company's classified work and are developed and sold under separate commercial divisions, though they draw on the same underlying engineering and technology base as Van Ritter's defense products.

### Cybersecurity and Intelligence Technology

Van Ritter produces government cybersecurity systems, intelligence gathering technology, encryption and secure communications infrastructure, and counter-espionage systems. AI-powered threat detection is developed in partnership with [[Troli Ustaras]]. This product line places Van Ritter in a close operational relationship with the [[Frumentarze]], which uses Van Ritter technology as the backbone of its surveillance and signals intelligence infrastructure.

### Government Black Projects

Van Ritter is the primary contractor for several classified federal programs. Three have been partially documented through leaks, official disclosures, or discontinued status.

The **Presidential Continuity Program** (2077–2084) was contracted to Van Ritter. Its purpose was to maintain government function in the event of thermonuclear war through AI models of key government figures, constructed from speeches, decisions, writings, interviews, neural mapping, and brain scans. The program created models of the President, five key Senators, three Supreme Court justices, and the [[Secretariat of Efficiency|Secretariat]] Director. It was officially discontinued in 2084 on grounds of ethical concerns, though available evidence suggests technical limitations were the operative factor. The underlying data remains archived and Van Ritter retains the capability to restart the program.

**Project NIRVEV** (2072–present) is an AI system for prediction of terrorist attacks and crimes, contracted to Van Ritter. Autonomous pre-emptive action, including arrest before a crime is committed, is authorized under the program's mandate.

The existence and scope of additional black projects remains classified. The [[Krasnova Case (2068)|Krasnova Case]] involved a journalist investigating alleged illegal weapons testing by Van Ritter; she was subsequently detained by the [[Frumentarze]] and sentenced to forty years in federal prison on espionage charges.

### Corporate Espionage

Van Ritter and [[Troli Ustaras]] are the most active mutual espionage actors among the [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]]. The competition centers on two overlapping areas: AI systems, where both companies develop capabilities with both military and civilian applications, and federal contracts, where the two companies frequently bid against each other for the same government programs.

Corporate espionage between Yarnojtes is an open secret tolerated by the [[Susian Federal Government]] under an informal framework with established limits. No violence, no sabotage of vital infrastructure, and no public exposure are the operative constraints. Disputes are settled privately where possible. The [[Frumentarze]] monitors corporate espionage activity but prosecutes selectively, focusing on incompetent operations that generate public exposure rather than on the practice itself.

Within this framework, the Van Ritter and TU rivalry is the most sustained and technically sophisticated. Van Ritter's interest in TU is primarily defensive: TU's AI research, conducted partly under federal contract, overlaps with Van Ritter's own classified AI programs, and the two companies compete for the same pool of engineers. TU's interest in Van Ritter is primarily offensive: military AI systems and defense-adjacent technology represent a market TU cannot access through normal commercial channels, and Van Ritter's classified projects represent the most advanced applied AI work being done in [[Susia]] outside TU itself.

The dynamic produces a structural irony. The two companies are also mandated partners on several federal programs, required by contract to share certain technical outputs while simultaneously running active intelligence operations against each other. Their engineers sometimes collaborate on joint deliverables in the morning and work in facilities designed to prevent the other company's agents from accessing their research in the afternoon.

Van Ritter provides defense contracting and project management, military training and simulation, intelligence analysis, security consulting, and aerospace engineering services to the federal government and to [[ONDD]] allied states.

## Free Economic Zones

### Eastern Industrial Complex

> *Main article: [[Eastern Industrial Complex FEZ]]*

The [[Eastern Industrial Complex FEZ]] is Van Ritter's primary FEZ, located four hours from [[Lore-Kurji]] in [[Misocévia]]. It houses the company's defense contracting and classified project operations. The FEZ is organized around a clearance-based residential structure: housing is assigned by security clearance level, with physical barriers separating residential districts at different classification tiers. Entry requires a security checkpoint each morning. Personal communications devices are prohibited in work areas. The facility has a reputation for security paranoia and a culture that is hierarchical, patriotic in explicit display, and characterized by compartmentalization even among colleagues.

### Aerospecial Test Center

> *Main article: [[Aerospecial Test Center FEZ]]*

The [[Aerospecial Test Center FEZ]] is located in southern [[Troli]], isolated from population centers. It functions as Van Ritter's primary testing facility for aerospace systems and, based on available public information, some weapons systems. Access is among the most restricted of any Susian FEZ.

### Harioslaj Naval Station FEZ

> *Main article: [[Harioslaj Naval Station FEZ]]*

The [[Harioslaj Naval Station FEZ]] is located southwest of [[New Duloc]] in [[Postia]], established following the conversion of the former [[Harioslaj Oil Zones FEZ|POCOIL facility]] after 2082. It operates as a naval test station. [[POCOIL]] continues to operate offshore oil rigs at the same site under a separate arrangement with the state of [[Postia]].

## Employment Structure

### Security Clearance Hierarchy

All Van Ritter employees hold security clearance as a condition of employment. Clearance level determines project assignment, residential district within FEZs, approved social contacts, permitted travel destinations, salary band, and the degree of family monitoring to which the employee and their dependents are subject. The four tiers are as follows.

**Classified** (approximately 40% of workforce): Standard defense work. Employees know they are working on defense projects but not their specifics. Standard social restrictions and general employee housing.

**Secret** (approximately 35%): Advanced systems including stealth technology and new fighter programs. Restricted residential neighborhoods, prohibition on travel to certain countries, regular security reviews.

**Top Secret** (approximately 20%): Cutting-edge weapons, AI systems, and counter-intelligence work. Employees live in highly restricted compounds. Constant surveillance is accepted as a condition of residence. Family members are monitored. Travel outside [[Susia]] requires prior approval.

**Special Access** (approximately 5%): Black projects that officially do not exist. Employees live in sealed compounds with physical security perimeters. Extreme compartmentalization is standard: colleagues at this level frequently do not know what others in the same facility are working on. Family members are subject to continuous monitoring and face practical restrictions on movement. This tier carries the highest compensation and the most complete subordination of personal life to the employment relationship.

### Employment Requirements

All positions require Susian citizenship. Dual nationals are not eligible. Background checks cover family members and associates, not only the applicant. Psychological evaluation for loyalty and stability is mandatory. Physical fitness standards apply to many positions. A minimum of a bachelor's degree is required for technical roles.

## Corporate Culture

Van Ritter's internal culture is organized around a security-first institutional logic that permeates daily life within its FEZs. Entry checkpoints, bag searches, and continuous surveillance are standard. The workplace operates on a need-to-know basis: employees are not expected to inquire about colleagues' work and compartmentalization is enforced rather than merely encouraged. Social life defaults to coworkers because the restrictions on discussing work with outsiders make external socializing structurally limited.

The company's aesthetic is explicitly patriotic. Susian flags are displayed throughout facilities. National anthems and formal ceremonies mark significant dates. The cultural slang term [[Mejdel]] (literally "flag eater"), used across [[Susia]] to describe performed rather than practiced patriotism, is specifically associated with Van Ritter culture, reflecting a widespread perception that the company's patriotic display is more institutional performance than organic conviction.

The military rank structure of the [[Susian Federal Government|Susian military]] influences Van Ritter's civilian organizational hierarchy. The company treats hierarchy as functional rather than ceremonial: clearance level, not seniority or title, is the primary determinant of access and authority.

Van Ritter employees at higher clearance levels carry an awareness of three active adversarial threats: [[Ashgerad|Ashgeradn]] espionage, [[Troli Ustaras|TU]] corporate espionage targeting military AI and technology, and industrial espionage from other [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]]. The TU rivalry is treated in the [[#Corporate Espionage|corporate espionage section]]. The company's position on all three is that the awareness is not paranoia but an accurate assessment of its operational environment.

Cognitive enhancement adoption among Van Ritter employees reached approximately 75% by the 2070s. Enhancement is not formally required but the performance demands of high-clearance technical work create structural pressure toward adoption among employees at Secret level and above.

## Public Perception

Van Ritter occupies a stable but ambivalent position in Susian public culture. Supporters treat it as the institutional expression of the [[Fortress Complex]]: the company that stands between [[Susia]] and [[Ashgerad]], doing necessary work under necessary conditions. Critics describe it as a surveillance apparatus that has turned employment into a form of monitored captivity and whose black project portfolio operates outside meaningful democratic oversight. Both characterizations reflect accurate observations about different aspects of the same institution.

The [[Van Ritter Patriots]] [[Crolball|crolball]] team participates in the [[Susian National Crolball League]], one of three corporate-sponsored clubs in the league. The team's fanbase is largely concentrated among Van Ritter employees and their families, and it is regarded by the broader Susian public with the same mixture of respect and wariness that attaches to the company itself.
