The **Neoveli Stock Exchange** is the primary securities market of [[Susia]], based in [[Neoveli]], [[Dripia]]. Trading opens each day with a bell at 10:13, the minute at which [[Armadesh Versij]] was hanged in Neoveli in 1844.

### 1955 bombing

> *Main article: [[Neoveli Stock Exchange Bombing]]*

On 30/05/1955 a bomb attributed to the [[Nevirs]] detonated in the exchange at 10:33, twenty minutes after the opening bell, killing 43 people.

## Culture

The life of Neoveli is organized around the exchange's opening bell.
