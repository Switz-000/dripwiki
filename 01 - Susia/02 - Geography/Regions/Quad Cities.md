The **Quad Cities** is a region of three cities in the [[Susia|Susian]] states of [[Dripia]] and [[Misocévia]]: [[Niqueparje]], [[Lore-Kurji]] and [[Jaffnidia]]. These cities are located at the center of the [[Lasman river]] Valley. [[Lore-Kurji]] used to be two separate cities alongside [[Soiteslaj]] and [[Jaffnidia]], hence the name.
