**Daričoy** is a region of southern [[Dripia]], [[Susia]], occupying the inland plains between the [[Neoveli metropolitan area]] and the state capital [[New Kentu]]. Historically an area of small agricultural freeholds, it became the principal center of the Susian technology sector and is the location of the [[Susian Innovation Center]].

## Geography

Daričoy lies in the southern plains of [[Dripia]], inland from the coast. It is bounded to the southwest by the [[Neoveli metropolitan area]] and extends north toward [[New Kentu]]. The terrain is flat and was historically agricultural. The region has no formally defined administrative boundary and is delimited by common usage.

## History

### Name

The name derives from *daričir*, the [[Susian language|Susian]] word for legally held property, and the collective suffix *-oy*, giving a meaning of approximately "collective of properties." The term originally described the region's pattern of small agricultural freeholds.

### Agricultural period

During the [[imperial-era|imperial period]], Daričoy was homestead country, divided among small freeholders and the market towns that served the surrounding farmland. The region held little administrative or strategic significance and appears rarely in imperial records.

### Development of the technology sector

From the late twentieth century, technology companies established operations in Daričoy, drawn by land costs lower than those of [[Neoveli]], proximity to the universities of [[New Kentu]] including the [[Federal Institute of Technology]], and access to the financial sector of the [[Neoveli metropolitan area]]. [[Troli Ustaras]] relocated its primary research and development operations to the region in 2007. As the sector grew, agricultural land was sold and consolidated, and farming was largely displaced.

## Economy

> *Main article: [[Susian Innovation Center]]*

Daričoy is the principal center of the Susian technology sector. The [[Susian Innovation Center]], operated by [[Troli Ustaras]], is the largest concentration of activity in the region and functions as the company's global headquarters.

Most settlements in Daričoy are ordinary municipalities hosting corporate offices and campuses. The Susian Innovation Center is the only [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ]] in the region and the only part of it under corporate rather than state administration.
