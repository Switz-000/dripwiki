The Silver Coast is the metropolitan region on the southwestern coast of [[Dripia]], along the [[Sea of Galil]]. It is the urbanized core of the state's southern plains, which are flat, densely settled, and open northward toward the interior. The region takes its name from the stretch of Dripian shoreline it occupies.

[[Neoveli]] sits at its center, built overlooking the water, and is the largest city and financial capital. [[Arkaime]] lies to the east at the seaward edge of the region, a major port at the head of the bay, connected across the Sea of Galil to [[Duolij]] on the [[Postia|Postian]] shore by the [[Arkaime Bridge]]. [[Poremoj]] lies just north of Neoveli as a residential suburb. Together with the smaller surrounding settlements, these cities form a continuous built-up area along the coast.

Inland to the north, the plains rise gradually into the agricultural corridor that runs toward the [[Susian Innovation Center]] and, beyond it, the state capital of [[New Kentu]]. To the southeast, the coast continues toward the mouth of the [[Lasman River]] and the border town of [[Jaffnidia]].
