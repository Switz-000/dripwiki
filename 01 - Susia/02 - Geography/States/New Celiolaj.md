**New Celiolaj** is a state in northern [[Susia]], bordered by [[Nessel]] to the west, [[Dripia]] and [[Misocévia]] to the south, [[Drippalia]] to the east and the [[Sea of Galil]] to the north. Its capital and largest city is [[Unarete]]. The state is the former Tekur Territory, into which the Tekur population of the [[Kingdom of Tekuria]] was deported in the [[March Against the Sun]], and [[Tekur Susians]] make up approximately 40% of its population.

## Geography

New Celiolaj has boreal and arctic conditions, with tundra and a sparse population. Its population was 5.5 million in 2080.

## History

### Imperial era

At the outbreak of the [[Dripstanian Civil War]] in 1815 the dukes of New Celiolaj joined [[Jartes I|Jartes]], recruiting their subjects on the promise of land at the end of the war.

### Tekur Territory

> *Main article: [[March Against the Sun]]*

After the war Jartes I deported the Tekur population of the Kingdom of Tekuria to the Tekur Territory, in the region that became New Celiolaj. The majority of the deportees died on the march. Under the empire the region was administered as a territory rather than as a province.

### Republic

At the founding of the republic in 1954 the state's governors opposed citizenship for the Tekurs. The accession of New Celiolaj and Nessel was secured by excluding Tekurs from Susian citizenship, which they received in 1997 under the [[Reconciliation Act]].

## Free Economic Zones

[[Atompron]] operates a [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ]] in New Celiolaj.

## Culture

[[Tekur Heritage Month]] is observed in the state. Regional foods and drinks include frozen pine tea, a traditional Tekur drink brewed from pine needles, and elk jerky; seal oil is used in place of butter in both New Celiolaj and Nessel. These are listed in [[List of susian foods and drinks]].

## Politics

New Celiolaj's governors have a more direct style of government than those of other states, a legacy of the state's administration as an imperial territory. [[List of political positions#Tekur Separatism|Tekur separatism]] is present in the state's regional politics without significant national representation.
