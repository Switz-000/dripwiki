**Nessel** is a state in northern [[Susia]], bordered by the [[Sea of Galil]] to the north and west, [[New Celiolaj]] to the east and [[Dripia]] to the south. Its capital and largest city is [[Kloshijie]]. It is the only Susian state in which [[Doremojism]] rather than [[Reformed Armotism]] is the majority religion, and it held greater autonomy under the empire than the other provinces.

## Geography

Nessel has boreal and arctic conditions, with plains and tundra and a sparse population. Its population was 8.3 million in 2080.

## History

### Imperial era

At the outbreak of the [[Dripstanian Civil War]] in 1815 the dukes of Nessel joined [[Jartes I|Jartes]], recruiting their subjects on the promise of land at the end of the war.

### Liberal Revolts

> *Main article: [[Liberal Revolts]]*

During the Liberal Revolts of 1840 to 1844, Nessel's Doremojists fought alongside the provinces as the [[Doremojian League]]. They sought greater autonomy from the empire and feared being "Konphed", destroyed as a people as the [[Konph]] had been, a fear sharpened by the empire's treatment of the Tekurs in the [[March Against the Sun]] and by the memory of the [[Dripstanian Wars of Religion]] under [[Emperor Agamilos|Agamilos]].

### Late empire

Nessel held greater autonomy within the empire than the other provinces. In the 1900s it could act in its internal affairs in ways that provinces such as Dripia and [[Postia]] could not.

### Republic

At the founding of the republic in 1954, Nessel's Doremojist majority opposed citizenship for the Tekurs. The accession of Nessel and New Celiolaj was secured by excluding [[Tekur Susians|Tekurs]] from Susian citizenship, which they received only in 1997.

## Culture

### Religion

Doremojism is the majority religion of Nessel. Nesselian Doremojists are West Gaiyanese, and their religious practice and customs differ markedly from those of the [[Armotism|Armotists]] of the rest of the country. Nessel's [[Sorzenko|Sorzenkos]] operate within this religious culture, which gives them a character distinct from those of the Armotist mainstream.

### Alcohol

The retail sale of alcohol has long been prohibited in Nessel. Possession and consumption are legal. Residents may buy alcohol outside the state, bring it into Nessel and transfer it to one another, because the [[Susian Constitution]] protects both imports and the transfer of property between private individuals; the prohibition reaches only commercial sale within the state. Drinking is not regarded as a social problem in Nessel.

Alcohol carries ritual significance in Nesselian custom. Nesselian teenagers mark their coming of age with a celebration that ends with the participants drinking winter's friend, a homemade alcohol.

## Politics

Nessel is characterized by conservatism, religiosity and skepticism of [[Free Economic Zone|FEZs]] and [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]], and has no FEZ. With rural [[Vitrika]] it is the strongest base of traditionalism in Susia, and it is one of the states from which the [[Coalition of State's Rights]] draws its strongest conservative support.
