- State Capital, university town, research, education hub
- Home to four major universities: [[Dripia State University]], [[Federal Institute of Technology]], [[University of New Kentu]] and [[Belluni College]]. Intense rivalry between the four.
- Famous for Political Radicalism (all ideologies), [[Academic Blend Coffee]], College [[Crolball]], the [[Susian National Library]]
- The Stereotype of a New Kentuan: Always trying to debate, caffeine addict, well versed in philosophy and politics, has two PHDs yet doesn't know how to change a tire.
