- Major port on the [[Sea of Galil]], connected to [[Duolij]] by the [[Arkaime Bridge]] (engineering marvel, built 1993 by [[Nayotai]]. It's the largest bridge in the world with 47 km). 
- Technically in [[Dripia]], functionally a satellite of both [[Neoveli]] and Duolij. "The city with two mothers" but neither wants to claim it fully. 
- The city is famous for its tradition of Oyster cultivation even having an oyster festival with shucking competitions.
