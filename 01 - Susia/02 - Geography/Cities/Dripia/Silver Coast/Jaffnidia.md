Border town, [[Dripia|Dripian]]-[[Postia|Postian]] border. Built around the still standing historical fort located in the mouth of the [[Lasman River]]. [[Satratonie]]'s betrayal started here, there’s a large museum dedicated to the [[Liberal Revolts]]. Symbolic important, where liberty died (temporarily). "Satratonie's Wall" (where he first met the countess) gets constantly covered in graffiti curses; the city has to clean it every week or so.

## Geography

Jaffnidia lies southeast of [[Neoveli]], at the mouth of the Lasman River where it empties into the Sea of Galil. It stands on the border between Dripia and Postia, which the Lasman forms along this stretch.
