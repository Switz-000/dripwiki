**Soiteslaj** (previously known as **St. Mantichev city**) is the capital of [[Susia]] and is inside the [[Neutral District]].

# History

The location of St. Mantichev city was personally chosen by [[Jartes II]], when he put his sword into the ground and announced “here shall be a town”. The city was built to comemorate the Empire's 200th anniversary in 1874 and designed to be the new administrative hub. It is characterized by its wide straight boulevards, parks and monuments. Visitors would often feel small by the tall, gilded buildings. 

The city also included: The Incria Palace, an exhibition showcasing the Empire’s colonies abroad. The Bicentennial Square, a large triumphal arch with statues and an extensive park complex. The royal opera house, a 2000 seat building with lavish ornamentation, a marble staircase and a ceiling painted with depictions of the Armotist Voyages.

After the dissolution of the empire, St. Mantichev continued as the capital of [[Susia]], seating the president, senate and the supreme court.

In 1978, president [[Čalsor Boňár]] renamed the city to Soiteslaj, in honor of [[Suizo Soites]], the republic's first president.
