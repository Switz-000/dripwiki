## Geography

Duolij lies across the water on the [[Postia|Postian]] shore, southwest of [[Jaffnidia]] and south of the border. It is connected to [[Arkaime]], on the Dripian side, by the[[ Arkaime Bridge]] across the [[Sea of Galil]].
