**Nikuria** is a city in [[Postia]], [[Susia]], with a population of 349,000 as of 2080.

## History

A battle of the [[Liberal Revolts]] was fought at Nikuria.

%% TODO: the battle's name, date, combatants and outcome are unestablished. Per [[Liberal Revolts]], it was not a decisive engagement. %%

On 30/09/1957, during governor [[Filevir Matri|Matri's]] [[Versij Day]] address in the city, a man drew a pistol on the governor and was disarmed by the crowd before firing, in the [[Matri assassination attempt (1957)|second of three attempts]] on Matri's life.

In 1958, Matri announced the construction of a highway connecting [[Duolij]] and Nikuria, at the event where [[Urgiri Tečlan]] shot him.
