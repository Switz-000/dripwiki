Named after the nickname of a famous [[Cericeiro]], “Yatovar”, meaning “Wanderer of the Plains”. Has a sizable confian community, due to the proximity to the confian border, most of which arrived as political refugees during the [[Continental Divide]]. Famous for being one of the most patriotic cities in [[Susia]], flags in every building, Cericeiro monuments everywhere.
