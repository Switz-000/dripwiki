The **Soites Federal Free Trade Zone** is a [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ]] operated by the [[Soites Group]] in [[Postia]], located two hours south of [[Duolij]]
## The core concept:

- No sales taxes within Soites FFTZ: Get an instant 8% rebate at the register (Soites eats the cost, worth it for the foot traffic)
- 15/15/15 rule: Everything within a 15-minute walk, 15-minute delivery of goods within the FEZ and a bullet train arrives every 15 minutes 
- The central cafeteria: a 5,000 seat mega-cafeteria where executives, retail workers, tourists, experimenters and families can all have lunch together. Same food, same space. Allows from class mixing, something rare in most Yarnotjes.
- Soites as [[Susia]]’s mall: Everyone from [[Duolij]] families shopping for week’s groceries to [[Neoveli]]businessmen looking for a break from work. [[Soites Group|Soites]] offers everything, everywhere for everyone. Shopping here costs 20% to 30% less than independent Susia. Before, Soites was “just a corporate zone” now, specially with the [[Kolkov line]], Soites became a part of everyday life for many susians, just a place you go to the weekend, or where you go to get groceries.  
- Kolkov line Boom: Before, the Soites FFTZ was just another vacation spot, now it's a regional shopping center the scale of a city. What was a 2 hour drive for many Duolijians, now is a 20min bullet train ride. Neovelians arrive in one hour instead of five. Business tripled, every [[Postia|Postian]] and [[Dripia|Dripian]] has been to the Soites FFTZ at least once in their lives.
