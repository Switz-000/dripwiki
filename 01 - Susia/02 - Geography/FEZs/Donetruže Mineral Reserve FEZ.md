The **Donetruže Mineral Reserve** was an [[FEZ]] operated by [[Vandernij & Milier|V&M]] located three hours west of [[Donetruže]], [[Troli]]. The zone was reverted back to the state after the revocation of V&M's [[Yarnojte]] status in 2083.
