The **Harioslaj Oil Zone** is a [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ]] operated by [[POCOIL]] untill the revogation of its [[Yarnojte]] status in 2087, since 2088 the FEZ is being operated by [[Van Ritter]] under the name Harioslaj Naval Station FEZ. The zone is located in [[Postia]], one hour north of [[Noňofriye]] and three hours south of [[New Duloc]].
