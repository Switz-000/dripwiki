The **Kolkov line** is a [[Susia|Susian]] maglev line between [[New Duloc]] and [[New Kentu]], connecting the major cities of [[Postia]] and [[Dripia]]. It opened in 2061 and is operated by [[Galil Ňavorasar]] (GŇ). The line is named after the philosopher [[Yário Kolkov]] and is known among frequent riders as *Rǎzuraz* or *RR*, meaning "New to New", a reference to its two terminal stations.

## Route

The line serves seven stations: [[New Duloc]], [[Soites Federal Free Trade Zone|Soites FFTZ]], [[Duolij]], [[Arkaime]], [[Neoveli]], the [[Susian Innovation Center]], and [[New Kentu]]. Trains operate at a maximum speed of 603 km/h. Express services run between New Duloc, the Soites FFTZ, Duolij, Neoveli, and New Kentu; local services call at all stations. The alignment crosses the [[Lasman river]] at the Postia–Dripia border near [[Jaffnidia]], which has no station.

The Soites FFTZ station was built by [[Soites Group]], which owns it. The station connects directly to the interior of the trade zone.

## History

> *Main article: [[Nayotai]]*

Construction began in the late 2050s under [[Nayotai]] as primary contractor, in one of the largest federal contracts of the post-transition period. Nayotai built the guideway and manufactured the trainsets. [[Troli Ustaras]] privately opposed the project and declined to bid on its train control systems; Nayotai developed the control and signaling systems internally, adapting its autonomous vehicle technology.

The line opened in 2061 with three tracks, two running tracks and a third bidirectional track used for peak-direction express services. A second construction phase, financed through an additional bond series, expanded the line to four tracks in 2070, separating express and local services.

## Funding

> *Main article: [[Federal Reserve Unit]]*

Construction was financed through a mixed structure. Bonds denominated in the [[Federal Reserve Unit]], underwritten by [[Silver Coast Bank]] and sold to institutional investors, provided 38% of construction capital. The [[Susian Federal Government]] contributed 20%, the states of [[Postia]] and [[Dripia]] 10% each, GŇ 14% in equity, and [[Soites Group]] 8%. The bond issues are among the largest Federal Reserve Unit instruments in circulation.

## Operations

The line is operated by [[Galil Ňavorasar]], a private railway company descended from the imperial railway system privatized after the [[Continental War]]. GŇ operates other rail services in addition to the Kolkov line. The line draws its electricity from the [[Atompron]] grid.

Rides are free for holders of a [[SoitesCard]] under an agreement between [[Soites Group]] and GŇ. Riders without the card pay standard fares.

## In popular culture

The names *Rǎzuraz* and *RR* are used primarily by frequent riders, particularly commuters and students. Occasional riders generally use the official name.
