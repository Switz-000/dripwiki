The **Kolkov line** is a [[Susia|Susian]] maglev line between [[New Duloc]] and [[New Kentu]]. It is coloqually known as *"Rǎzuraz"* or simply *RR*, literally meaning "New to New", a reference to the northernmost and southernmost stations. Other stops include: [[Soites Federal Free Trade Zone|Soites FFTZ]], [[Duolij]], the [[Susian Innovation Center|SIC]], [[Neoveli]]. The Kolkov line operates eight tracks, with a maximum operating speed of 603 km/h.

The project was a conjoint state backed initiative by [[Postia]] and [[Dripia]], with part of the funding provided by the [[Susian Federal Government|Federal Goverment]]. Many companies also funded the project, the most notable of which was the [[Soites Group]].
