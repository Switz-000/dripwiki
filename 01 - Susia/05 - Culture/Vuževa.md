**Vulre ro žaveža** (literally "silicon dreamers"), commonly shortened to **Vuževa**, is a [[Susia|Susian]] term for a person who has withdrawn from physical social life into digital activity. It denotes both the individuals and the phenomenon as a whole. Estimates place over two million Susians in the category, approximately 0.8 percent of the population as of 2080, concentrated in the major cities.

## Origins

The full form combines *vulre*, to dream, and *žaveža*, silicon, in the relational construction *ro*. Both forms are recorded in the [[Susian lexicon by root|lexicon]] and in the [[List of susian idioms]].

The two forms carry different registers. The full form is clinical and dismissive and functions in use as a diagnosis rather than as an insult. The shortened form originated in the digital spaces the term describes, is used within the group itself, and carries a warmer sense.

## Digital activities

The activities associated with the term are gaming, social media, neuroexperiences, and simulated interaction through commercial artificial intelligence services. The virtual spaces in which these activities take place are mediated predominantly by [[Troli Ustaras]] hardware and platforms.

## Social media

Susian social media has played a central role in shaping the identity of the Vuževas, turning it into a recognizable online subculture with its own vocabulary and self-description. [[Lizne]], the messaging and social platform owned by [[Soites Group]], is the principal platform associated with the group.

## Treatment

Specialized clinics treating digital withdrawal issue patients a slab of obsidian cut to the dimensions of a smartphone, to be held in place of the device. Reported results have been positive.

## Contemporary relevance

The term is used in Susian public discussion as shorthand for anxieties about citizens who opt out of civic life. The proportion of the population it describes is larger than the proportion resident inside a [[Free Economic Zone]], which stood at approximately 0.25 percent in 2080.
