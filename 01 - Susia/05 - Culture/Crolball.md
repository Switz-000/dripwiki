**Crolball** is the most popular sport in [[Susia]] and is played across much of the [[Dripstan]] continent, with active followings in [[Confia]], [[Zaphonia]], [[Pierej]], and [[Kamino]]. The game originated during the imperial era and is organized domestically through the [[Susian National Crolball League]].

## Rules

Crolball is played between two teams of eight players. Players may pass, dribble, or kick the ball. A player in possession may not hold the ball in their hands for more than five seconds. Outside of the Breči period, contact between players is restricted, and aggressive physical interactions result in a penalty play.

Scoring requires a team to carry the ball into the opposing team's dead zone and retain possession there for the duration of a thirty-second countdown. This countdown is called **Breči**. During Breči, all forms of physical interaction between players are permitted, including those prohibited in normal play. The Breči is the central tactical and physical event of any given match: possession entering it is contested at full contact, and its outcome determines whether a score is recorded.

Protective equipment is available but not mandatory. Its use is strongly recommended given the nature of Breči-phase play.

## The Susian National Crolball League

> *Main article: [[Susian National Crolball League]]*

The [[Susian National Crolball League]] (SNCL) organizes professional crolball competition within Susia. Teams are based by city and state. Most clubs have roots in local civic and working-class identity; corporate-sponsored teams are a minority and have had a mixed record.

### Corporate teams

Three [[Yarnojte|Yarnojte]]-sponsored teams currently compete in the SNCL. Corporate-sponsored clubs are a distinct minority in the league and occupy an ambivalent position in Susian sports culture: they attract audiences connected to their parent companies but generally lack the civic roots that sustain the most popular clubs.

| Team | Sponsor |

Two additional corporate sponsorship attempts failed without establishing a lasting presence. [[Soites Group]] operated a team from 2066 to 2071; it folded after failing to develop an authentic fanbase and was widely criticized for its promotional character. [[Vandernij & Milier]] operated a team from 2078 to 2080 before its dissolution, with attendance constrained by its workforce's working conditions and the company's broader financial deterioration at the time.

## College crolball

College crolball is played at Susian universities and is particularly prominent in [[New Kentu]], where it forms part of the city's broader student culture alongside academic and political life.
