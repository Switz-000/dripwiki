Today, the sun decided to scrape my scales. It has made my limbs numb, my back red and my eyes swollen. Worst of all, my socks are wet and my feet itch.

How could I describe the state I am in as anything but one of the worst conceivable? If men and fish were made separate, what must I have done to become like one? I could swim the world as I am, but I hear duty calling inland. 

To take a fish of the sea is one arduous step, many dedicate their lives to search my home for food to bring. Yet, I have been summoned by one of the landsmen to stand before a room of many. How can they summon me, presuming I know the subject of these discussions?

As I checked my pockets, I found a letter, folded exactly six times.  It was obvious that the man who printed this couldn't even fold a seventh time. He lacks any authority, unable to impose his will upon this mere paper.

```
Techno-Federative Republic of Susia
State of Orlítia, Office of the Clerk

Welcome! You have been chosen from a random selection of citizens in your municipality to serve as a potential volunteer in this year's State Sorzenko Congregation event.

Within fifteen (15) days of reciving, this automatic electronic mail, access the State of Orlítia official website at: ttj.orlitia.su, select 'Sorzenko', then click on 'Orlítia 2082 Congregation event', answer the questions honestly and follow the instructions on the site.

For any questions, call the State Information Line +01 (32) 5719-9499

DO NOT APPEAR AT YOUR CITY COUNCIL UNTIL SUMMONED AND SPECIFICALLY INSTRUCTED TO DO SO!
```

I was in complete awe. The republic itself drafted a letter to me. What wicked offense have I done to be officially called by the state to stand before a trial? And they asked me to dial? How can I even defend myself over the phone or through the internet? I judged the man who printed this letter too harshly, I was at war with him and now, its his turn to judge me.
