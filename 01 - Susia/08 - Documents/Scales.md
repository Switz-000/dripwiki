Today, the sun decided to scrape my scales.

It has made my limbs numb, my back red and my eyes swollen. Worst of all, my socks are wet and my feet itch.

How could I describe the state I am in as anything but one of the worst conceivable?

If men and fish were made separate, what must I have done to become like one? 

I could swim the world as I am, but I hear duty calling inland. 


To take a fish of the sea is one arduous step, many dedicate their lives to search my home for food to bring.

Yet, I have been summoned by one of the landmen to stand before a room of many.

How can they summon me, presuming I know the subject of these discussions?
