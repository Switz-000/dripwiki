# Format

This year book is traditionally published by the [[Susian Naval Academy]], with each graduating cadet getting a spread with a formal portrait in dress uniform, a hand-drawn caricature by a classmate, and a semi-affectionate roast written in the first person plural by his friends.

# Text

## Hervan Naveo

I'd almost say a character like this one can make an impact on even the most aggressive girls. Of course, we owe it all to his adolescent, autumnal look, which served him as more than one argument for reaching the summit.

During his stay in this place, remembered only by the mosquitoes, he enjoyed the prestige of "Maznasor", and it was basically all down to his eternally good sense of what was coming, whether finals, women or rain.

That was an effect that would later lead him to occupy the place he currently inhabits, 2nd floor, 5th. Of course, it wasn't all work and effort... I'd be in a tight spot if anyone asked me: what became of his three previous years?

The record of his passage through time leaves us a trail of memories such as "no smoking in first year", given the severe sanctions a cadet accountant can impose, "I must not act like a double dry-point compass" because the result is that it grips very little, and above all, look for his girls as close by as possible, to avoid having to tell his comrades one thing for another, so as not to reveal his greatest weakness... (Česeda)

That last part always served Matev and Bach for putting him fist in the dock at "Controversy in the Train Car"

In his second consecutive year, he was seen inhabiting a very mechanical place, THE WORKSHOP! and as was logical to expect, he took advantage of the free-study period and its hours... to go play [[Crolball]] with the aforementioned.

But let's move on to a more agreeable aspect, what did you do on tuesday and thursday nights? There was no problem finding him, everyone knew the cinema, a favorite spot along with Matev and Bach, was his during the screenings.

There was a moment in '81 that marked, for his character, a milestone in his professional life: he embarked on a submarine. The "SNS Neoveli" was a foretaste of his aspirations to become a submariner.

My dear friend: I have only one more thing to say to you: WHEN ARE YOU GOING TO WRITE MY BIOGRAPHY?!?!?!

Best of luck (though you've got a surplus), 
Matev and Bach

## Matev Serpis

We were given the difficult mission of writing the biography of a difficult character, because nobody knows Metev "Telch" Serpis, not even his own classmates!

Only his three cronies, Maznasor, Bach and the old Sivori, gave us any information about the life this specimen led here at the school.

From the far lands of [[St. Yepodij]], where he was simply known as "Farmer drought", and using a sack for a parachute and a mask for googles he dropped onto the parade ground of this institute back in 1978.

From that unexpected and incredible moment of his entry, until he was seen wearing two stripes on his name tag, nobody, not even his family, had any news of him.
