| "Versij died for this?"                        | Expression of disappointment, invoked at mediocrity, pettiness, or institutional failure.                      | Ironic and exasperated.                                                                           | Invokes [[Armadesh Versij]]'s 1844 execution. | "Pulling a [[Kaichet Satratonie\|Satratonie]]" | Betraying one's principles for money or status.                                                                | Damning. There is no rehabilitative reading in common use.                                        | Originated from [[Jartes II]]'s successful bribery of Satratonie during the [[Liberal Revolts]]. The idiom preserves the public account of the defection while omitting the personal motive in the historical record. |
| "New Kentu radical"                            | A person who is impractically ideological; theory unburdened by consequence.                                   | Dismissive, anti-intellectual in flavor. The type is old enough to have its own satirical literature.                                         |
| "It's misocévian good"                         | Mediocre; acceptable and nothing more.                                                                         | Mildly condescending toward [[Misocévia]]. Misocévians themselves use it flatly, without offense. | Contrasts implicitly with FEZ standards.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| "Bridge whisky taste"                          | Minor differences that people treat as major distinctions.                                                     | Deflating. Used to end arguments, not start them.                                                 | Refers to the [[Lore-Kurji]] distillery rivalry: the two cities compete fiercely on flavor, and outsiders cannot tell the products apart.                                                                             |
| "I'll see you in Velúria"                      | A threat to blow the whistle, addressed by an employee to a corporate superior.                                | Defiant. Half threat, half resignation, since saying it usually means the career is already over. | [[Velúria]]'s ID system and [[Velúria Archives]] make it the standing refuge for corporate whistleblowers, which is why the [[Yarnojte\|Yarnojtes]] despise the city.                                                 |
| "Fresh start, sold parts"                      | A Velúrian saying on the price of reinvention: the new identity is bought with what was liquidated to fund it. | Gallows humor.
