The **Dripstanian calendar** is the calendar of the [[Dripstanian Empire]]. It divides the year into ten months that alternate between 36 and 37 days, for a common year of 365 days, and adds a leap day to the last month every fourth year. Years are counted forward from year 0 and backwards before it.

## Months

The year opens with Teosary. Odd-numbered months have 36 days and even-numbered months have 37.

| No. | Month        | Root      | Days                  |

|   1 | Teosary      | Teosio    | 36                    |
|   2 | Olody        | Olod      | 37                    |

|  10 | Vereny       | Verene    | 37, or 38 in a leap year |

## Years

Years are numbered forward from year 0. Years before year 0 are counted backwards and carry the suffix AS: the year before year 0 is 1 AS, and the tenth year before it is 10 AS.

## Leap years

Every fourth year is a leap year, in which Vereny gains a 38th day. Leap years fall on years 4, 8, 12 and onward, and on 4 AS, 8 AS, 12 AS and onward before year 0. Year 0 is not a leap year.

The leap day is marked by a religious festival.

## Contemporary use

After the dissolution of the [[Dripstanian Empire]], [[Susia]] renamed the eighth month from Jartean to Versijean. The other nine months keep their imperial names.
