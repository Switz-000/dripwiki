**Cognitive enhancement** refers to a set of medical technologies and procedures developed in [[Susia]] that modify neural pathways to improve cognitive function, including memory, focus, learning capacity, and specific skill acquisition. First developed in the 2030s through a partnership between the [[Soites Group]] and [[Troli Ustaras]] (TU), cognitive enhancement became commercially available in the 2060s and has since become one of the most significant technological and social developments in Susian society.

As of the 2080s, approximately 17% of the Susian population has undergone cognitive enhancement procedures, though adoption rates vary significantly across [[Free Economic Zone|Free Economic Zones]](FEZs), ranging from 98% in the [[Susian Innovation Center]] to 16% in independent Susia.

## Development History

### Early Research (2032-2038)

The Soites Group had developed predictive models of human behavior through its [[Soites Experimenter Program|Experimenter Program]], a psychological research initiative that collected behavioral data from thousands of participants. This research revealed patterns in cognitive decision-making and raised the possibility of behavioral modification through technological intervention.

In 2034, the Soites Group partnered with Troli Ustaras, which contributed expertise in artificial intelligence systems, computing infrastructure, and hardware engineering. Between 2035 and 2037, early prototypes were tested on willing participants from the Soites Experimenter Program who received additional compensation and signed non-disclosure agreements. These early tests showed promising results but were characterized by crude implementations and common side effects including headaches, mood disturbances, and occasional seizures.

Development during this period faced significant constraints from Susia's pre-transition regulatory environment, which imposed strict oversight on experimental medical procedures.
### Federal Program Era (2040-2048)

Following [[The Great Transition]] and the establishment of the [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ system]], the federal government launched the [[Federal Cognitive Enhancement Program]] in 2040. The new regulatory framework provided FEZs with greater flexibility and allowed the federal government to expedite research under national security justifications related to the Cold War. Ethics oversight, while still present, operated under a streamlined process.

The period from 2040 to 2048 became known as the "Disaster Years" due to significant safety failures. Between 2040 and 2046, the program resulted in nine deaths during or shortly after enhancement procedures. Three deaths were attributed to intraoperative complications such as hemorrhage and stroke, four to post-surgical infections or seizures, and two to implant malfunctions causing cascading neural failure. Additionally, 23 individuals suffered permanent cognitive damage, and 63 experienced moderate injuries requiring extended recovery.

Families of deceased and injured participants received incomplete compensation and were pressured to sign non-disclosure agreements. The first death occurred in 2041 when a participant experienced a fatal seizure during the procedure; the family received 500,000 Drips and signed an NDA under pressure.

In 2047, separate from but related to the enhancement program, Troli Ustaras was implicated in a [[Troli Ustaras Corruption Scandal (2047)|corruption scandal]] involving an attempted bribe of a secretariat member, resulting in the temporary loss of its [[Yarnojte]] status and the suspension of the enhancement program.

### Public Exposure and Reform (2049-2051)

In 2049, families of three deceased participants simultaneously broke their non-disclosure agreements and coordinated with journalists to expose the existence and safety failures of the secret federal enhancement program. The leak coincided with the TU corruption scandal, creating a major crisis of public trust. Congressional hearings, an investigation by the [[Federal Authority of Commerce and Competition]] (AFCC), and a [[Secretariat of Efficiency]] review followed, ultimately leading to the termination of the federal program.

### Commercial Development (2051-2061)

Following the collapse of the federal program, [[Troli Ustaras]] underwent significant restructuring in 2051, including the appointment of new executive leadership and the adoption of a new corporate vision. The company committed to continuing cognitive enhancement research publicly and transparently, with enhanced safety protocols, and limited to medical applications only.

From 2051 to 2057, TU conducted public clinical trials with rigorous safety testing. The company achieved a safety record with no deaths over six years, demonstrating that the underlying technology could be implemented safely. In 2057, cognitive enhancement procedures received federal approval for treatment of severe post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), limited to approximately 3,000 procedures annually. In 2058, approvals expanded to include severe depression, anxiety disorders, and borderline personality disorder, with annual procedure limits increased to 20,000.

During this period, a growing black market emerged as individuals sought enhancement procedures for their cognitive benefits, including improved focus and memory, by obtaining false diagnoses of approved conditions.

### Cognitive Rights Act (2061)

The [[Cognitive Rights Act of 2061]] fundamentally transformed the legal and social landscape of cognitive enhancement in Susia. The legislation established several key principles:

**Core Rights:**
- Right to cognitive autonomy (protection from forced enhancement)
- Right to cognitive privacy (protection of mental contents and neural data)
- Right to cognitive mobility (ability to move between enhanced and unenhanced communities)
- Anti-discrimination provisions prohibiting differential treatment based on enhancement status in employment, housing, and insurance

**Procedural Requirements:**
- Minimum age of 25 for enhancement procedures
- Comprehensive informed consent process
- Three-month waiting period before procedures
- Right to cancel appointments at any time
- Federally funded counseling services

**Community Protections:**
- Creation of [[Sanctuary Zone|Sanctuary Zones]], designated communities with unenhanced-majority populations
- One quarter Rule limiting Sanctuary Zone designations to 25% of a state's counties
- Prohibition on state-level bans of enhancement clinics or procedures

The Act represented an attempt to manage the rapid adoption of enhancement technology while protecting both individual autonomy and community cohesion.

### Enhancement Revolution (2060s-2090s)

Following the passage of the Cognitive Rights Act, enhancement technology underwent rapid advancement and widespread adoption. By the 2070s, first-wave enhancement procedures offered improved focus, accelerated learning, basic skill conditioning, and trauma therapy. By the 2090s, mature enhancement technology enabled targeted memory editing, personality trait optimization, and complex skill acquisition. Experimental research into consciousness transfer and collective minds continued but had not achieved practical implementation by 2110.

## Technology and Procedures

Cognitive enhancement involves the surgical implantation of neural interfaces developed by Troli Ustaras, combined with biological compatibility pharmaceuticals and medical procedures administered by [[Lischev-Verene]]. The two companies maintain a mandated partnership under federal regulation requiring cooperation on enhancement hardware and procedures or forfeiture of military contracts.

Enhancement procedures modify neural pathways to improve specific cognitive functions. The exact mechanisms remain proprietary, but the technology involves direct interface with brain tissue through implanted devices that interact with neural activity.

## Medical Effects and Side Effects

### Efficacy

Enhancement procedures have demonstrated measurable improvements in cognitive function, including:

- Increased processing speed and focus
- Enhanced memory formation and recall
- Accelerated learning and skill acquisition
- Reduction in symptoms of specified mental health conditions (when used therapeutically)

### Common Side Effects

Side effects vary significantly between individuals and across different generations of enhancement technology. Approximately 10% of enhanced individuals experience common side effects, which are typically temporary but recurring:

**First Generation (2051-2061):**

- Emotional numbness or flattening
- Depersonalization
- Reduced libido
- Sedation or lethargy
- Diminished empathy
- Suppression of basic drives (hunger, thirst, sleep)

**Second Generation (2061-2090s):**

- Overwhelming or racing thoughts
- Insomnia or disrupted sleep patterns
- Anxiety, particularly performance anxiety
- Emotional detachment
- Loss of "routine attention" (difficulty with mundane tasks)

Enhanced individuals are commonly prescribed sedatives to manage anxiety and insomnia. Stimulants are also popular among the enhanced population.

### Rare Side Effects

Approximately 1% of patients experience rare but more severe side effects, including:

- Manic episodes
- Hyperactivity or inability to rest
- Vivid nightmares
- Existential crisis or philosophical distress
- Suicidal ideation
- Appetite suppression

### Observable Markers

Enhanced individuals may display certain physical and behavioral markers:

- Faster speech patterns
- Reduced blinking frequency
- Blank or distant gaze
- Diminished display of strong emotions

### Historical Safety Record

The early federal program (2040-2046) resulted in 9 deaths, 23 cases of permanent cognitive damage, and 63 moderate injuries. Since the implementation of enhanced safety protocols in 2051, the safety record has improved significantly, with no deaths reported during the commercial development phase (2051-2057).

## Long-Term Effects

The long-term effects of cognitive enhancement remain a subject of ongoing research and debate. Independent research suggests potential long-term effects on personality, mood, emotional attachment, and sense of self. Troli Ustaras contests these findings, while Lischev-Verene has suggested that any long-term effects may be reversible, though scientific consensus has not been reached on either position.

The most extensive data on long-term effects comes from early prototype testers in the 2030s, particularly participants in the Soites Experimenter Program. However, this data is considered methodologically limited, as these individuals lived under significantly different circumstances than current enhanced individuals, and the research was discontinued following the 2049 scandal. Many early experimenters either relocated to Velúria or are believed to have died by suicide.

## Social and Economic Impact

### Adoption Rates

As of 2085, cognitive enhancement adoption varies dramatically across Susian society:

**By Free Economic Zone:**

- [[Troli Ustaras]]: 98%
- [[Nayotai]]: 85%
- [[Van Ritter]]: 75%
- [[POCOIL]]: 50%
- [[Soites Group]]: 45%
- [[Atompron]]: 40%
- Independent Susia: 16%

**Overall national rate:** 17% of the Susian population

### Economic Effects

Enhanced individuals earn between 200% and 300% more than unenhanced individuals performing similar work, creating significant economic stratification. This wage differential has become a central element in political debates about enhancement policy and has contributed to economic pressure on unenhanced workers to undergo enhancement procedures despite legal protections against coercion.

### Social Stratification

The disparity in enhancement rates and economic outcomes has created distinct social groups within Susian society. Enhanced individuals tend to cluster in specific FEZs and occupational sectors, particularly in technology, finance, and high-skill manufacturing. Unenhanced communities maintain presence primarily in independent Susia and in designated Sanctuary Zones.

## Political Debates

Cognitive enhancement represents one of the most significant political divisions in contemporary Susian society, with major political parties advocating substantially different approaches:

### Enhancement Subsidies

The [[Modernist Alliance]] has proposed legislation to subsidize enhancement procedures by 30% to 50% based on income level. Proponents argue this would increase access and reduce social stratification by making enhancement available to broader segments of the population. Many Modernist Alliance voters are themselves enhanced, creating alignment between policy advocacy and constituent interests.

The [[Coalition of State's Rights]] opposes subsidy proposals, characterizing them as a step toward "forced universal enhancement." Coalition representatives argue that enhancement is fundamentally coercive, that long-term effects remain unknown, and that remaining unenhanced is not a viable option for many who desire enhancement but face economic or social pressure.

### Enhancement Tax

The Coalition of State's Rights has proposed special taxation on enhanced individuals to fund unenhanced job programs in the public sector. Advocates note the significant income disparity between enhanced and unenhanced workers and argue for redistributive taxation to support unenhanced employment opportunities.

Critics question the precedent of taxing individuals for medical procedures and doubt whether sufficient public sector jobs can be created to meaningfully address the economic disadvantages faced by 62% of the population (the unenhanced majority). Modernist Alliance representatives characterize such proposals as "affirmative action for cognitive Luddites," arguing that the policy would become increasingly untenable as the enhanced population continues to grow.

### Philosophical Divisions

Political debates about enhancement often reflect deeper philosophical disagreements:

**Pro-Enhancement Position:**
- Technology extends human evolution beyond biological constraints
- Enhancement represents the next stage of human development
- Democratic systems must adapt to accommodate enhanced humans
- Individual cognitive autonomy includes the right to self-modification

**Enhancement-Skeptical Position:**
- Unknown long-term effects justify caution
- Enhancement creates irreversible social stratification
- Economic coercion undermines genuine choice
- Traditional human cognitive capacity should be protected
## Corporate Involvement

### Troli Ustaras

[[Troli Ustaras]] develops and manufactures the neural interface hardware used in enhancement procedures. The company maintains a strong organizational culture supportive of enhancement, with 98% of TU employees undergoing enhancement procedures. TU advocates for expanded access to enhancement technology and characterizes cognitive enhancement as a natural extension of human-technology integration.

TU's involvement in cognitive enhancement has been controversial due to the safety failures during the federal program era (2040-2048) and the 2047 corruption scandal that temporarily resulted in loss of Yarnojte status. Following restructuring in 2050 and regaining of Yarnojte status in 2051, the company has maintained improved safety standards and transparency.
### Lischev-Verene

[[Lischev-Verene]] develops biological compatibility pharmaceuticals and performs enhancement procedures in clinical settings. The company was formed in 2065 through a merger of[[ Lischev Pharmaceuticals]] and [[Verene Biosciences]] and received [[Yarnojte]] status in 2073.

Lischev-Verene maintains a philosophical position emphasizing biological approaches to human enhancement, captured in its motto "Consciousness IS biology." The company has expressed reservations about purely technological approaches to cognitive modification and has suggested that long-term effects of enhancement may be reversible through pharmaceutical intervention, though this remains unproven.
### Mandated Partnership

Federal regulations require Troli Ustaras and Lischev-Verene to cooperate on enhancement technology under threat of forfeiture of other contracts. The companies maintain cross-licensing agreements for relevant patents. Despite the mandated partnership, the relationship is characterized by significant tensions, including aggressive recruitment of each other's researchers and competing academic publications challenging each other's philosophical approaches.
### Soites Group

The [[Soites Group]]'s early [[Soites Experimenter Program|Experimenter Program]] provided the foundational behavioral research that led to the development of enhancement technology. While not directly involved in current enhancement procedures, the company's historical role remains significant. The early experimenters who participated in prototype testing during the 2030s faced significant health risks, and many experienced adverse outcomes following the termination of the program.

## Future Directions

As of the 2090s, cognitive enhancement technology continues to advance, with research ongoing into more sophisticated forms of cognitive modification. Experimental work on consciousness transfer and collective minds has not yet achieved practical implementation. The long-term trajectory of enhancement adoption, the resolution of uncertainties regarding long-term effects, and the evolution of social and political responses to cognitive diversity remain subjects of ongoing debate and research.
