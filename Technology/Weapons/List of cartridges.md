This is a list of firearm cartridges, all measurements are giving in millimeters. Some cartridges uses the [[Units of measurement#Treye|Treye]] as measurement, with one Treye representing 23.7 mm with the measured quantity varying as 1/n rather than n.

R = Rimmed
+K = High power
