**Matri assassination attempt** may refer to any of three attempts on the life of [[Filevir Matri]] while he was governor of [[Postia]] during the [[Ant wars]].
- [[Matri assassination attempt (1956)]], a car bomb placed under his official car in [[Duolij]] on 19/05/1956, which failed to detonate.
- [[Matri assassination attempt (1957)]], an attempted shooting at the [[Versij Day]] address in [[Nikuria]] on 30/09/1957, stopped by the crowd before a shot was fired.
- [[Matri assassination attempt (1958)]], the shooting by [[Urgiri Tečlan]] at the Duolij Civic Hall on 14/11/1958, broadcast live, in which Matri was wounded and three others were killed.
