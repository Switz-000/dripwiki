[[Armotist democracy]] is an ideology present primarily in [[Confia]] and [[Pierej]]. The term has also been the name of multiple political parties throughout history.
- [[Armotist Democracy (Piereji party, 1946)]], a Piereji political party. The party of the country's founder, [[Alfrin Žobran]].
- [[Armotist Democracy (Piereji party, 1984)]], a post-Saron Piereji political party that purported itself to be the ideological successor of the 1946 iteration.
- [[Armotist Democracy (Confian party, 2000)]], a Confian political party born out of the collapse of the [[Armotist Democratic Party]]
