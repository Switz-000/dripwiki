INTERPRETATION CHANGES

The complicity model, revised  
The cluster's relationship to the cannibal zone is not active laundering through deniable extraction contracts. It is open sanction plus tolerated smuggling plus non-intervention. The regime is straightforwardly evil; the cluster's stance is structural cowardice. _They condemn and will not pay what removal would cost._ Sharpens upstream readings of partition "stability" — stable in the sense that nothing changes, not in the moral sense. Affects Bershad articles, Sorophine, Susian foreign-policy posture, and Wave 2 critical historiography framing.

The "no cannibal zone" as the regime's structural self-indictment  
The fact that the regime can suspend the practice within a walled district when it chooses proves predation is policy, not cultural compulsion. They eat the people they choose to eat. This is the load-bearing detail that makes the polity legible without making it sympathetic. Affects Western Coalition Zone, San'motko, and any Susian commentary or video treatment.

Coexistence-under-hatred: four-mechanism typology  
The cluster reveals four working models for coexistence among groups that don't get along. One group wins so hard others stop being political subjects (Yar-Firol with the Buta). Exclusion formalized below the level of public discourse (Sekyo with Lawhá). Demographic absorption before politicization (Veskera). Shared procedural commitment with the unsolved pieces postponed (Susia). Bershad got none. Replaces flat "diverse society" framings. Affects Yar-Firol, Sekyo, Veskera, Susian identity articles, and any cluster overview material.

Susia is not Bershad-with-better-luck  
Susia's coexistence works because the deferred questions were small enough to slow-resolve through institutional persistence. The Tekur case is the cleanest example. Susia is in a different problem space from Bershad, not the same problem space with better outcomes. Replaces the framing where Susia is a latent catastrophe waiting to be triggered. Affects cluster comparative material, Susian identity articles, any video-essay handling of Susia's coexistence machinery.

Sienes as the cleaner Dripstan-thesis case than Nguan  
Sienes is the model the bible studies: reasonable doctrine defeated by structural conditions through normal institutional machinery. Nguan is where the thesis strains — personal monstrousness is overdetermined alongside structural impossibility, and reading him purely as _institutions did it_ understates the man. Both belong in canon. The thesis explains Sienes cleanly and works around Nguan. Affects any comparative leadership material, future Nguan article framing.

Bogtu's regime as the last functional Bershadi state  
Despite brutality, Bogtu's apparatus was internationally engaged — a state, not a faction. The 1989 assassination is the inflection point that opens the long descent toward the pariah cannibal polity, and the same moment Buta statelessness becomes permanent. Heavier historical weight on 1989 than previously held. Affects Bogtu article, Bershadi civil war narrative, Buta diaspora narrative.
