That loop is the whole thing. You owe competence to your neighbors. Not a metaphor, civics class teaches you to shoot because a right nobody can defend is a privilege waiting to be revoked, and you cannot defend it alone. The [[Sorzenko]], the [[Hăjaven]] hunt, the conscript eating beside a stranger from a state he's never seen, They are the republic. The liberty is the process, not the outcome: the 1954 preamble unites people "not by blood, creed, or tongue, but by agreement on the manner in which they settle their differences." You are not Susian because you believe the same things. You are Susian because you agreed to the same way, and you armed yourself to keep the way open.

Main inspirations.

The citizen-soldier, not the frontiersman. The American gunman is alone on his property, defending what's his. The Susian gunman is in a Sorzenko, in a unit, at a communal hunt, and his weapon is a credential of his standing among others, an enrollment, not a fence. Closer to the classical republic, the citizen who is free because he is armed, and armed because the city is his to defend. The duty is the dignity.

The [[Cericeiro]]'s obligation to intervene. carry, be polite, and step in when the situation demands it. Capacity creates obligation. The man who can act and doesn't is the failure, worse than the man who acts wrongly. This is what "liberty" bottoms out to here, not "leave me alone" but "be capable, and be there." [[Kaichet Satratonie]] is the national villain precisely because he used his capacity for his selfish interest and the pussy of a woman that didn't love him back.

Process over substance, and the cost of it. Because the union is procedural, it can hold people who agree on nothing else, which is its genius and its blade. It means a Modernist and a Coalition gunsmith and a [[Sužielaj]] nurse are all equally, fully Susian, and it means the process can be fed anything. A liberty defined as how we decide rather than what we decide has no built-in answer when the thing the process freely chooses is its own enclosure. The republic of armed, dutiful citizens has no doctrine against signing yourself, voluntarily, competently, into a FEZ that asks for no gun and offers no neighbor.

The seam I'm building toward. The civic-duty liberty is what makes Susia ungovernable by tyrants and it is exactly what the consensual cage cannot be stopped by, because the cage doesn't violate the process. It uses it. The [[FEZ]] resident who finds a gun "primitive," who has no [[Sorzenko]], whose neighbors are all the same employer, hasn't had his rights taken. He set them down. Freely. Competently. The way everything dangerous in this world gets done.
