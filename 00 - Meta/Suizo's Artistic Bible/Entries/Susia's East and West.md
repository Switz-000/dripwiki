East and West, my interpretation (suzio)

The simplest way to hold it: the West has the _theory_ of Susian liberty and the East has the _body_ of it. [[Armadesh Versij|Versij]] was published in the West, the constitution was argued in the West, the courts and the banks and the [[Free Economic Zone|FEZs]] sit in the West. Go east and you find the thing the theory was describing all along, still being lived by people who would struggle to cite a page of it. The West can explain why you owe competence to your neighbor. The East just shows up at your neighbor's barn fire with a truck and three sons.

The mechanism is density and space, and it runs deeper than scenery. [[Neoveli]] and [[Duolij]] are vertical, compact, millions stacked along a coast, every relationship mediated by money, by the Exchange bell, by which tower you work in. A city like that produces the Versijian individual in his purest form, sovereign, rights-bearing, and alone, because that is what the form of the city allows. The East is wide. Suburbs and mid-sized cities spread thin over plains, the largest city on the continent by area, and that horizontal looseness keeps the older unit alive: the neighbor, the [[Sorzenko]], the congregation, the people you actually depend on because no tower and no bank stands between you and them. Communal liberty needs room to be communal. The West paved over the room.

So the holidays tell on people. [[Hăjaven]] in the West is a household dinner, the family at the table, the hunt a quaint thing other people do. Hăjaven in the East is the street, the block, the Sorzenko, coworkers and strangers fed at the same long table. Same federal holiday, same date, two different things, and neither side quite believes the other is doing it right. The Westerner finds the Eastern version exhausting and a little performative. The Easterner finds the Western version lonely and a little ashamed of itself.
