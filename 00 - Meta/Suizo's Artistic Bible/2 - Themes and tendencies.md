Each theme must live in _furniture and behaviour_, never in commentary. These are not a checklist, but vectores and directions where results can point towards.
# Susia

- **Logical steps to an irrational outcome.** Build monstrous or absurd results out of small, individually reasonable decisions. Walk each sensible step, then pull back to show the collective thing nobody designed and no one can be singularly blamed for.
	- **Exemples: [[Continental Divide]]'s 100 day crisis, [[Soites Group]], [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ system]].**

- **How ideas distort through interpretation.** Senate floor, denominational schism, geopolitical reality, corporate co-optation. An idea never survives contact with the people who need it to mean something for them.
	- **Exemples: [[Kolkovianism]], [[Apajianism]], [[Suizo Soites]].**

- **Trade-offs and unintended consequences.** Every institution solves a problem by creating the next one. Track the downstream. When a new piece is added, silently re-examine what it changes upstream and surface it.
	- **Exemples: [[Yarnojte|Yarnojtes]], [[Secretariat of Efficiency]], [[Cognitive Rights Act]].
## Post 2040s Susia

These may only be applied in [[Susia]] during and after [[The Great Transition|The Great Transition of 2038]]

- **The limits of the human.** Materialism, loss of meaning, dehumanization. Enhancement, life-extension, optimization. The question is never "is this evil", it is "is this good for me, and is that the problem?"
	- **Exemples: [[Cognitive enhancement]], [[Bodily Autonomy Act of 2072]], [[Garden of Latice]]**

- **Consent and privacy.** The most dangerous arrangements are the ones freely chosen. "You decided" is how everything gets laundered. Surveillance delivered as service. Care delivered as control.
	- **Exemples: [[Soites Experimenter Program]], [[VITAKEI]], [[Soites Federal Free Trade Zone|Soites FFTZ]]**
