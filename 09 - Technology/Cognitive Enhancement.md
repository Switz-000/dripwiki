# Enhancement Timeline

Pre-Transition Susia (Pre-2038): Regulatory Hell:

- Traditional democratic government, heavily regulated research environment 
- [[Troli Ustaras|TU]] and other companies frustrated (can't innovate fast enough)
- Losing ground to [[Ashgerad]]
- Result: [[Great Transition]] creates [[Free Economic Zone|FEZ]] system with lighter regulation
### Phase 1: The [[Soites Group|Soites]]-[[Troli Ustaras|TU]] Partnership (2032-2038)

 Soites launches [[Soites Experimenter Program|Experimenter Program]] 2032: 
- “Consumer research”, actually: psychological research at unprecedented scale
- What Soites discovered: predictive models of human behavior (scary accurate), cognitive patterns in decision-making. "If we can predict behavior, can we... modify it?"
##### The Partnership (2034):
- TU’s Expertise: AI systems, computing infrastructure hardware engineering
- Soites' Expertise: Human behavioral data, cognitive research, real world testing infrastructure (experimenters = ready subjects)
##### 2035-2037: Early Prototypes:
- Tested on willing Experimenters (paid extra, signed NDAs)
- Results: Promising but crude
- Side effects common (headaches, mood swings, occasional seizures)
- The Problem: Pre-transition regulatory environment
 

### Phase 2: Post-Transition Federal Program

- New Reality:
 
- [[Free Economic Zone|FEZs]] have regulatory flexibility
 - [[Susian Federal Goverment|Federal government]] can move faster
 - "National security" exemptions (Cold War justifies everything)
 - Ethics oversight lighter (still exists, but streamlined)
 - 2040: [[Federal Cognitive Enhancement Program]] Launched
 
- The Disaster Years (2040-2048)
 
- 2040-2042: Rapid Development, Reckless Testing
 - Pressure from the cold war mounting, [[Troli Ustaras|TU]] pushing boundaries (missionary zeal), safety protocols… relaxed.
- 2041: First death, seizure during enhancement procedure, family not compensated the full promised amount, signs NDA under pressure and receives 500k
 - 2042-2046: 8 more deaths, 23 with permanent cognitive damage, 63 moderate injuries. Each family: Incomplete compensation, pressured NDAs
 - 2047: The TU scandal, separate but related. TU tries to bribe a secretariat member, loses Yarnojte status. Program paused
 
- The Leak: 2049
 
- Families of 3 deceased participants break NDA simultaneously, coordinate with journalist, 
 - ”Secret Federal enhancement program exists, kills people, covers it up”
 - TU scandal + Enhancement Leak = complete chaos
 - Public trust shattered, congressional hearings, [[AFCC]] investigation, secretariat review.
 

### **Phase 3: TU Goes Public and Commercial (2051-2061)**

#### - TU’s Pivot:
 
- Federal Program Dead (politically toxic)
 - Restructured, New CEO, entirely new management, new vision
 - Technology almost worked (just needed safety improvements)
 - "TU will continue cognitive enhancement research. Publicly. Transparently. With proper safety protocols. For medical purposes only."
 
#### Development Hell (2051-2057)
 
- Public trials, rigorous safety testing, can’t afford another death. Slow but promising results
 - No deaths in six years, proved technology is safe.
 - 2057: Federally approved, Indicated for Severe PTSD, only 3,000 procedures/year
 - 2058: Expanded Approvals, Severe Depression, Anxiety, BPD, only 20,000 procedures/year
 - Growing black market, fake diagnosis so people could do enhancement (the focus and memory effects were very desired)
 
#### [[2061 Cognitive Rights Act]] (2061)
 
- Allowed enhancement procedures without medical need
 - Right to cognitive autonomy (can't force enhancement)
 - Right to cognitive privacy (can't force scan brains without consent)
 - Cannot discriminate based on enhancement status: employment, housing, insurance etc.
 - Must be 25 or older
 - Strict informed consent, 3 month waiting period, may cancel appointment at any time, free counseling provided by the federal government 
 - Creation of Sanctuary zones, unenhanced-majority communities protected
 - ¼ Rule, only ¼ of a state’s counties may apply for Sanctuary Zone status. Enhancement clinics or procedures cannot be banned at a state level.
