Tekurs are a major racial group in the [[Dripstanian Continent]]. They are the majority population in [[Drippalia]], [[Kapiogg]] and [[Celiolaj]] and are sizeable minorities in [[Susia]], [[Confia]] and [[Pierej]].

# Etymology

The Susian word "tekur" and the Confian word "tekur/тѣкур" both derive from [[Konph]] "tiɣ-ɣuúr", meaning "thatched home-dwelling". Notably, this term was an epithet used derogatorily by Konphs to refer to the Tekurs' perceived backwardsness, the standard Konph word to refer to the race being "xaás-te", meaning "green-skinned".
